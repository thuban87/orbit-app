## Phase Goal

**As a** person keeping people in orbit, **I want to** see my favourites on a home-screen widget with their status colours and act on them in one tap, **so that** I can mark contact or open someone without launching the app.

> MVP slice: the two remaining node-testable WDG-01 cores the widget render consumes — the base64 thumbnail encoder and the headless colour resolver. Pure/unit-tested, ahead of the native surface. Depends on 12-01 for the shared status tokens the resolver returns.

<objective>
Build the base64 avatar encoder and the headless status-colour resolver.

Purpose: WDG-01 requires "photos as base64" (RemoteViews cannot read file://, and network on the widget path violates local-first) and "each avatar carrying its status colour." The widget render runs in a headless task with NO ThemeProvider mounted, so colours must resolve directly from theme-presets, never useTheme.
Output: `widget-photo.ts` (data:-URI encoder reusing the existing ImageManipulator chain) and `widget-colors.ts` (palette + status→ring colour + weight), each with its Wave-0 test green.
</objective>
