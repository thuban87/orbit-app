<objective>
Give the digest ON/OFF toggle its durable, backup-exportable home: a new
`app_settings.digest_enabled` column (migration 005, owner-ruled), threaded through the
app-settings DAO exactly like its sibling notification toggles.

## Phase Goal (user story)
**As a** person tending my relationships, **I want to** get one calm Sunday-morning
notification that opens a live "your week" screen, **so that** I get a scheduled
retrospective without being nagged — and I want to be able to turn it off and have that
choice stick.

This slice is the toggle's persistence. Its Settings UI row lands in 15-05 (the row must
fire the schedule reconcile, which only exists after the schedule service in 15-03 — a
dependency-driven ordering, not a horizontal split). The schedule service (15-03) READS
digest_enabled from here.

Purpose: DGST-01 requires "defaults on, independently toggleable" — a durable OFF that
the launch sweep cannot re-enable (RESEARCH Pitfall 1). That requires a persisted flag.
Output: migration 005 + its test, database.ts registration, and the DAO thread.

## Owner ruling (NON-NEGOTIABLE — do not "bug-fix" this migration away)
CONTEXT.md §Persistence: the owner RULED that `digest_enabled` persists as migration 005.
"Adds NO new schema" means no new TABLES and no new PER-CONTACT state — the digest stays
a pure read surface storing nothing per contact. A single global settings column is the
toggle's proper home and is consistent with how every prior toggle shipped (decay/birthday
migration 002, ai_* migration 004). Phase 15 owns 005; Phase 16 owns normalized custom-field
migration 006 and Phase 17 owns tombstones in 007 (already reflected in ROADMAP). Removing or
weakening this migration would REVERSE a recorded owner decision — escalate, never do it.
