Build the Sunday alarm: a WEEKLY local-notification trigger under `digest:weekly`, its own

## Phase Goal (user story)
**As a** person tending my relationships, **I want to** get one calm Sunday-morning
notification, **so that** I'm reminded to look back at my week without being nagged — and
it must survive a reboot and re-arm itself.

After this slice, the digest actually schedules on every real foreground launch and fires
Sunday morning (device-observable behavior — the vertical outcome), even before the screen
is styled.

Purpose: DGST-01 (the WEEKLY fire, defaults-on, reboot-surviving). The single genuinely
new mechanism in this phase is the WEEKLY trigger; everything else is composition.
Output: digest-schedule.ts + test, the notification-ids/channels additions, App.tsx wiring,
and the non-clobber regression on the existing reconcile.

- The digest schedule is its OWN service + its OWN sweep hook. NEVER fold it into
  reconcileSchedule — isOwnedIdentifier matches only decay:/birthday: (notification-schedule.ts:287-289)
  and notification-schedule.test.ts:505 already asserts a digest id is never cancelled.
- No network on any path. All copy frozen/generic (names no one) — safe on a public lock
  screen; the channel is PRIVATE regardless (mirrors birthday).
- Channel importance/visibility are IMMUTABLE at creation — version the id (digest-v1);

- Delivery hour reuses the SHARED app_settings.deliveryHour (11-notify's morning hour); the
  weekday is a top-of-file tunable DIGEST_WEEKDAY = 1 (Sunday) — device-spike verified in 15-06.
