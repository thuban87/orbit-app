**Shared contact-import pipeline now terminalizes nameless bulk rows, safely canonicalizes OEM birthdays, and exits already-linked single imports through a back-safe completion screen.**

## Accomplishments

- Bulk rows missing a name now skip terminally, avoid photo staging, finalize in the batch driver, and report in their own completion bucket.
- Birthday parsing accepts unambiguous slash formats, rejects ambiguous or impossible dates through the existing strict gate, and reports imported unreadable values without schema changes.
