# Phase 37.1 Physical-Device UAT

**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Target:** Physical Pixel 6 Pro over USB (`DEVICE` topology), Android package `com.bwales.orbit`  
**Runtime:** Debug client with Metro in tmux session `orbit`; `adb reverse tcp:8081 tcp:8081` present  
**Privacy:** Evidence below uses aggregate counts and synthetic `UAT-*` category labels only. No private contact contents or database files were captured.

## Target and safety gate

- `emu-connect device` and `emu-connect status` reported `DEVICE`, the physical Pixel 6 Pro, local Metro listening, and reverse port 8081 set.
- Every device operation re-resolved the serial from `~/.local/bin/adb devices`; cardinality remained exactly one authorized `device` row throughout.
- Package and session were retrieved from checked-in configuration/runbooks and live tmux state: `com.bwales.orbit`, `orbit` (two windows).
- No alternate database, executor override, development route, test-only production infrastructure, or induced DAO failure was used.
