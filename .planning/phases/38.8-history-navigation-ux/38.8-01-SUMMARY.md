# Phase 38.8 Plan 01: History Seed Fixture Summary

**A dev-only, deterministic History seed of eight synthetic contacts (including a 912-interaction multi-year contact and a 25-record day) plus two group events. It writes through the app's Core writers, is idempotent by uid prefix, and is verified by count before it reports `present`.**

## Accomplishments
- `buildHistorySeedPlan(now)` is a pure, deterministic spec using a mulberry32 PRNG with a fixed seed. It does all date work in local components and never slices UTC ISO strings. Today-dated stamps are clamped to `now`.
- `seedHistoryFixture(exec, deps)` writes, inside one transaction per contact:
  - `createContactFullCore`
  - `insertInteractionCore` and `recomputeLastContactCore`, so `last_contact` is only ever derived
  - `recordEventCore`
  - `setCurrentStateValueCore`, in chronological order

  After those transactions commit, it calls `createGroupEvent` (with `now` passed explicitly) and then `archiveContact`.
- `verifyHistoryFixture(exec, now)` checks each dataset's per-family counts, its group links and its archive state. It returns content-free `<key>: <problem>` strings.
- Dev-menu item **"History: seed fixture (38.8)"** in `uat-probes.ts`:
  - has its own busy flag and lazily requires `./history-seed` and `@/db/database`
  - calls `bumpShellRefresh()` after a `created` result (C4-04)

## Datasets (for Plan 06)

Each dataset has the uid `dev-history-seed-<key>`. The counts below are for a seed run in 2026.

| Key | Contact name | Cadence | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| long | History Long | 7 days | 912 interactions plus 2 group-linked, across 2026, 2025, 2024, 2022 and 2021. The year three back (2023) is empty. Each populated year has more than 20 records; there are 2 today and 2 yesterday. 14 knowledge changes and 7 lifecycle records (a bind, then three snooze/unsnooze pairs); half fall on interaction days, half on days with none. |
| dense | History Dense | 14 days | Its newest day (yesterday at seed time) holds exactly 25 interactions. 30 older interactions and 2 knowledge changes. |
| sparse | History Sparse | 30 days | 3 interactions in 3 different years: 1 of its own (3 years back) and 2 group-linked. |
| notes-only | History Notes Only | 30 days | No interactions. 6 knowledge changes and 4 lifecycle records (snooze/unsnooze pairs). |
| unbound | History Unbound | none, tracking off | 12 interactions and 1 knowledge change. |
| quiet-year | History Quiet Year | 30 days | Every record predates the current year: 32 interactions, 2 knowledge changes, 2 lifecycle records. |
| archived | History Archived | 30 days | 10 interactions, 1 knowledge change and a snooze/unsnooze pair, then archived at seed time (adds an `archive` event). |
| empty | History Empty | 30 days | No records of any family. |

There are two group events, each with Long and Sparse as participants:
- "Synthetic group dinner": about 12 days before the seed day, never today.
- "Synthetic group hike": last year, early October.

## Expected log lines
- First run: `history-seed created contacts=8 interactions=N knowledge=N lifecycle=N groups=2 spec=1`, followed by the menu item's `history-seed result status=created`.
- Later runs on an intact fixture: `history-seed present`, then `history-seed result status=present`. Nothing is written.
- A damaged fixture: `history-seed partial <n> problems`, plus one `history-seed problem <key>: …` line per problem, then `history-seed result status=partial`. Nothing is written. Treat this as a stop-and-report.
- An exception: `history-seed failed <message>`.

Log lines carry counts and dataset keys only, never names, notes or values.

## Decisions Made
- **History Sparse gets one interaction of its own; the two group events supply the other two.** Both group events include Long and Sparse, so this keeps Sparse at exactly three interactions in three different years.
- **The archive event is dated at the seed's `now`, which is today.** That is how `archiveContact` behaves.
- **Every per-family count is constant.** Only the dates move with `now`, so verification compares counts and not dates.
