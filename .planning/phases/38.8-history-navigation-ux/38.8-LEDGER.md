- **Asked:** Jump + highlight (Recommended; no card, stream scrolls to that day and briefly highlights it, day-detail sheet retired; supersedes card-first) · Card, then jump (card kept, its button scrolls instead of opening a sheet) · Filter to that day (chip + Clear; hides surrounding history).

  > Jump + highlight (Rec.)
