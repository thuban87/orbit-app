# Phase 38.8 Plan 03: Heatmap to Stream Navigation Summary

**A heatmap tap now takes you to the records. A populated day or cycle span opens and grows the stream as needed, scrolls the Profile to the target (the newest record of a span) and highlights its days with an accent bar and tint that fade over 5 seconds, announced once to screen readers. A truly empty day or span shows a dismissible note under the heatmap with an honest Log offer. The count card and the date-detail sheet are gone, with their contract tests migrated.**

## Accomplishments

- **Scroll plumbing (`module-host-model.ts`, `ProfileModuleHost.tsx`).**
  - `scrollWithinHistory(offsetY)` is a plain additive scroll (`hostY + historyY + offsetY`). It holds no generation state and does not touch a pending expand reveal.
  - `completeHistoryScroll` checks the request's own `isCurrent()` right before scrolling. A stale request neither scrolls nor reports.
  - The `profile-history-anchor` View has a ref and `collapsable={false}`. `requestHistoryScroll` uses the ref form of `measureLayout` (no `findNodeHandle`). A failed measure logs a content-free warning and reports failure only to a current request.
  - `HistorySection` is keyed by the contact id, so its view state resets on a contact change (D-62).
- **Jump logic (`history-stream-logic.ts`, pure and tested).**
  - `resolveHeatmapTarget` decides emptiness from the all-family day index, never from the heatmap's interaction count. A cycle span reveals every day in it that has records, newest first, and may span two years.
  - `streamStateOnReveal` opens each target year and raises its requested count in whole 20-record chunks until the day-snapped slice covers the target day.
  - The scroll-request reducers are `historyScrollMode` (read at tap time), `pendingScrollOn` and `isCurrentScroll`.
  - Also added: `revealAnnouncement`, the note reducer `historyNoteOn`, and the note texts.
- **`HistorySection.tsx`.**
  - Node registry, a per-mount generation, and the pending scroll changed only through `commitPending`. That helper writes the ref synchronously; no effect mirrors it.
  - An `immediate` target scrolls from a layout effect. An `await-layout` target scrolls from its own `onNodeLayout`.
  - After a failed measure there is one retry, on the next layout or after `HISTORY_SCROLL_RETRY_MS = 300`. The timer is cleared whenever the request is replaced or cleared, and on unmount.
  - Every other stream action cancels a pending jump: lens, preset, prev/next, Show older, year expand, a non-reveal heatmap tap and closing the note.
  - The highlight state is cleared once by a timeout per generation. There is exactly one `announceForAccessibility` call site.
  - Nothing here writes the heatmap window `day` (D-56).
- **`HistoryHighlight.tsx`.** One Reanimated shared value drives two self-contained `useAnimatedStyle` callbacks: tint opacity (progress × 0.14) and bar opacity. Normal motion uses `withTiming(0, { duration: 5000, reduceMotion: ReduceMotion.Never })`. Reduced motion uses `withDelay(5000, withTiming(0, { duration: 0, reduceMotion: Never }), Never)`. Blur or backgrounding ends the highlight at once and it never restarts. It ignores touches and is hidden from accessibility.
- **`HistoryTargetNote.tsx`.** A bordered `surfaceElevated` row containing the text, a tertiary "Log interaction" button and an icon-only close button ("Dismiss note"). It is a polite live region, so it is read once when it appears.

## Behaviour changes for the Plan 06 docs update (`docs/systems/interaction-history.md`, `docs/systems/profile.md`)

1. Tapping a heatmap day that has any record (interaction, lifecycle or knowledge) no longer opens a card. The stream opens the day's year if it is collapsed and loads whole Show older chunks until the day renders. The Profile then scrolls so the day sits just below the app bar (instant under reduced motion), and the day's records are highlighted. The stream is never filtered.
2. Tapping a cycle cell that has records does the same for the whole span. It scrolls to the newest record in the span and highlights every day in the span that has records. A span that crosses New Year opens and grows both years.
3. The highlight is an accent bar down the left of each target day group plus a faint accent tint. It fades over 5 s (`HIGHLIGHT_FADE_MS` in `HistoryHighlight.tsx`). Under reduced motion it holds for 5 s and then disappears without a fade. Leaving the screen or backgrounding the app ends it, and it never replays. A screen reader hears one announcement: "Showing Friday, October 2, 2026", or for a span "Showing <start> to <end>" with years. The rows carry no live region.
4. A truly empty day shows a note directly under the heatmap: "Nothing logged on Tue Oct 5", with "Log interaction" (prefilled to that day) and a close (✕) control. A truly empty span shows "Nothing logged Sep 1 – Sep 7", and its Log interaction opens the detailed Log form with no date, so it starts at now. The note clears on close, on another heatmap tap, on a lens or preset change, on prev/next paging, and when its Log is pressed. The stream does not move for an empty target.
5. The count card ("1 interactions" / See details) and the date-detail sheet are gone. Records are reached only through the stream. InteractionDetail, Edit, delete, group scope and Make this a group are unchanged.
6. Day groups in the stream now have a small left inset (`SPACING.sm`) so the highlight bar never overlaps the day label, as in the mockup's `.day` padding.
7. Last Interaction / View history still land at the top of the History section (D-61). Scrolling or jumping never changes the heatmap/Intensity window (D-56).

## Decisions Made

- **The cycle `reveal` carries an optional `span`.** The announcement names both ends of the tapped span. This adds one optional field to the planned `HeatmapResolution`; nothing reads it except `revealAnnouncement`.
- **The reduced-motion seed race.** `useReducedMotion()` seeds asynchronously, starting at `false`, in every new component. A highlight mounted under reduced motion would otherwise start an animated fade. When the value arrives, the highlight re-issues only the remaining time on the hold path. The end time never moves.
- **Issue-once guard.** `ProfileModuleHost.requestHistoryScroll` is a new function on every render, so `issueScroll` and the layout effect re-run each render while a request is pending. `issuedRef` (`gen:attempt`) makes sure each attempt is issued once.
