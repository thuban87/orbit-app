## Gaps and observations (plain language)

**G1: Last Interaction / View history can stop short when History was collapsed (bug, D-61).**
If History is collapsed and you tap the Last Interaction tile or Orbit Status → View history, History opens. Sometimes the
page then stops with the History title near the bottom of the screen, instead of scrolling it to the top. This happens
every time on the debug build and about 1 time in 6 on release. It works every time when History is already open.
The cause: the scroll fires while History is still showing "Loading history…", so the page is too short to scroll that
far, and nothing scrolls again once the history loads. The reveal mechanism dates from 38.3. It is likely not new, but
this phase did not prove that either way.
*Recommendation:* fix it in a small gap plan, so the reveal scrolls again once History has finished loading. No decision
changes.

**G2: In the Year view, the "Nothing logged on …" note can appear off-screen.**
The note sits under the heatmap (D-55). The Year heatmap is taller than the screen, so tapping an empty day near the top
of the year shows nothing you can see. The note is there, below the fold. The other views are fine.
*Recommendation:* a small gap fix that scrolls the note into view when it would land off-screen. It keeps D-55's position.
Your call: the alternative is to leave it.

**Observations, no action recommended:**
- **O1.** When a record is added above where you are (a Log from the + button, or a new "Current location"), the stream
  keeps the day you were on, but the view moves down by the height of the new row. That is the designed behaviour (the
  page keeps its scroll position). *Recommendation:* leave it.
- **O2.** After editing a record while scrolled to the very bottom of the page, the view came back about a sixth of a
  screen lower. The record was still visible. With content below it, the place was exact. *Recommendation:* leave it.
- **O3.** The jump highlight is clearly visible for about 4 of its 5 seconds, because the fade eases out. That is the
  designed timing. It is a look item for you, below.
- **O4.** Jump to date into a month deep in the oldest year takes about 1 s. *Recommendation:* leave it. If it feels slow
  to you, the follow-up would be a bounded read or a virtualized list.
- **O5.** At maximum font, the Jump sheet's last month row starts below the sheet's height limit, but it scrolls into
  view. A scrim tap from adb did not close the sheet (Back did). That is the shared sheet component, which this phase did
  not change. Your item 4 checks it with a real finger.
- **O6.** The release APK is 208 MB (all four CPU types) against 81 MB on 2026-10-05. This phase did not cause it, and it
  was not investigated.
