What to look at
Records first. Newest first, grouped by year, then month, then day. Day labels read Today, Yesterday, then "Fri Oct 2" (D-14, D-15, D-33).
Rows. Interactions show channel, time, direction / No reply / quality, and two lines of note. Group interactions add the event name. Status events (Snoozed etc.) are grey and can't be tapped. Profile updates show the field and the new value (D-16, D-40 to D-43).
Older years are collapsed rows with an interaction count. An open year shows about 20 records, then "Show older" (D-34, D-35, D-39).
Heatmap tap scrolls to that day or cycle and highlights it with a bar and tint for about 5 seconds. An empty day shows a note under the heatmap with Log interaction. An empty cycle offers Log without a date (D-19 to D-21, D-53 to D-55).
Jump to date opens a year stepper and month grid. Dots mark months with records. It opens on the month in view (D-48 to D-50).
Unchanged: lenses and presets (labels now in full), the Intensity line, and the no-history, loading and error states.
