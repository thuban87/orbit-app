## Phase Boundary

**Objective:** Replace the Profile History module's Rolodex date wheels with an activity-first
chronological stream of the contact's actual records, inside the existing in-Profile History
section. Today a user must pick a date on three wheels and then press See details before any
record appears; after this phase, records show immediately, newest first, and a heatmap tap or a
month/year Jump lands on the right place in the stream.

**In scope**
- The stream: newest-first records grouped by year → month → day; the current year open and older
  years collapsed with interaction counts; ~20 records then 'Show older' inside an open year; Today /
  Yesterday / dated day labels; a flat list (D-10, D-14..D-16, D-18, D-32..D-47, D-62).
- Jump to date: a button above the list opens a year stepper + month grid with dots on months that
  have records; an empty month gets an explicit state (D-17, D-48..D-52).
- Heatmap ↔ stream: a populated day or cycle tap jumps to it and highlights it (accent bar + tint,
  ~5 s fade); an empty day gets a dismissible note under the heatmap with Log; the stream never
  moves the heatmap (D-19..D-21, D-23, D-28, D-53..D-56).
- Retiring the Rolodex (archived as reference) and the DateDetailSheet / count-card flow (D-12,
  D-13, D-22).
- Small heatmap items the owner already ruled on: full-width lens labels, Cycles prev/next by
  whole page, and Month shown for contacts with no cadence (D-24, D-25, D-26, D-27).
- The stream's place surviving edits, deletes and refreshes; a fresh start on each new visit (D-31, D-62).
- Renaming the section's visible title to 'History' (D-60).

**Out of scope**
- Any other heatmap or analysis change: Intensity wording, labels or math; a collapsible analysis
  block; new lenses; changing presets (owner scope ruling, ledger Batch 5).
- History search or filtering; an audit log; new event families such as memory, custom-field or
  category changes (D-08).
- A standalone History screen or a "See all" route (D-11).
- Changes to the detailed Log form, InteractionDetail, Edit, Delete or group-event surfaces
  (D-02, D-28).
- The no-history, loading and error states; archived-contact behaviour (D-57..D-59).
- Digest Your Week (D-09).
- Phase 39 onboarding teaching, including any History hint (it stays Phase 39's open item).
- New storage, migrations or backup format changes.

**Preserved behavior** (CARRIED — D-01..D-09)
- Count-only heatmap/Intensity from interaction rows; group child counted once; no parent row.
- Canonical InteractionDetail → Edit (sole recency writer) / confirmed hard-delete; group edit
  scope prompt; "Make this a group interaction".
- Detailed `LogContact { contactId, prefillDate }` backfill, never Quick Log.
- Profile-revision refresh; today-following with past views staying put; in-place History reveal.
- The `interaction-history` module identity, visibility, order and collapse override.
- Vertical, fit-to-width heatmaps with no horizontal scroll; minute timestamps; large-text reach.
- Theme tokens only; no colour-only meaning; reduced motion; non-gesture paths.
- Three record families; knowledge rows open MemoryHistory; lifecycle rows read-only.
- Digest Your Week semantics and its shared seams.
</domain>

## Implementation Decisions

### Preserved behavior (carried)
- **D-01 [informational]:** [CARRIED · ADR-119] Heatmap and Intensity stay count-only from `interactions` rows; a group-linked child counts once per person; a Group Event parent is never a row or a count.
- **D-02 [informational]:** [CARRIED · ADR-122 as amended by ADR-127] An interaction row opens the canonical `InteractionDetail`. Edit goes through `EditInteraction` / `editTouchpointFull`, the sole recency writer. Delete is a confirmed hard-delete via `deleteTouchpoint`. Group-linked Edit keeps the participant/event scope prompt. Standalone Detail keeps "Make this a group interaction".
- **D-03 [informational]:** [CARRIED · ADR-123; phase-11 dossier §Z] Historical backfill uses the detailed `LogContact { contactId, prefillDate }` form, never Quick Log.
- **D-04 [informational]:** [CARRIED · ADR-165] History re-reads from the parent Profile revision. The analysis window follows today only when it is showing the current period, so a past view stays put on rollover. Last Interaction / View history reveal and expand History in place, and they are not offered when the layout hides History.
- **D-05 [informational]:** [CARRIED · ADR-108/150] The `interaction-history` module keeps its identity, template visibility/order and per-contact collapse override. No owner layout is reset.
- **D-06 [informational]:** [CARRIED · ADR-152/174] Heatmap guarantees: vertical progression, no horizontal scroll, fit-to-width day/cycle grids, non-overlapping tap zones (`cellHitSlop`). Timestamps show minute precision. Sheet and dialog actions are reachable at large text.
- **D-07 [informational]:** [CARRIED · CLAUDE.md; ADR-121 a11y intent] Every colour comes from theme tokens. Nothing is distinguished by colour alone. Reduced motion is respected, and every navigation has a non-gesture path.
- **D-08 [informational]:** [CARRIED · ADR-122; phase-11 dossier :658, :666, :877] There are three record families: interactions, read-only lifecycle events, and current-state knowledge changes. Knowledge rows open the field's `MemoryHistory`. There is no audit log and no History search/filtering.
- **D-09 [informational]:** [CARRIED · ADR-148] Digest Your Week keeps its behaviour. The `services/history/*`, `heatmap-cell` and `heatmap-fit` seams it consumes keep their semantics.

- **D-12:** [DECIDED · 2026-10-07] `RolodexBrowser`, `RolodexWheel`, `rolodex-logic` and `rolodex-logic.test` are archived as reference rather than deleted: moved out of `src/`, with no production imports, and not behind a runtime flag. (owner, Q-03)

### Stream shape
- **D-14:** [DECIDED · 2026-10-07] The stream is reverse-chronological, newest at the top. (owner, Q-04)
- **D-15:** [DECIDED · 2026-10-07] Records are grouped year → month header → day label, and only dates that have records render. The current year is expanded. Each older year with records is a collapsed row showing the year and its entry count, and a tap expands it. Inside an expanded year, a long list shows its newest chunk followed by a 'Show older' button that adds the next chunk. (owner, Q-05)
- **D-16:** [DECIDED · 2026-10-07] An interaction row shows the channel icon and label, the local minute time, the direction / No reply / quality meta and up to two lines of the note (amended by D-41). A group-linked child also shows a restrained group-event title label. A tap opens the canonical `InteractionDetail`. (owner, Q-06)
  - The group title already comes from the read (`src/db/history-read.ts:144-151`); today's sheet rows don't render it (`DateDetailSheet.tsx:211-244`).
- **D-17:** [DECIDED · 2026-10-07] 'Jump to date' opens a month + year picker (form per D-48). Choosing a month scrolls the stream to that month's header, expanding its year and loading older chunks as needed. Future months can't be chosen. An empty month shows an explicit state naming the month, offers the nearest records before and after it, and offers Log per D-28. (owner, Q-07)
- **D-18:** [DERIVED · owner-approved 2026-10-08] Stream mechanics:
  - Chunk size and the initial visible count are tunable constants at the top of their owning file, both starting at ~20 (D-39).
  - Only years that have records get a row.
  - A jump or heatmap target inside a collapsed year expands that year and loads chunks until the target renders.
  - Expand/collapse and loaded-chunk state are transient. Nothing is persisted per contact.
  - The order across families is deterministic: equal timestamps go interactions, then lifecycle events, then knowledge changes, then id descending, with stable family+id keys. Today there is no tie-break (`DateDetailSheet.tsx:126`).
  - Lifecycle rows keep the existing labels (`EVENT_LABELS`) and stay read-only. Knowledge rows keep the existing presentation and navigation to `MemoryHistory`.
  - The picker's years run from the earliest record's year (or the current year when there are no records) to the current year.

  (derived from D-11, D-15, D-17)

(owner, Q-08) ⚠ SUPERSEDES phase-11 dossier §L :398-400 (partial — card-first for populated cells); ADR-123 (partial — the cell → card → sheet and drawer → sheet flows); ADR-122 (partial — DateDetailSheet as the History record-list surface).
- **D-20:** [DECIDED · 2026-10-07] Tapping a cycle cell that has records scrolls to the newest record within that span and highlights every record in the span. It uses the same no-card, no-filter model as D-19. (owner, Q-09)
- **D-21:** [DECIDED · 2026-10-07] Whether a target is empty is decided across all three record families, not by the interaction count. A truly empty cycle span or month offers Log interaction without a prefilled date. (owner, Q-10)

## Specific Ideas

- The owner finds the Rolodex unintuitive (`phase-39-onboarding-design-decisions.md:252`). Records should be visible immediately, without picking a date first.
- **Scope ruling (owner, 2026-10-08):** this phase is about the new history section replacing the Rolodex. Touch the heatmap only where it connects to the stream (D-19..D-21), plus the heatmap items already ruled (D-24..D-27).
- **Rejected:** keep the wheels and fix them (synced dates, tappable rows). Records would still be hidden behind a date pick (Q-01).
- **Rejected:** a calendar + day agenda. You must pick a day before seeing anything (Q-01).
- **Rejected:** a period-paged list. Old records need many page flips (Q-01).
- **Rejected:** Profile preview plus a full-screen stream, or full screen only. Either reverses ADR-165 (Q-02).
- **Rejected:** deleting the Rolodex outright. The owner wants it archived as reference (Q-03).
- **Rejected:** a card before the jump, or filtering the stream to the tapped day (Q-08).
- **Rejected:** prefilling the last or first day of an empty span or month. That invents a date (Q-10, Q-18).
- **Rejected:** a History-specific Log button (Q-16).
- **Rejected:** opening recent records across a year boundary in early January; only the current year opens (Q-23).
- **Rejected:** sticky month headers, and putting Jump in the section title bar (Q-21).
- **Rejected:** gap markers, per-day cards, long-press menus, the AI sparkle, duration or day counts on rows (Q-26, Q-27, Q-34..Q-37).
- **Rejected:** a 'Back to newest' button, and a heatmap that follows the list's scroll (Q-41, Q-45).
- **Rejected:** remembering per-contact list position between visits (Q-54).
- **Process note (owner, 2026-10-08):** the first draft was rejected because it asked too few questions; the interrogation was reopened (Batches 7–15) and most owner-visible details are now owner decisions rather than DERIVED items.
