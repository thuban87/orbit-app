# Phase 38.8 Plan 04: Jump to Date and Keeping Your Place Summary

**A 'Jump to date' button above the stream opens a year stepper and a 3×4 month grid. Months that have any record show a dot, and their accessible label says so. Future months are disabled. A month with records opens and grows the stream until that month's header renders, then scrolls the Profile to the header. It adds no highlight and is announced once. An empty month shows "No activity in October 2025." directly under the button, with the nearest record dates as tappable jumps that highlight like a heatmap tap, plus an undated Log offer. After an edit, redate or delete started from a row, the stream keeps the user's place.**

## Behaviour changes for the Plan 06 docs update (`docs/systems/interaction-history.md`, `docs/systems/profile.md`)

1. A secondary "Jump to date" button sits right-aligned directly above the first stream record, below Intensity. It is the stream header's only control and is not sticky.
2. It opens a compact bottom sheet titled "Jump to", with a ◀ YYYY ▶ year stepper over a 3×4 grid of short month names.
   - The years run from the earliest record's year (any family; the current year when there are none) to the current year.
   - Months after the current month are disabled and dimmed.
   - A month holding any record (interaction, lifecycle or knowledge) shows a small dot, and its accessible label ends ", has records".
   - The month it opened on has an accent border.
3. The sheet opens on the month currently in view in the stream, or on the current month. Because the button is not sticky, it opens on the current month in practice (D-68).
4. Choosing a month with records opens its year if collapsed and loads whole Show older chunks until the month renders. The Profile then scrolls so the month header sits just below the app bar. There is no highlight. A screen reader hears "Showing October 2025".
5. Choosing an empty month leaves the stream where it is. A note appears directly under the Jump to date button: "No activity in October 2025." It shows "Nearest:" with the nearest record dates after and before the month (e.g. "Nov 3, 2025"), a "Log interaction" offer and a close (✕) control.
   - Each date jumps to that day and highlights it exactly like a heatmap day tap.
   - Log opens the detailed Log form with no date, so it starts at now.
   - The note clears on close, on Log, on a nearest-date tap, on choosing another month, and on any heatmap tap.
   - The empty-day note under the heatmap is unchanged.
6. After an edit, redate or delete started from a stream row, the stream refreshes without a loading flash and the Profile keeps its scroll position.
   - If the record was redated to a newer day, the Profile scrolls once to its new day.
   - If the row's day no longer has any record, the Profile scrolls once to the nearest remaining day (a tie goes to the newer day).
   - Nothing re-scrolls on ordinary refreshes. A failed delete keeps the row with InteractionDetail's own retry.
   - Knowledge rows anchor their day only.
7. All of this is per visit. A new Profile visit starts fresh and nothing is persisted (D-62).

## Decisions Made

- **The month target is the month group View.** It begins at the header. One node serves as both the scroll target and the picker's measured header.
- **Measurement bound.** The picker opens when every month header has reported, or on the current month after 150 ms (top-of-file `PICKER_MEASURE_TIMEOUT_MS`). A header reporting a non-finite y is skipped. A per-press token prevents a stale callback from opening the sheet twice.
- **`earliestYear` never exceeds the current year,** and `pickerModel` clamps an out-of-range year. All-future-dated data therefore cannot invert the stepper bounds.
- **The empty-month state closes when its Log is pressed,** matching Plan 03's heatmap note.
