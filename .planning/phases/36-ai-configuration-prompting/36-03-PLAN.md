<objective>
Take ownership of prompt transmission: render the egress fields the read boundary already carries but
the template has never rendered (shared memories + gated recent-interaction notes), add the ephemeral
Adjust layer, and retire the last inert legacy AI-fuel confirm surface — all while keeping Off Limits
out of every shape (ADR-107), Group Notes never transmitted, and `resolvePrompt` the sole byte-identical
builder.

Purpose: This is where "what leaves the device" is finalized in the prompt. Getting the closed allowlist
and the ADR-107 off-limits exclusion exactly right here is the phase's central privacy guarantee.
Output: extended `prompt-template.ts`/`prompt-types.ts`, verified `ai-context-read.ts` gate, and the
removed AI-fuel confirm path.

**Decisions honored (CONTEXT.md):** D-06 — the off-limits-egress mechanism it specifies (avoidance constraints) is SUPERSEDED by ADR-107 (owner-ratified): Off Limits is never transmitted in any form, so this plan builds no off-limits shape (enforce, do not re-open); D-07 (Group Notes never transmitted; per-interaction Allow-AI gate honored); D-11 (no silent behavior — the sole byte-identical resolver, no second builder).
</objective>
