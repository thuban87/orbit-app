<objective>
Build the central AI permission manager: the surface where the user decides what categories of contact
information are eligible to leave the device for AI, and reviews/withdraws what already is. Two areas —
type defaults for new information (default OFF) and searchable review of existing AI-enabled information —
with bulk disable direct and bulk enable gated by an impact confirmation. Off Limits and Group Notes
are structurally absent from this surface.

Purpose: This is the user-facing control for the closed egress allowlist. Its correctness (new-items-only
default semantics, no enable-everything, off-limits/group-notes exclusion) is a privacy guarantee.
Output: `ai-permissions-dao.ts`, `AIPermissionsScreen.tsx` + logic.

**Decisions honored (CONTEXT.md):** D-07 (this phase owns the per-interaction Allow-AI type default — OFF, new-items-only; interaction notes covered/reviewable/withdrawable; Group Notes never surfaced), D-11 (deliberate resolution only — bulk enable behind explicit impact confirmation, no silent widening).
</objective>
