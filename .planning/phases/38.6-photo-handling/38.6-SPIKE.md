## Conclusion

- `revision-query` works on the device: the Profile, Contacts List and Contacts Grid all follow a replaced photo with no restart and after one, and no filename changed (`avatars/contact-23.jpg` throughout, D-21).
- `PHOTO_DISPLAY_STRATEGY` stays `"revision-query"`; the `no-cache` fallback was not needed and was not exercised.
- 38.6-03 builds on `revision-query`: spread `notifyPhotoBytesChanged` to the remaining byte-change points, route the other expo-image canonical-photo sites through the display source, and remove the crop screen's redundant call-site bumps and the dead `cacheBust` prop.
