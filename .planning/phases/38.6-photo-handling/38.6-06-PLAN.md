<objective>
Make Contacts page photos bigger — List +50% (D-05), Grid about 2× sized from the real card width (D-06) — with the Grid card's star and checkbox moved to 5 px from the corners (D-07), two-line names that fill line 1 first (D-08), and one height per row (D-09).

Purpose: the owner wants to see faces on the Contacts page. FlatList's row already stretches its cards (`FlatList.js` row `flexDirection: 'row'`), so pinning the secondary lines to the bottom gives D-09 without measuring text.

Output: a pure, tested grid geometry module, the reworked Grid card and List row, and render tests.
</objective>
