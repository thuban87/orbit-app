# Phase 38.6 Plan 06: Bigger Contacts Photos, Grid Corners, Two-Line Names Summary

Contacts List photos are now 72 (was 48), and the row grows to 96. Grid photos are sized from the nominal card width by a pure `gridCardGeometry` module: 89 at 393 dp in three columns, and 96 in two columns. The star and checkbox are now absolute 48×48 corner boxes with the glyph 5 px from the card edges. Names wrap greedily to two lines. Every card in a Grid row takes the row's height, and each card's secondary lines are pinned to the bottom.

## What was built

- **Task 1 (tracer, TDD), RED `d92c510` then GREEN `25d9c62`:**
  - `grid-card-geometry.ts` holds every tunable at the top.
  - `gridCardWidth` is `(W − 32 − 8·(cols − 1)) / cols`.
  - `gridCardGeometry` clamps `floor(cardWidth − 26)` to 48..96. It derives `ringBox = avatar + 8`, then solves `avatarTop` in closed form so the ring circle stays at least 4 px from both corner glyph boxes. The result is floored at border + padding (9).
  - `CardGrid` memoises the geometry per `(width, numColumns)`, passes it to every `GridCard`, and adds it to `extraData`.
  - `GridCard`'s `AVATAR_SIZE`/`RING_BOX` constants are gone.
  - The tracer's automated verify was re-run end to end before any expansion task. The plan is autonomous, so there was no human-verify stop.
- **Task 2 (TDD), RED `767d822` then GREEN `9e7e042`:**
  - `GlassSurface` gains an optional `contentStyle`. Without it, the content wrapper is still exactly `styles.content`, and a test renders the real component to prove this.
  - `GridCard` changes:
    - The surface is `{ flexGrow: 1, minHeight: 144, padding: 0 }` with `contentStyle { flexGrow: 1 }`.
    - The star (Pressable in normal mode, hidden View in selection mode) and the checkbox are absolute `top: 0` / `right: 0` or `left: 0` boxes, 48×48, `zIndex: 1`. The glyph is aligned to the corner with 4 px padding (5 − border + optical offset 0).
    - A `layout` View (`flexGrow: 1`, horizontal and bottom padding 8, no top padding) holds the ring box, the name and the secondary block. The ring box has `marginTop = avatarTop − 1`.
    - The name uses `numberOfLines={2}`, `ellipsizeMode="tail"` and `textBreakStrategy="simple"`.
    - The recency line, or the search explanation plus snippet, sits in a `marginTop: "auto"` block. Each line stays one line.
    - The old negative margins and the pull-up are gone.
- **Task 3 (TDD), RED `27e5fe5` then GREEN `800159d`:**
  - `LIST_AVATAR_SIZE = SPACING["2xl"] * 1.5` (72) and `LIST_ROW_PADDING = SPACING.md` (12) now sit at the top of `ListRow.tsx`.
  - The row's `minHeight` is `72 + 24`.
  - No `getItemLayout` exists, and the swipe backing and `HomeScreen`'s swipeable row do not assume a height.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The 38.5 swipe-panel geometry proof pinned ListRow's old source text**
- **Found during:** Task 3
- **Issue:** `src/theme/tokens/surface.test.ts` ("geometry: the content column never reaches the panel's glyphs") matched `padding: SPACING.md,` and the old avatar size literal in `ListRow.tsx`, and derived `ROW_CONTENT_INSET` from a 48 Avatar. The D-05 change broke the pin, but not the invariant.
- **Fix:** The pins now match `padding: LIST_ROW_PADDING`, `size={LIST_AVATAR_SIZE}` and the two `export const` lines. The inset is derived as the nearer side, `2 + 12 + min(72, 48) + 12`, which is still 74, so the owner's swipe-panel contrast proof and exemption are untouched. Only the left inset grows, to 98, which moves text further from the left panel's glyphs. The Avatar was already outside the text-contrast set because it is opaque (C2-L4).
- **Files modified:** src/theme/tokens/surface.test.ts
- **Commit:** 800159d

**2. [Rule 2 - Correctness] Spacing between the name and the secondary lines**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** `marginTop: "auto"` collapses to 0 in the tallest card of a row, which would put the recency line flush against the name. Before, the gap was 4 px.
- **Fix:** Added `paddingTop: SPACING.xs` to the secondary block, and `alignSelf: "stretch"` on the name and the secondary block so one-line text ellipsizes within the card width.
- **Commit:** 9e7e042

`gridColumnCount` moved into the geometry module, which the plan's action explicitly allows. The GlassSurface regression case lives in `grid-card-render.test.tsx` and uses `vi.importActual`, with `expo-blur` mocked and the theme mock extended with `mode` and `package`.

## Surface to owner

1. **The Grid star's hit area now covers part of the photo.** The plan asks for the 48×48 box to sit above the photo, and it does. At 393 dp in three columns, the box spans x 67–115, y 0–48, and the ring box spans x 9–106 from y 18. A tap on the photo's upper-right shoulder therefore toggles the favourite instead of opening the card. The checkbox has the same effect on the upper-left in selection mode. Please look at this during the 38.6-07 device pass. **Recommendation:** keep it unless it feels wrong on the phone. A smaller hit area would break the 48×48 rule, and pushing the photo lower costs height. `GRID_CORNER_CLEARANCE` or `GRID_AVATAR_MAX` are the single-number levers if you want the photo to sit lower or be smaller.
2. **Visible-glyph inset.** `GRID_CORNER_OPTICAL_OFFSET` is 0, so the 20 px icon box, not the visible glyph, sits 5 px in. The Ionicons glyph has some internal padding, so the visible star will look slightly further in than 5 px. It needs measuring from a device screenshot in 38.6-07. This is a one-number change.
