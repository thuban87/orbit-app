# Phase 38.6 Plan 05: Profile Photo Lightbox, 224 Hero and App-Bar Star Summary

Tapping a contact's Profile photo now opens a reusable full-screen `PhotoLightbox`. It uses the `usePhotoDisplay` source, a `colors.background` scrim at 0.96, a labelled Close photo button, and Back to close. It supports pinch zoom, double-tap zoom and pan while zoomed, all as UI-thread Reanimated worklets. A downward swipe at 1× fades the scrim and closes. The Profile photo is now 224 and grows upward into an overlay app bar, and the favourite star has moved into that bar beside ⋮.

## What was built

- **Task 1 (tracer), `b6bdf5d`:** `PhotoLightbox` is an RN `Modal` (transparent, fade, status/navigation bar translucent, `onRequestClose={onClose}`). It has its own `GestureHandlerRootView` and `UnscopedTheme`. Accessibility focus moves to the ✕ when it opens. It shows a square expo-image at the window's short side with `contentFit="contain"` and the label `Photo of <name>`. `ProfileHero` wraps the Avatar in a `View photo of <name>` button only when a photo exists. `ContactProfileScreen` opens it with `setOverlay("photo")`. The tracer's automated verify was re-run end to end before any expansion task. The plan is autonomous, so there was no human-verify stop.
- **Task 2 (TDD), RED `a0b93c7` then GREEN `07ede3c`:** Pure worklet math, with each callee declared above its callers, and both files added to the worklet forward-reference guard. The gestures are `Gesture.Race(doubleTap, Gesture.Simultaneous(pinch, pan))`:
  - Pinch zooms about the focal point and re-clamps the image to its edges.
  - Pan (`minDistance(8)`) moves the image only while zoomed. At 1×, a one-finger drag drives `dismissY`, and on release `shouldDismiss` either animates the image out and closes or springs it back.
  - Double-tap switches between 1× and 2.5× about the tap point.
  - Shared values reset on every open. Every hook sits above the single `return null`.
  - Animation durations are 0 under OS reduced motion (`useReducedMotionShared`).
- **Task 3 (TDD), RED `1d807a9` then GREEN `de8cd85`:**
  - **Geometry:** new constants and helpers in `contact-profile-logic.ts`. `profileContentTopPadding()` is 20 and `profileNameTop` is 260. Glyph clearance is ≥0 at 360, 393 and 412. Hit-box clearance is ≥0 at 393 and 412, and pinned at -5.5 at 360.
  - **Hero:** `ProfileHero` loses its utility row and favourite props. The Avatar is `PROFILE_HERO_AVATAR_SIZE` with a root gap of `PROFILE_HERO_GAP`.
  - **App bar:** the new exported `ProfileAppBar` is rendered after the ScrollView and absolutely positioned (top/left/right 0, height 56, zIndex 1, `pointerEvents="box-none"`). It holds back, then the star (the same two labels, 44 minimum hit area, the same disabled rule, `Icon favorite` active/default), then ⋮.
  - **Scroll scrim:** when scrolled past 8 dp, a `pointerEvents="none"` `colors.background` backing at 0.92 appears. It is switchable by `PROFILE_APP_BAR_SCROLL_SCRIM`, and its state changes only when the threshold is crossed.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] History reveal would scroll its target under the new overlay bar**
- **Found during:** Task 3
- **Issue:** `ProfileModuleHost` scrolls History's reveal to the section's content y (host y + section y). Once the app bar overlays the ScrollView, that puts the section header under the 56 px bar, and once scrolled, under its 0.92 scrim.
- **Fix:** Added a pure `profileScrollTargetY(y) = max(0, y - PROFILE_APP_BAR.height)`, applied in `ContactProfileScreen`'s `onRequestScrollTo`, and tested it.
- **Files modified:** `src/screens/contact-profile-logic.ts`, `src/screens/ContactProfileScreen.tsx`, `src/screens/contact-profile-logic.test.ts`
- **Commit:** `de8cd85`

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] No `react-test-renderer` for the null → path → null re-render test**
- **Issue:** The plan suggested `react-test-renderer`'s `update`, but the repo does not have it. Installing packages is excluded from auto-fix, and the repo documents "no react-test-renderer" (`use-reduced-motion.ts`).
- **Fix:** `PhotoLightbox.test.tsx` mocks every hook to record its name, renders the same props sequence (null, path, null, path), and asserts that the hook sequences are identical and non-empty and that `console.error` is never called. This is the same invariant React enforces on a kept instance. The test was re-checked after Task 2 added `useSharedValue`, `useMemo` and `useAnimatedStyle`.
- **Commit:** `b6bdf5d`, extended in `07ede3c`

### Small additions within the plan's intent

- `isZoomed(scale)` was added to the logic module as the single 1.01 "zoomed" test shared by `doubleTapTarget`, `shouldDismiss` and the pan handler, so the threshold is not duplicated.
- `profileAppBarScrolled(y)` is a pure form of the scrim threshold test.
- The app-bar render test is a new file, `src/screens/profile-app-bar-render.test.tsx`. It imports the screen with its dependencies stubbed and renders only `ProfileAppBar`, which the plan allowed.
- Pinch updates the translation incrementally about the current focal point, and pan applies `onChange` deltas. This way two fingers pinching and panning at the same time do not overwrite each other's translation.
- Lightbox animations respect OS reduced motion (0 ms).

## Surface to owner

1. **Star hit box overlaps the photo by about 5.5 px at 360 dp** (expected by the plan). At the narrowest supported width, a tap on the top-right edge of the photo circle could toggle Favourite instead of opening the lightbox. Clearing it would mean shrinking the D-04 photo, lowering the name, or moving the D-17 star, which are all owner-set. Nothing was changed. The value is pinned by test for your look in 38.6-07 Task 4. At 393 and 412 dp there is no overlap.
2. **App-bar scroll scrim is on by default** (`PROFILE_APP_BAR_SCROLL_SCRIM = true`, 0.92 over the background once scrolled 8 dp or more) so you can judge it in 38.6-07. Turning it off is a one-line change.
3. **Device checks for 38.6-07 (not verifiable in vitest):**
   - whether a double-tap is recognised cleanly against pan and pinch (`Gesture.Race`);
   - whether TalkBack focus lands on the ✕ when the Modal opens;
   - whether the swipe-down feels right with the 120 dp / 1000 dp/s thresholds.
4. **Pre-existing, not changed:** if the Profile fails to load (error state, no snapshot), no app bar is rendered, so there is no on-screen back button. Hardware Back still works. This was true before this plan.
