  <behavior>
    - `nextAllowedFireInstant(baseDate, deliveryHour, quietStartHour, quietEndHour, staggerMinutes, now)`:
      - Candidate = local midnight of baseDate at deliveryHour:00 plus staggerMinutes.
      - If the candidate hour falls inside the quiet window [quietStart, quietEnd) — and the window WRAPS midnight when quietStart > quietEnd (e.g. 21→08) — roll the candidate to quietEndHour on the correct morning.
      - If the candidate is <= now, roll forward to the next day's delivery slot (still respecting the quiet window).
      - Returns a local Date; hour args are clamped to integer [0,23] (out-of-range or non-integer → clamp, never NaN).
    - Explicit quiet-window-wrap cases: deliveryHour 9 with quiet 21→08 is allowed (9 is outside quiet); deliveryHour 6 with quiet 21→08 rolls to 08:00; a 23:00 candidate rolls to next 08:00.
    - `nextNudgeDate(dueDate, now, cadenceDays)`: returns the first of {dueDate, dueDate+cadence, dueDate+2·cadence, …} whose date is >= today's date (so an overdue contact's next weekly tick is today-or-future) — derived purely, no stored state.
  </behavior>
