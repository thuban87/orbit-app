| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-11-PERM | Denial of service | permission wrappers | low | mitigate | A denied/revoked permission resolves to granted:false; the app degrades to in-app only and never re-prompts (orchestrator pick 6). |
