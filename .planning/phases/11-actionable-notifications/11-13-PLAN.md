| T-11-CHANRACE | Information disclosure | channel-init vs first schedule | high | mitigate | ensureChannels() + ensureNotificationCategories() are AWAITED before installSweepTrigger fires the cold-start reconcile (item 6 / A1), so the first scheduleNotificationAsync always has its (immutable) private/public channel already created — a notification cannot land on a wrong/default channel and leak a name on a fresh-install/restore path. |
| T-11-HEADLESS | Availability | headless task import | medium | mitigate | The headless-task module is imported at module scope so registerTaskAsync runs in the killed-app context; importing it runs nothing else. On-device FCM-less proof is the A2 spike (Pixel-UAT). |
</threat_model>
