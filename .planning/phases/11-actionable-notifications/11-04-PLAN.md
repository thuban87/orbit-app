<objective>
Create the notification read layer: a `decay-suppression` SQL fragment (the "eligible to receive a decay nudge at some future date" predicate, composed from status.ts) and `notification-read.ts` exposing `listDecayEligibleCandidates` (every schedulable, non-suppressed contact — NOT only currently-overdue) and `listBirthdayNotificationCandidates` (every non-archived contact with a birthday). Splitting "eligible for a future date" from "currently overdue" is what lets 11-10 pre-schedule (review H5).

Purpose: the scheduler asks "who could be notified, and from what base date?" here — deriving nothing itself. Rogue is read from status.ts, the birthday parser from birthday-logic.ts; neither is re-implemented.
Output: `decay-suppression.ts`, `notification-read.ts` (+ test).
