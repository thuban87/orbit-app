<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| live DB + settings → OS-scheduled alarms | the reconcile decides what is scheduled and on which channel |
| scheduled body → lock screen | body text is frozen at schedule time and may be glanced |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-11-01 | Information disclosure | scheduled decay body | high | mitigate | Body is generic (name-only via decayBody) — no fuel/PII is ever frozen into a scheduled notification; the private channel is the default. |
| T-11-LOCK | Information disclosure | channel selection | high | mitigate | Decay posts to the private channel unless lockscreen_public is explicitly on; birthday uses the single private channel (OQ-2). |
| T-11-STALE | Integrity | frozen scheduled content | medium | mitigate | Reconcile-on-launch + cancel-on-mark/snooze/mute/purge + generic body ensure a nudge never fires for a since-contacted contact with stale text. |
| T-11-SWEEP | Availability | sweep registration | medium | mitigate | Reconcile registers ONLY via registerSweepHook; import runs nothing and the headless action path can never reach it (Pitfall 5). |
</threat_model>
