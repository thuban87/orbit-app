# Phase 38.11 Plan 08: Profile Birthday tile and sheet Summary

**The Profile shows a Birthday tile after Contact Frequency (completed into saved layouts at read time) and edits the birthday in place from a bottom sheet that saves at once, never stores the 2000 placeholder, and offers Undo on Clear or a dropped year.**

**What changed for the user:**

- The Relationship Overview has a Birthday tile, right after Contact Frequency. It shows "May 3, 1990", or "Dec 31" when the year is unknown.
- The tile only appears when the person has a birthday. Without one, the other tiles close up with no gap.
- Older layouts get the tile in the same place, on the Profile and in the layout editor.
- Tapping the tile opens a Birthday sheet, not Edit Contact. It has the date, Year unknown, Clear and Close.
- Picking a date saves straight away and says "Birthday saved".
- Turning Year unknown off asks for the year. Cancel leaves it on. The year 2000 is never saved unless picked.
- Clear removes the birthday, closes the sheet and says "Birthday cleared" with Undo.
- Turning Year unknown on for a birthday with a year saves straight away, with Undo to get the year back.
- If a save fails, the sheet stays open and says "Couldn't save the birthday. Try again."
- A save reschedules the birthday reminder and refreshes the widget.

## Owner calls made by the executor

These are small presentation choices inside the plan's "Claude's discretion" space. The owner sees all of them at the 38.11-14 device check:

1. **"Birthday cleared" uses the warning (removal) snackbar,** and "Birthday saved" / "Birthday restored" use success. This follows the kinds documented in `snackbar-store.ts`. The plan named the copy but not the kind.
2. **Undo screen-reader labels:** "Undo clear birthday" and "Undo year unknown".
3. **"Saving…" caption while a save is pending,** reusing the Profile section header's existing word. The controls are blocked until the save settles.
4. **Error feedback is the error snackbar only,** with no inline caption or Retry. This follows the plan's "errors use the warning snackbar" note. The Frequency and Snooze sheets show an inline error plus Retry instead.

None of these reverses a recorded decision.
