<objective>
The Profile's Background sheet becomes a compact chooser for this person (D-58 points 2-4): global and
category management moves out (it lives on the Settings page from 38.11-06), cards lose Delete and shrink
to a 2x2 layout, a "Manage global backgrounds" button links to Settings, Reset to default joins the sheet
(D-65 background half), Choose photo stays (D-79) and the header gets the standard Back (D-43).

Decided here ("this plan only"; RESEARCH assumption A2): a card's Assign applies the background to this
person at once (with a confirming snackbar), since the sheet no longer offers global or category scope.
"Reset to default" replaces the separate "Use inherited background" action (both cleared the person's own
background) and shows only when the person has one. It is reversible (the background stays in the library),
so per 38.9 D-30 (`docs/systems/ux-standards.md` "Destructive actions": a reversible action happens at once
and shows a snackbar with Undo) it needs no confirm but its snackbar carries Undo, which re-assigns the
previous background; tapping Undo shows its own confirming snackbar (38.9 D-39). This differs from the
layout reset in 38.11-05, which discards the person's layout and so asks first. Copy (Claude's discretion;
owner sees it at the 38.11-14 backgrounds checkpoint): snackbars "Background set for {name}" / "Background
reset to default" (Undo) / "Background restored".
Output: slimmed sheet, model updates, contract test, one ContactProfileScreen prop.
</objective>
