See `sheet-contract.ts:31` (`expanded: false`) and `Sheet.tsx:105-114`; the prop is documented as "Ignored by `expanded`".
- **Actual cause:** the warning comes from `NestedReorderableList` (a FlatList, `ProfileLayoutEditor.tsx:191-202`) inside `ScrollViewContainer` (`:394`). The library defaults `scrollEnabled` to true (`react-native-reorderable-list@0.18.1 ReorderableListCore.js:45`). React Native warns whenever `scrollEnabled !== false` (`VirtualizedList.js:1156-1165`).
- **Failure:** the acceptance check `grep scrollBody` passes with a no-op change, and the warning stays.
- **Fix:** rewrite step 3 to address the list itself. Options: pass `scrollEnabled={false}` to `NestedReorderableList` and check that drag still works (the library toggles the prop during drag, `ReorderableListCore.js:261-276`); or render the buckets without virtualization; or scope a `LogBox.ignoreLogs`. Replace the `scrollBody` grep with a render test that asserts the prop.
