# 38.15 seed: group messaging (message several people at once)

**Status:** idea captured for this phase's dossier; not yet interrogated or decided. Raised by the owner 2026-10-19 during 38.13 execution. Owner placement: "Yes, add to 38.15 for now please" (after the orchestrator recommended 38.15 over 38.14 and over a separate phase; the owner wants it before Phase 39).

## The idea (owner's words, condensed)

"A way to message a certain group of people at once." From a group event, a button hands every member who has a phone number to the phone's texting app (or every email address to the email app). The same path from Contacts multi-select, without an event. Compose works the same way, but its final step passes several recipients instead of one. Owner: "I think this would be an insanely useful feature."

## Surfaces named by the owner

1. Group events: "Message group" from an event (fits this phase's future-event planning).
2. Contacts Card-view multi-select: a Message action for the selected people. 38.13 D-90 retired the per-card menu, which had a single-person Message; the selection bar has no Message yet.
3. Compose: multi-recipient hand-off.

## Orchestrator notes for the dossier (not decisions)

- **Existing threads:** the texting app decides, not Orbit. For plain MMS groups, Google Messages usually reopens the thread with the same participant set. RCS group chats are keyed by a group ID, so the same numbers may open a new thread. Test on the owner's phone before promising it.
- **Mechanics:** an Android `smsto:` intent with several numbers (the separator varies by OEM) and `mailto:` with several addresses. Local-first holds: no network, just an intent to the user's app.
- **Open questions:** people with no number, or with several (which number?); telling the user who was left out; carrier group-text caps (often ~10–20); privacy (a group text shows everyone's number and sends every reply to everyone; email could offer BCC); WhatsApp, Signal and other apps can't take a recipient list this way.
- **Owner decision required (CLAUDE.md data-layer rules):** AI-drafting one message for several people sends several people's data to the configured provider. That widens what the AI feature transmits.
- **Rough size:** about 2–3 plans (event Message, selection-bar Message, Compose multi-recipient). AI compose and per-person number-choice settings not counted.
