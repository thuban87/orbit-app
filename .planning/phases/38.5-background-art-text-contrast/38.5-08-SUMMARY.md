# Phase 38.5 Plan 08: Signed v3 Art Treatments, Proof and ADR-177 Summary

PLAN08_BASE=8922dedc5a12f3f4752e1eeea66401d396c56d9f

**The owner's signed v3 answers now drive the five table-driven components.** A per-cell guard syncs the table with `38.5-scrim-signoff-v3.json`. ## Accomplishments

- **The table.** `ART_TREATMENTS` is built from `SIGNED_V3_BACKINGS`, a readable F/T/N row per combination transcribed from the v3 file. Every cell is `mode`, has no local ⋯ backing, and keeps `activeTriggerBacking` at `none` (I1 was not ruled). `TABLE_DRIVEN_COMPONENTS` lists the five components.
- **The levels.** `ART_SEE_THROUGH_OPACITY` now holds the signed levels:
  - 0.05 for every List row and Contacts card in all four package × mode pairs;
  - Galaxy Dark chrome 0.05 and Standard Light chrome 0.5, both kept by reference;
  - the Standard Dark count label, 0.
  `CARD_GLASS_OPACITY` and the bodies of `cardMatchesMode`, `cardTintOpacity` and `chromeScrimOpacity` are unchanged; only their comments were updated (D-28).
- **The proof.** 67 cases (40 see-through, 27 none), one per combination × table-driven component. Each proves the foregrounds the component actually paints:
  - List and Card entries: `textPrimary`, `textSecondary` and `accentText` (every accent), each at 4.5; the status hues and `rogue` at 3.0.
  - Count label: `textSecondary`.
  - Contacts header: `textPrimary` (title) and `textSecondary` (⋯).
  - Digest header: `textPrimary`.
  See-through cells use the signed tint over each variant's declared extrema with the palette the component renders (glass in Standard Light over art, root elsewhere). None cells use the bare veil model at every density. No exclusion was needed. E-1 was not extended, because no table-driven component renders `danger`.
- **The regimes.** 11 regime rows were added, one per signed group. `measure-background-extrema.py` accepts the three treatments, and self-test fixtures were added. `--check` passes, and the Starfield `featureAllowance` was re-evaluated over the union that includes the new regimes: within the allowance, 446 px / 50 components / largest 13 px and 2680 px / 380 components / largest 9 px.
- **ADR-177.** The superseded-by lines on ADR-115, ADR-087 and ADR-113 changed by one line each. The registry and index were regenerated (177 ADRs, 39 superseded). The runbook and `app-shell.md` have 38.5 changelog entries, and the stale "Grouped shared-library picker" line is gone.
