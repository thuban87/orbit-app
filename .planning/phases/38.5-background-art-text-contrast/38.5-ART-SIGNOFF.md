## 5. Addendum (2026-09-29) — the declared Starfield text-bearing bounds

**Owner acknowledgement, verbatim (2026-09-29, on the phase code review's WR-02):** "Accepted and noted".

§2 records the checker's channel-wise `textBearingBound` for the two Starfield variants. 38.5-05 declared different
bounds for two of those four cells in `src/theme/backgrounds.ts`. The code keeps them.

| Variant | Field | Checker's bound (§2) | Declared (`backgrounds.ts`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| galaxy-starfield · light | `darkestPixel` | `#CED9DC` (L = 0.679) | **`#D3DDE0`** (L = 0.709) |
| galaxy-starfield · light | `brightestPixel` | `#FFFFFF` | `#FFFFFF` (same) |
| galaxy-starfield · dark | `darkestPixel` | `#000000` | `#000000` (same) |
| galaxy-starfield · dark | `brightestPixel` | `#2C2868` (L = 0.0305) | **`#262452`** (L = 0.0228) |

**Why they differ.** The checker's bound is channel-wise: each channel's minimum or maximum over the text-bearing
pixels. That makes a synthetic colour that no pixel of the file has, and it sits further out than any real text-bearing
pixel. Over the checker's bounds the TS proof fails on colours that no text-bearing pixel produces:

- Starfield dark, card and chrome `textSecondary`: 4.36:1 (floor 4.5).
- Starfield light, bare coral `accentText` at the presentation veil: 4.37:1 (floor 4.5).

The per-pixel checker passes both files. Each declared bound therefore sits inside an empty luminance band of its own
file, between the text-bearing pixels and the stars:

- **Dark.** The brightest non-star pixel is `#222544` (L = 0.0208); the dimmest star is L ≥ 0.064. `#262452` (L = 0.0228)
  sits in that gap.
- **Light.** No pixel lies between L 0.3 and L 0.740; the darkest non-star pixel is `#D6E0F2`. `#D3DDE0` (L = 0.709)
  sits in that gap. A bound brighter than about L 0.72 starts admitting background pixels and fails `--check`, so the
  declared value keeps a margin.

**The same pixels are text-bearing.** `measure-background-extrema.py --check` over the declared bounds gives exactly
the signed allowance unions, reproduced on 2026-09-29:

- Starfield dark: 2,680 px (0.170%), 380 components, largest 9 px.
- Starfield light: 446 px (0.028%), 50 components, largest 13 px.

No star is re-admitted and no background pixel is excluded. No art, opacity, allowance (D-31: 18 px / 0.5%) or proof
changed.

**Luminance-valid, not channel-wise.** The dark bound `#262452` is not a channel-wise upper bound of the text-bearing
pixels: the real pixel `#222544` has G `0x25` > `0x24`. It is valid because every check (the TS proof,
`--check` and the per-pixel checker) compares composite relative luminance, and every text-bearing pixel composites
below it under every regime. `measure-background-extrema.py`'s header records that an allowance variant's declared
bound may be luminance-only.

The source of the deviation is `38.5-05-SUMMARY.md` (deviations, the two Starfield entries). Mirrored in the dossier
D-31 entry and in D-31 of `38.5-CONTEXT.md`.
