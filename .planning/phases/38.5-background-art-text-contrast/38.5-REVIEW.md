## Warnings

### WR-01: A see-through List row shows the swipe action panel through its content mid-swipe (OWNER-DECISION)

**File:** `src/components/ListRow.tsx:194-212`, `src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx:227-253`, `src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx:349-372`

**Issue:** The swipe actions sit behind the row:

- `SwipeableListRow` wraps each `ListRow` in RNGH `ReanimatedSwipeable`.
- Its action containers are absolute-fill layers behind the row, at opacity 0 at rest and 1 as soon as a swipe starts (`ReanimatedSwipeable.tsx:372-405`).
- `SwipeActionSurface` paints a 96 px `surfaceElevated` panel with a `textSecondary` icon and a `textPrimary` "Log"/"Edit" label.

Before 38.5 the row had a solid `surface` fill, so the panel was only ever visible beside the row. Now the signed table makes the row `seeThrough` at 0.05 in 12 of 16 combinations. `listRowBacking` then drops the solid fill and draws a 5% tint.

**Failure scenario:** The user drags a row about 40 px right in Galaxy Dark · Aurora.

- Roughly 56 px of the translated row overlaps the revealed "Log" panel.
- The panel's icon and label show through the row's avatar and name at 95% strength, so the text overlaps other text.
- The row's name and recency then sit on `surfaceElevated` plus panel text, not on the art.
- The signed-treatment proof (`surface.test.ts` "Signed art treatments") never models that background.

The effect lasts only for the swipe, but it happens on every swipe.

**Fix (owner's call, not a routine fix):** Any change alters a signed List-row treatment during an interaction, so under D-28 it belongs to the owner. It is already listed for the 38.5-09 gap list ("a see-through List row mid-swipe"). Options to present:

- **(a) Solid fill while dragging.** The row draws its solid `surface` fill while the swipe is open (drive `solidFill` from `onSwipeableWillOpen`/`onSwipeableClose` state, or put an opaque `surface` underlay View inside the Swipeable child that shows only while dragging).
- **(b) Inset the panel.** The action panel draws its content inset so it never sits under the translated row.
- **(c) Accept it.**

Do not change the signed 0.05 level.

### WR-02: Declared Starfield text-bearing bounds differ from the values the signed art record says 38.5-05 declares

**File:** `src/theme/backgrounds.ts:162-169` (light `darkestPixel: "#D3DDE0"`) and `src/theme/backgrounds.ts:179-187` (dark `brightestPixel: "#262452"`), against `.planning/phases/38.5-background-art-text-contrast/38.5-ART-SIGNOFF.md:125-132` (`#CED9DC` and `#2C2868`, "38.5-05 declares these for the two Starfield variants")

**Issue:** The owner-signed record fixes the text-bearing bound as the checker's channel-wise `textBearingBound`. The code declares different values, and both are less conservative:

- the light darkest bound is brighter than the signed value;
- the dark brightest bound is darker than the signed value.

The reason is recorded only as an executor deviation in `38.5-05-SUMMARY.md:201-209`. Over the signed bounds the TS proof fails:

- Starfield dark card/chrome `textSecondary` is 4.36:1;
- Starfield light bare coral `accentText` is 4.37:1.

The chosen bounds sit in empty luminance bands of each file. I re-ran `measure-background-extrema.py --check`, and it confirms the out-of-bound union equals the signed allowance union (2,680 px / 0.170%; 446 px / 0.028%). The per-pixel checker also passes. So users see no contrast regression.

The problem is the record. It still says the proof bound is `#2C2868`/`#CED9DC`. A future re-check against the ART-SIGNOFF table (or a reviewer syncing code to it, as this review was asked to) finds a mismatch with no signed explanation. The dark bound `#262452` is also not a channel-wise upper bound: the real pixel `#222544` has G `0x25` > `0x24`. It is valid only because every check compares composite luminance. That departs from the "channel-wise bound" contract stated in `measure-background-extrema.py:26-30`.

**Fix:** Keep the code. Append an addendum to `38.5-ART-SIGNOFF.md` §2, and mirror it in the dossier's D-31 entry. The addendum should record:

- the declared bounds;
- the empty-band rationale;
- the reproduced unions;
- the fact that the bound is luminance-valid, not channel-wise.

Put it on the owner's end-of-phase list for acknowledgement. It does not change the signed allowance. It does change what the signed record says is declared. Also add one sentence to the `measure-background-extrema.py` header saying an allowance variant's declared bound may be luminance-only.

### WR-03: The bare-text classifier counts table-driven `ChromeScrim`/`ShellAppBar` text as always backed, which will skew the 38.5-09 D-24/D-25 report

**File:** `src/theme/__contract__/bare-text-sites.ts:17`, `:140`, `:666-667`, `:753-758`

**Issue:** `GLASS_TAGS` (from `SCOPE_ELEMENTS`) treats every `<ChromeScrim>` and `<ShellAppBar>` as a glass backing, unconditionally. The file never mentions `artComponent`. Since 38.5-06/08, the opted-in instances draw no backing in the signed `none` cells:

- `HomeScreen.tsx:1627-1631` (count label), N in 7 combinations;
- `HomeScreen.tsx:1761-1766` (Contacts header title and ⋯), N in 11;
- `DigestScreen.tsx:239` (Digest header), N in 11.

In those cells the text sits directly on the art, which is D-02's definition of bare.

**Failure scenario:** 38.5-09 re-runs `scripts/bare-text-inventory.ts` on HEAD to produce:

- the final D-24 figure (red sites ÷ all bare sites);
- the D-25 red-string report for the owner (38.5-09-PLAN lines 33, 55).

Those sites will be reported as `backed:glass`, so the denominator and the bare-site list the owner decides from will be wrong. Contrast is still proven for them, separately, in `surface.test.ts` "Signed art treatments". The defect is the accuracy of an owner-facing report, not what the user sees.

**Fix:** In `bare-text-sites.ts`, treat a `ChromeScrim`/`ShellAppBar` whose opening element carries an `artComponent` attribute as a per-combination backing. The simplest option is to classify its descendants `mixed` with a note naming the table cell. Add a fixture in `bare-text-sites.test.ts`. This file is already in 38.5-09's `<files>`, so it fits that plan.

### IN-04: The manifest parser can read a commented-out declaration

**File:** `scripts/background_manifest.py:221-223`

**Issue:** `re.search(r'brightestPixel: "(#…)"', vbody)` (and the `darkestPixel` / `require(` equivalents) takes the first match anywhere in the variant body, comment lines included. The Starfield variants already carry long comment blocks that discuss the old signed bounds. A future edit that quotes one as `darkestPixel: "#CED9DC"` inside a comment would be parsed as the declaration. The Python validators would then check a different bound from the one the app and the TS proof use, and nothing would report it.

**Fix:** Strip `//` line comments from `vbody` before matching, or anchor the patterns to `^        (brightestPixel|darkestPixel): "` with `re.M`.

### IN-05: The release-guard regex misses template-literal specifiers

**File:** `src/theme/dev-override-release-guard.test.ts:40`

**Issue:** `DEV_SPECIFIER` matches only `"…"` / `'…'` specifiers, so `require(\`./__dev__/x\`)` would pass unflagged. The guard is also static: it proves the source shape, not the release bundle.

**Fix:** Add backtick quoting to `DEV_SPECIFIER`. Optionally, have the 38.5-09 release-build step grep the release bundle for `art-treatment-dev-overrides` / `ArtSheetComboSync`.

### New findings

#### WR-A (WARNING, OWNER-DECISION on the fix): Merge asks the D-47 question, and Cancel aborts the whole merge, even when the backup's settings will not be applied

**File:** `src/screens/backup-restore-logic.ts:104-155` (`restoreApplyConfirmation` / `confirmRestoreApply`), `src/screens/RestorePreviewScreen.tsx:334-350` (preview notice), `src/backup/restore-apply.ts:1489-1502` (gate) against `:1639-1641` (`applySettings`) and `:1797-1812`

**Issue:** In Merge, the backup's `appSettings` are written only when `manifest.appSettings.modifiedAt > local settings.modifiedAt` (`restore-apply.ts:1639-1641`). The D-47 prompt and the gate do not look at this. They fire whenever the backup holds an unavailable id, whether or not the settings will be written.

**Failure scenario:**

1. The user changes a setting on this phone after taking a backup on a newer app version that had an extra background slot.
2. The user restores that backup with Merge.
3. The preview says "Orbit will ask before switching to the default background", and the dialog asks "Switch to the default background instead?"
4. **If they press Continue:** nothing about the background changes, because the settings are older and are skipped. The copy told them something false.
5. **If they press Cancel:** the whole merge (contacts, interactions and so on) is abandoned over a setting that would never have been written.

D-47's record says "Continue restores everything, with that package's background set to its default". That does not hold on this path. No data is lost or corrupted; the consent dialog is wrong or unneeded in this case.

**Fix:** The owner decides which option. Both keep the DAO strict and change no format.

- **(a) Ask only when the swap will happen.** Read the local `app_settings.modified_at` in `confirmRestoreApply` for Merge, the same way it already reads the destination for Replace-all, and pass `unavailableBackground && (mode === "replace-all" || backupModifiedAt > localModifiedAt)`. Show the preview notice under the same condition. In `applyRestore`, move the unavailable gate after `applySettings` is known, or pass the same predicate, so an unconsented Merge whose settings are skipped still applies. This narrows "Merge shows it as a Continue/Cancel dialog" to "when the backup's settings will be applied", which is why it is the owner's call.
- **(b) Keep always-ask, fix the copy.** For Merge, say something like "…if this backup's settings are newer than this phone's, the background will switch to the default." That makes the notice truthful without changing behaviour.

#### IN-A (INFO): the text-never-reaches-the-glyphs geometry rests on a hand-measured constant at font scale 1

**File:** `src/theme/tokens/surface.test.ts:2006` (`PANEL_GLYPH_EXTENT_MEASURED = 60`), `:2030-2056`; `src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx:2474-2492` (`swipeAction`, `swipeActionLabel` 13 / 600)

**Issue:** The exemption can stay text-free only because the content column (74 dp in) never reaches the panel glyphs (measured at about 60 dp). The geometry test pins:

- the panel width;
- the centring;
- the row insets.

It does not pin:

- the label's `fontSize: 13`;
- the `gap: 4`;
- the icon size.

The app also honours OS font scaling. At 200% the "Edit" label is about 48 dp wide, which puts the glyph extent at about 72 dp against a 74 dp content inset, a 2 dp margin. A small label or icon change, or a longer localized label, would put text over the glyphs, where it measures 3.21:1 (textPrimary) and 1.28:1 (textSecondary). No test would fail.

**Fix:** In the geometry test, pin `swipeActionLabel` `fontSize: 13` and `gap: 4` and the icon size. Better, derive the glyph extent from them at the maximum supported font scale, or set `maxFontSizeMultiplier` on the panel label so the extent is bounded by construction.

#### IN-B (INFO): D-47 "no snapshot, no staging" assertions are vacuous in the fixture

**File:** `src/backup/restore-apply.test.ts:2631-2653`

**Issue:** Neither `not.toHaveBeenCalled()` assertion can fail in this fixture:

**Fix:** Set `backup_folder_uri` on the destination, and give the source contact a photo (with `readPhotoBase64` returning data). Then assert that the same fixture with `unavailableBackgrounds: "use-default"` DOES call both, so the negative assertions have teeth.
