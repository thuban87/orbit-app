**How the new kinds are counted (C2-M3).**
- The analyzer is now treatment-aware (M-2). It adds two `bare_kind` values:
  - `see-through-entry`: text on a List row or Card entry whose signed cell is see-through in at least one

  when the token is `danger`. Under bare + mixed, a row counts once.
- The per-combination breakdown below counts such a row as bare only in the combinations where its text is on the
  art. Everywhere else it counts as backed.
