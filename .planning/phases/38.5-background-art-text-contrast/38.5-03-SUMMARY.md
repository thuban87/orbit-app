# Phase 38.5 Plan 03: Art-acceptance proof tooling Summary

**A committed art checker now runs on constants a vitest guard ties to the TS theme. It reproduces the brief's Aurora
baseline: H1 PASS at max L\* 18.3, H2 FAIL on one pixel. The extrema script now validates every variant under the
BackgroundHost veil too, and it can enforce an owner-signed small-feature allowance with the same union semantics as
the checker. Proof exclusions now name their treatments: E-1 covers card and chrome only, and bare text gets
`E-1-bare` because D-24 came out `extend`. A bare-text proof exists and is enforced over the None background.**

## Accomplishments

- **Checker (`scripts/check-background-art.py`).** It keeps the brief's model: the veil blended in sRGB with JS
  rounding, the whole canvas, all 8 accents and the card/chrome section. It adds:
  - `textPlaceholder` among the bare tokens;
  - `--json` with per-token worst ratio and failing %, `unionFailingPct`, 8-connected component count and largest,
    L\* min/p1/median/p99/max, the card/chrome verdict and `result`;
  - `--feature-max-px`/`--feature-max-pct`, plus `textBearingBound` when an allowance is given;
  - `--all` over the manifest, which applies each variant's `featureAllowance` and applies
    `--exclude-galaxy-dark-danger` only to galaxy/dark variants;
  - `--accepted-exclusions` with `--slot`, which reports the excluded pixel count per record;
  - `--self-test` with 39 fixtures (uniform, allowance, union, exclusion, schema, and the shared C2-L3 fixture).
  - `grep -c "#[0-9A-Fa-f]\{6\}"` on the checker returns 0.
- **Constants (`scripts/art-checker-constants.json`).** It holds `veil`, `surface`, `card`, `bare`, `accentText`,
  `standardLightGlass` and `floors`, keyed `galaxy-dark` … `standard-light`. `art-checker-constants.test.ts` rebuilds
  the file from `resolvePalette`, `resolveAccent`, `resolveGlassForegroundPalette`, `backgroundVeilOpacity` and
  `cardTintOpacity`, and names every stale, missing or extra key. Mutation check: changing one hex (Standard Light
  glass coral) fails the guard with `stale standardLightGlass.accentText.coral`; reverted.
- **Shared parser (`scripts/background_manifest.py`).** Both scripts import it:
  - `parse_manifest()` returns one record per slot/mode, with `featureAllowance` parsed and validated. It accepts the
    one-line form and the formatter-wrapped form.
  - `parse_accepted_exclusions()` enforces the whole C2-M2 schema:
    - `id` of the form `X-<n>`, unique;
    - the token and treatment vocabularies;
    - `card` and `chrome` together or neither (C3-L3);
    - exactly one of `region` / `feature`;
    - a `region` only with `bare`/`card`/`chrome` (C3-M1);
    - dates;
    - allowance fields, unique per slotId × mode;
    - unknown keys rejected.
  - `ALLOWANCE_FIXTURE` is the one synthetic image both self-tests build.
- **Extrema script.**
  - The regime table now has 20 rows, adding 12 `veil` rows with `density`. A veil row without a valid density is
    rejected.
  - `--check` with an allowance takes the union of out-of-bound pixels across every regime and measures its 8-connected
    components. Without an allowance it behaves as before.
  - `--help` states the union semantics.
  - The self-test covers:
    - the 2-px allowance fixture both ways;
    - union fixtures (disjoint pixels that each fit fail together, and the same pixels in two regimes count once);
    - the C2-L3 shared fixture;
    - the real-manifest `featureAllowance` line;
    - eight malformed-`allowances` cases.
- **`backgrounds.ts`.** `BackgroundVariant.featureAllowance?` is documented with the union semantics. No variant has
  one.
- **`surface.test.ts`.**
  - `ProofExclusion.treatment` is a required list drawn from card, chrome, bare, listEntry, cardEntry and artChrome.
    `slotId` and `inventoryPath` are new optional fields.
  - The pure `matchesExclusion()` sits behind `isExcluded()`.
  - The card/chrome suites pass `slotId` and `treatment`, and the `TEXT_FGS` loops now honour exclusions.
  - The exclusion suite checks each row in the entry's own inventory. It accepts only the ADR-084 Galaxy Dark danger
    shape or ART-SIGNOFF variant allowances.
  - `BARE_FOREGROUNDS` and `bareTextFailures()` are enforced over the None background: 4 pairs × 3 densities × default
    and all 8 accents.

## Decisions Made

- **The allowance union covers both checker sections.** M-4 says the union covers "every foreground token's failure
  mask". The card/chrome tokens are foreground tokens too, and the extrema script unions card, chrome and veil
  regimes. So the checker unions the bare and card/chrome failure masks. Without an allowance any failing pixel in
  either section fails, exactly as the brief did.
- **`--margin` stays bare-only.** This is the brief's meaning: its card/chrome proof always ran at margin 0.
- **The card/chrome section's token set is the TS card proof's.** It is textPrimary, textSecondary, danger, status,
  rogue and every accent, with no `textPlaceholder`. In Standard Light the glass palette supplies them. Galaxy Dark
  `danger` is always dropped there (E-1, card + chrome).
- **Checker `--slot`.** Exclusion records are keyed by slot × mode, and a single image has no slot. So
  `--accepted-exclusions` needs `--slot`, and without it the checker exits 2 rather than silently applying nothing.
- **Sign-off `allowances` are validated, not applied, by the checker.** The checker applies `featureAllowance` from
  the manifest (`--all`) or from the flags. 38.5-05's sync test ties the sign-off allowances to `featureAllowance`.
- **Variant mode.** `ProofExclusion.mode` already exists and a variant renders only in its own mode, so only `slotId`
  was added. The doc comment says so.

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] `featureAllowance` also parses in the formatter-wrapped form**

- **Found during:** Task 1/2.
- **Issue:** The plan puts `featureAllowance` "on one line". The one-line form is 91 characters, and Biome's default
  line width (80) would wrap it into a multi-line object on the first format pass, which a one-line parser would then
  reject.
