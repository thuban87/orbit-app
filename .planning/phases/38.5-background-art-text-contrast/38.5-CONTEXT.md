<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Make every piece of text that sits directly on shipped background art (bare text, the former 38.4 finding F-1) meet its
WCAG floor (4.5:1 text and links, 3:1 glyphs and large text) in every theme × mode × background combination. The primary
fix is regenerated art, with separate light and dark versions of each kept background. Scrims apply only where the owner's
signed-off combination sheet says. User-uploaded custom backgrounds are excluded.

**Trigger to plan:** Phase 38.4 complete, the owner-signed scrim combination sheet exists (D-04), and the discuss session
has resolved the OPEN items below.
</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

Restated from the dossier and the 38.4 owner rulings. These are not new owner rulings. Rulings from the discuss session
get appended here as D-08+.

- **D-01:** The authoritative contract is the 38.5 dossier. The primary input is the 38.4 background-art brief and its
  supporting files. The brief's numbers carry MEASURED / COMPUTED / ESTIMATED labels, and plans keep them.
- **D-02 (carries 38.4 D-38):** the scope is text that sits directly on the background art, with no glass or opaque
  backing, across every theme × mode × background. 38.4 did not fix it (its Plan 16 scrim task was cancelled). The current
  site list is the brief's `bare-text-sites.csv`: 294 sites on 50 routes, of which 100 are hand-verified and 194 are
  AST-traced only, plus 24 mixed components.
- **D-03 (carries 38.4 D-39):** Galaxy and Standard each keep both a light and a dark mode. Never propose collapsing the
  packages into one Dark/Light switch; that was never the owner's intent. The fix direction is separate light and dark art
  per background. The owner may cut one background per theme (about 12 images). Galaxy light mode may use different
  pictures rather than recoloured dark ones.
- **D-04 (carries 38.4 D-40):** scrims are not abolished. Buttons that open overlay menus, and the overlay menus
  themselves, keep their scrims. Every other per-combination treatment (full, transparent or none; per component, per
  theme × mode × background) comes from the owner-signed screenshot sheet. The planner does not choose scrim placement.
- **D-05 (carries 38.4 D-41):** user-uploaded custom backgrounds (for example contact profile photos) carry no contrast
  guarantee and get no adaptive-text engineering.
- **D-06:** owner-bucket, as in 38.4: any protected-hue retune (ADR-084), glass or veil opacity change (ADR-115), art
  direction or look, and any reversal of a recorded ADR/HANDOFF decision.
- **D-07:** the asset contract. Every new asset ships with measured extrema (`scripts/measure-background-extrema.py
  --check`), updated `darkestPixel`/`brightestPixel` in `src/theme/backgrounds.ts` and `assets/backgrounds/README.md`, and
  green `surface.test.ts`/`backgrounds.test.ts`. A bare-text contrast proof is added (brief H3–H5). Any schema or
  persisted-setting change is a new forward-only migration at head+1, verified on disk at plan time, and a backup-format
  bump is an owner decision.
</decisions>
