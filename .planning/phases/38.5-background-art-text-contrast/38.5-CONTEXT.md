## Phase Boundary

Make every piece of text that sits directly on shipped background art (bare text, the former 38.4 finding F-1) meet its
WCAG floor (4.5:1 text and links, 3:1 glyphs and large text) in every theme × mode × background combination. The primary
fix is regenerated art, with separate light and dark versions of each kept background. Scrims apply only where the owner's
signed-off combination sheet says (D-08). Where the sheet thins or removes a backing over the art, the text on the art
takes the colour that suits the art (D-10). User-uploaded custom backgrounds are excluded. The phase runs art-first: new
art, then an owner pause, then a re-sign-off on the new art, then the scrim and text-colour work (D-13).
