<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Make every piece of text that sits directly on shipped background art (bare text, the former 38.4 finding F-1) meet its
WCAG floor (4.5:1 text and links, 3:1 glyphs and large text) in every theme × mode × background combination. The primary
fix is regenerated art, with separate light and dark versions of each kept background. Scrims apply only where the owner's
signed-off combination sheet says (D-08). Where the sheet thins or removes a backing over the art, the text on the art
takes the colour that suits the art (D-10). User-uploaded custom backgrounds are excluded. The phase runs art-first: new
art, then an owner pause, then a re-sign-off on the new art, then the scrim and text-colour work (D-13).

**Trigger to plan:** Phase 38.4 complete, the owner-signed scrim combination sheet exists (D-04; **met 2026-09-27**,
D-08), the O-9 `codex-edu` image spike has run (D-14), and the discuss session has resolved the OPEN items below.
</domain>

- **D-09 (owner, 2026-09-27):** in Galaxy Light and Standard Dark, the "transparent" contact entries (List rows and Card
  view cards) are **truly see-through**.
  - **Galaxy Light:** like Galaxy Dark's card transparency, not frosted white. Their text flips to light (D-10). Every
    other scrimmed component in Galaxy Light keeps the frosted-light look.
  - **Standard Dark:** likewise truly transparent. Their font flips to suit the art beneath (D-10). Every other scrimmed
    component keeps the slightly-black scrim where the table says T and is fully black where it says F.
  - This, see-through List rows (solid in every combination today), and N for the count label and headers reverse
    parts of **ADR-115**:
    - ADR-115 keeps opaque cards in mismatched package/mode pairings and rejects "Always-translucent cards".
    - It also gives bare chrome a local `ChromeScrim`.
  - These are the owner's calls (D-06), made with today's rule printed on the sheet. Planning needs a new ADR that
    supersedes those parts. D-09 covers contact entries only; other content cards keep ADR-115 (see O-8).
  - *Confirmed by the owner as D-15 (2026-09-27).*
- **D-10 (owner, 2026-09-27): text-colour rule.** Wherever a component goes from full to transparent or none over the
  art, the text sitting on the art takes the colour that suits the art, not the mode default.
  - Examples: white text over dark Galaxy art in Galaxy Light; dark text over light art in Standard Dark.
  - Text on a truly transparent contact entry (D-09) counts as text on the art.
  - Text not on the art keeps the mode's default colours.
  - All colours resolve through theme tokens. The dossier's P-9 covers how the rule interacts with the new
    mode-specific art and the bare-text proof.
- **D-11 (owner, 2026-09-27):** Digest content (section headings, Up Next items, Horizon items, Your Week) has **no
  scrim** in every combination, including Galaxy Light and Standard Dark. It relies on D-10 and the new art. The Digest
  header follows the table.
  - **WATCH:** the Digest outliers must be checked during 38.5: Galaxy Light, Standard Dark, and mixed art such as Dusk
    and Mesh. Plans carry an explicit check for each.
- **D-12 (owner, 2026-09-27):** the Contacts pop-up menus (Population, Filters, Sort) and all Orrery controls and menus
  keep a **full scrim everywhere**. This reaffirms D-04 (38.4 D-40).
- **D-13 (owner, 2026-09-27): art-first sequencing.**
  1. New background art is produced first, in the early plans (the D-14 loop).
  2. An owner review pause follows; at minimum, the blocking art sign-off that ends the D-14 loop.
  3. A plan redoes the scrim sign-off sheet on the **new** backgrounds, with every v2 choice (D-08..D-12) actually
     rendered or mocked, at least for the screens and components the owner changed.
  4. Only then come the scrim and text-colour implementation plans.
  - Rationale: scrims changed before the art would have to be redone.
  - The implementation builds from the re-signed sheet. Where the owner changes a cell there, the newer answer governs.
- **D-14 (owner, 2026-09-27): Claude/Codex split.**
  - The owner runs discuss, plan and plan-review-convergence with Claude, as usual.
  - In execution, background images are made by a Codex agent. The Claude orchestrator invokes it through the owner's

  - The art task is an iterative loop:
    1. Claude hands Codex the art brief, the target bands, `check_art.py` and the owner's direction.
    2. Codex produces the images.
    3. Claude verifies them with `check_art.py`, `measure-background-extrema.py --check` and a visual review against the
