## 1. Method

**Analyzer (committed, re-runnable).** `src/theme/__contract__/bare-text-sites.ts` is TEST/TOOL SUPPORT ONLY, never
imported by app code. It uses the TypeScript compiler API and replaces the brief's uncommitted scratch walker. Its
tests are `src/theme/bare-text-sites.test.ts` (9 fixtures). The CLI is `scripts/bare-text-inventory.ts`:

```bash
npx tsx scripts/bare-text-inventory.ts --out <csv> [--route <Name>] [--compare <csv>] [--sites <json>]
```

- **Corpus:** every non-test `.tsx` under `src/screens` and `src/components`, excluding `__dev__` folders and the
  Orrery Skia canvas components. Route registrations and render sites are also read from `src/navigation`.
- **Foregrounds:** `AppText`, RN `Text`, `Icon`, `Ionicons`, `ActivityIndicator`, `StatusGlyph`, `TextInput`, and the
  fill-less `Button` roles (`tertiary` draws `accentText`; `iconOnly` draws `textPrimary`). A `TextInput` is always
  self-filled, so it is never bare.
- **Backing classes:** the 38.4 RG029 §0 classes.
  - **glass:** `GlassSurface` (any treatment), `ChromeScrim`, `ShellAppBar` (the `SCOPE_ELEMENTS` set).
  - **opaque:** a `backgroundColor` from `surface`, `surfaceElevated` or `background`, or a `Sheet`, `Modal` or
    `ConfirmDialog`.
  - Also counted as backed:
    - a **solid fill** from any other palette key, such as an accent or danger pill. This is a 38.5-01 addition; text
      on a solid fill is not on the art;
    - **wrapper components** whose rendered prop lands inside a backing (found by fixpoint);
    - **render-helper arguments** rendered inside a backing (analyzer fix `27222b1`, §5).
  - A chain that ends at the `Orrery` route is treated as no-art: that route forces the `none` background.
- **Walk:** a foreground with no backing inside its own component is followed across component boundaries to every
  render site: a JSX tag, a component passed as a prop, or a route registration. It goes up to a route in
  `src/navigation`. The classification follows from where the chains end:
  - **bare:** every chain reaches a route unbacked;
  - **mixed:** some chains are backed and some are not;
  - **backed:** every chain is backed;
  - **unmounted:** no chain reaches a route.
- **Tokens:** read from the colour expression, or from the role default, written `X(default)`. Floors are 4.5 for
  text and links and 3.0 for glyphs and large text. `4.5*` marks the 20sp semibold headings, which are kept at 4.5
  (brief §E).
- **Layout columns** (x band, alignment, vertical position) are not derived. They are written `ESTIMATED(n/a)`.

**Hand verification.** Each verified row was read in the file itself: the site, every ancestor up to its component
root, every render site on its chain, and the route registration. The result is recorded in the CSV:

- `verification` is set to `VERIFIED(hand-read 38.5-01)`;
- `parent_chain_note` starts with `HAND 38.5-01 [set]: <chain read> ; string: … ; analyzer error: …`, then carries the
  AST chain;
- `bare_kind` is changed to `backed(hand-corrected)` where the analyzer was wrong.

### Error-rate check (M-1): plausible denominator range per unit (COMPUTED)

- The false-bare upper bound of 8.86% is applied to the 158 ESTIMATED rows, all in the bare unit (every mixed site is
  VERIFIED). At most ⌈0.0886 × 158⌉ = **14** of them could be backed.
- False backed can only **add** bare sites. At most ⌈0.1135 × 1008⌉ = 115 backed non-`TextInput` sites could be bare.
  That raises every denominator and lowers every share, so it cannot push a unit toward strict. Any danger site among
  them is excluded by set (d).

| Unit | Denominator range | Flip denominator | Crosses? | Worst-case share (numerator ÷ low end) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bare-only | 288 – 302 (up to 417 with false backed) | 260 | no | 9.03% |

```python
rng = random.Random(385001)                      # seed
pool.sort(key=lambda r: (route, file_line))
```
