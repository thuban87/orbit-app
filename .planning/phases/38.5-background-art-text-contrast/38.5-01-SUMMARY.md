## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The analyzer walked render-helper arguments as if they rendered in place**

- **Found during:** Task 2, sample row `AIConnectionScreen.tsx:418`.
- **Issue:** JSX passed as `card(lane, <Body/>)` is rendered inside `card()`'s `GlassSurface`. The analyzer walked the
  call site's parents instead and called it bare. The brief's walker had the same bug.
- **Fix:** `helperBacking()` now follows the argument into a same-file helper's rendered parameter. JSX handed to a
  call inside an object literal (a store or portal request) is flagged `hosted:…(UNTRACED HOST)` for hand review. Two
  fixtures were added.
- **Effect over the whole corpus:** exactly 3 rows changed (the AIConnection `card()` bodies). Only the two
  `FilterPanelContent` rows are flagged hosted, and both are hand-corrected.
- **Files modified:** `src/theme/__contract__/bare-text-sites.ts`, `src/theme/bare-text-sites.test.ts`.
- **Committed in:** `27222b1`.

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Route registry wrapped in `as const satisfies`** (Task 1). The first Digest tracer run attributed
the rows to the tab route `DigestTab`. The registry check now unwraps `as` and `satisfies`. Fixed before the Task 1
commit.

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] ShellAppBar internals and `body = (<JSX/>)` assignments** (Task 1).

- ShellAppBar paints its scrim as an absolute-fill **sibling** of its text, so its own internals are now backed by
  construction.
- A JSX value assigned to a local (`body = …`) is now followed to its reads.

Both were fixed before the Task 1 commit. The second is how `DigestDayDetail` resolved to its `surfaceElevated`
backing.
