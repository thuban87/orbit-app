**Source:** `GlassSurface` `treatment` default `"card"` (:85) with `"orrery-overlay"` opt-in. Add tests that default props keep today's opacity.
**Apply to:** ShellAppBar (25 consumers), ChromeScrim (7 screens), GlassSurface (25 files).
