## Phase Goal

> **Note (planner):** ROADMAP Phase 6's `**Goal:**` line is descriptive, not a user story. The
> story below is derived from that goal + the 4 success criteria for MVP framing; surfaced to the
> owner in the planning return. It is not invented scope.

**As a** person maintaining relationships, **I want to** log a touchpoint in one tap and later
refine, edit, or delete it on a full profile timeline while seeing that contact's status, gravity,
intensity, and rogue state, **so that** the app honestly reflects each relationship and shows me
who has drifted out of orbit.

This plan is the first vertical slice: **one tap logs a real touchpoint through the single writer.**

<objective>
Deliver the one-tap "Log contact" loop end-to-end: extend the Phase-2 single-writer recency DAO
with a shared future-date guard on the record path, then wire a one-tap affordance on the contact
profile that records a touchpoint with the Cluster-G-revised defaults (`direction='outbound'`
explicit, `channel='unspecified'`, `connected=1`, `quality=null`, `source='manual'`).

Purpose: "Mark contacted" is the app's most-fired action and the whole product exists to make it
cheap. This slice makes it real while closing LOG-06's landmines (future-date trap, same-day
collapse, UTC off-by-one) at the DAO chokepoint.

Output: `src/db/log-guards.ts` (pure guard) + guard test; `recordTouchpoint` extended with the
pre-transaction reject; extended `recency-dao.test.ts`; a one-tap button on `ContactProfileScreen`.

CRITICAL — do NOT fork a second `last_contact` writer. `recordTouchpoint` already routes through
`recomputeLastContact` behind the shared mutex + hand-rolled transaction. Extend it in place.
</objective>
