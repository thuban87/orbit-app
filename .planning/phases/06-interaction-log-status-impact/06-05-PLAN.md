<objective>
Render gravity — accumulated familiarity, the grace buffer — on the contact profile as named tiers
with a bar. Gravity is brand-new derived-never-stored math: an age-decayed weighted sum of the
contact's interactions toward a floor (recent weighs more; ancient history fades but never to zero),
mapped to coarse named tiers (never a raw number — a raw score is gameable like a streak).

Purpose: LOG-03's gravity half. Gravity is the concept that motivated turning the plugin into an
app; it lives on the profile only (Cluster D/G: nothing log-derived on the dashboard card).

Output: `impact.ts` tunables + orchestration, `gravity-logic.ts` (pure) + test, `impact-read.ts`
(the DB read feeding gravity + intensity) + test, `GravityBar.tsx`, and the profile render.

CRITICAL — gravity is DERIVED-NEVER-STORED: no column, no write statement anywhere in this plan.
Tunables (half-life, floor weight, tier boundaries/names) sit at the TOP of impact.ts as single-
number-edit constants (CLAUDE.md).
</objective>
