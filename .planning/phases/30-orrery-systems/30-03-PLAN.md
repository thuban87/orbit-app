<objective>
Complete the Systems DAO: the single sanctioned writer of the Systems tables, covering create/rename/delete/duplicate/reorder/hide-show, membership overrides (include/exclude/reset), rule writes for custom Systems, case-insensitive name uniqueness, and built-in/Category immutability — each mutation guarded by changes===1 and composed through the shared write transaction.

Output: A full systems-dao with core+mutex pairs for every mutation, deterministic duplicate naming, override reset, and immutability guards, fully unit-tested.
