<objective>
Extend the Orrery switcher to surface the full Systems catalog: custom Systems in management order with All Contacts pinned and hidden Systems omitted, live member counts, and distinct empty-valid vs broken indicators (icon shape + text), plus a Manage Systems entry into the flat management screen.

Purpose: The switcher is the primary System-switching control (§U, extends Phase 29 E2). It must show every selectable System with honest state, without becoming a management surface. This plan adds the three central semantic icon keys and the ordered/visible/counted descriptor build.

Output: The three icon-registry keys, an extended buildSystemChoices (order/visibility/counts/severity), and the switcher rows rendering them.

## Artifacts this plan produces
- `src/components/icons/icon-registry.ts`: `system-empty`, `system-broken`, `system-overrides`
- `src/components/orrery/orrery-controls-logic.ts`: ordered/visible/counted/severity `buildSystemChoices` + a `SystemChoice` descriptor with count + severity + overrides flag
- `src/components/orrery/OrrerySystemSelector.tsx`: custom rows, counts, severity indicators, Manage Systems entry, and a new `catalog` prop
- `src/db/systems-catalog-read.ts`: `readSystemsCatalog(exec)` (customs + prefs + override-presence flags) and `countSystemMembers(exec, ref)` (bounded count) — the reads the OrreryScreen owner calls
- `src/screens/OrreryScreen.tsx`: loads the catalog + bounded counts and threads them to `OrrerySystemSelector` (data-flow owner)
