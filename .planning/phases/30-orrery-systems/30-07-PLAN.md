<objective>
Build the Manage Members surface: a searchable, virtualized, multi-select avatar grid over the contact-picker selection LOGIC, where rule matches enter preselected, deselection expresses an in-place Exclusion, Add People adds eligible active contacts as manual inclusions, and counts report total/added/excluded.

Purpose: This is the direct-manipulation half of membership authoring. The dossier is explicit: reuse the existing selection foundation (the logic modules), not the single-select ContactPicker modal, and never invent a second selection system. Building the grid as a self-contained component now lets plan 08 embed it as a builder page.

Output: ManageMembersGrid + its pure selection/override logic + an active-contact read source, unit-tested for the exclude-in-place, added, counts, and eligibility semantics.
