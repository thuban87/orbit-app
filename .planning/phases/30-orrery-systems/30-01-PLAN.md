<objective>
Establish the Systems persistence foundation and prove the whole Phase 30 architecture with one thin, production-quality vertical slice: a manual-only custom System created in the DAO, resolved through the UNCHANGED Phase 29 read pipeline, and selectable in the switcher.

Purpose: Migration 022 is a one-way door (irreversible on unreachable devices). Proving the custom-System identity, persistence, resolution, and switcher wiring end-to-end on one manual-only path catches any architectural dead-end after one commit instead of after the whole authoring UI is built. All later plans expand outward from this proven slice.

Output: Migration 022 + registration + schema-invariant tests; a minimal systems-dao (create + override write + the reads the resolver needs); the OrrerySystemRef `custom` kind + widened `orrery_last_system` grammar; a minimal manual-only resolver; custom-kind routing in the read layer; and custom Systems surfaced by buildSystemChoices.
