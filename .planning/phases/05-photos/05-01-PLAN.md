<objective>
Enable the phase's native platform surface and land the deterministic avatar colour tokens.

Phase Goal (user story): **As a** user, **I want to** set a contact's (or my own) photo from my library or a pasted URL and frame it with an in-app crop — and see a distinctive themed initials avatar when there is no photo — **so that** my people are recognisable at a glance and their photos stay private on my device.

This plan is the mobile analog of "framework init": it installs the seven first-party native modules the whole phase needs (one coordinated rebuild instead of many), hardens the picker config so no camera/mic permission ever enters the manifest, wires the Reanimated babel plugin and the gesture-handler root, and adds the `avatarSwatches`/`avatarSwatchText` theme tokens PHOTO-04 depends on. No feature logic ships here — it is required shared enablement for every later slice.

Purpose: every subsequent plan (Avatar render, crop screen, pipeline, URL path, custom-field photo) depends on these modules and tokens being present.
Output: updated `package.json`, `app.config.ts`, `babel.config.js`, `App.tsx`, and the two theme files + their test.
</objective>
