## Phase Goal

**As a** person tending my relationships, **I want to** add a new contact through a short form, **so that** I can start tracking someone in under a minute.

**This slice (Plan 04):** the CreateContactScreen wired to `createContactFull`, plus the profile scaffold it lands on. After this plan a user can create a contact end-to-end on device and see its profile — the first full vertical slice of the phase.

Purpose: CRUD-01/02 as a user-reachable capability. This is the MVP thinnest-viable-slice that makes Plan 02's tested DAO real for the user.
Output: CreateContactScreen, ContactProfileScreen scaffold, both registered on the navigator (replacing Plan 01 placeholders).
</objective>
