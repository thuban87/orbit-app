## Phase Goal

**As a** person tending my relationships, **I want to** add, edit, archive, and permanently delete the people I track — with their links, custom fields, and when we last spoke — **so that** my orbit only holds people I still care about and their details stay right.

> ROADMAP's `**Goal:**` line for Phase 4 is prose, not a user-story; this story is derived faithfully from that goal + the four success criteria (autonomous run — no owner prompt available). It invents no scope.

**This slice (Plan 01):** stand up the real navigation shell the whole phase's screens hang off, and place the two CRUD-05 "separate homes" (Settings → Custom Fields, Settings → Archived contacts). After this plan a user navigates Home ⇄ Settings ⇄ Custom Fields with a native header + back gesture, replacing the Phase-3 hand-rolled toggle.

<objective>
Introduce `@react-navigation/native` + native-stack (owner-locked in CONTEXT "Navigation shell"), migrate the existing `HomeScreen` `useState<Route>` toggle into the navigator, and relocate the Custom-Fields reachability route into a new Settings stack alongside an Archived-contacts entry. This fulfils the Phase-3 forward note ("Phase 4 introduces the real navigation shell") and reverses nothing — the dependency-free routing was an explicitly temporary Phase-1→3 state.

Purpose: every screen built in Plans 03–09 (Create, Profile, Edit, Archived) is a navigator route; without the shell there is nowhere for them to live. CRUD-05 also requires archived contacts to live in a "distinct, low-traffic" Settings home separate from never-contacted.
Output: a native-stack navigator, a Settings screen, and a migrated Home shell — all colours through theme tokens.
</objective>
