## Phase Goal

**As a** person tending my relationships, **I want to** permanently delete a contact I am sure about, seeing exactly what will be destroyed first, **so that** I can clear out people for good without fear of a silent mistake.

**This slice (Plan 09):** the irreversible purge — the second stage of the lifecycle. After this plan the Archived list offers "Delete permanently", shows an impact summary, and fans out the deletes in one transaction. This completes CRUD-06 and the phase.

<objective>
Add the purge fan-out DAO (impact counts + an archived-guarded one-transaction delete of every owned child + the contact, including `field_history`) and the "Delete permanently" action + impact-summary confirm on the Archived list. Photo-file and scheduled-notification cleanup fire POST-COMMIT via an idempotent adapter that Phases 5 and 11 register.

Purpose: CRUD-06. Purge is the only unrecoverable action and there is no server/backup — the impact-summary confirm is the safeguard, and explicit deletes keep the blast radius auditable (not reliant on FK cascade).
Output: `purge-dao.ts`, Archived-list purge UI. (The `danger` token this UI uses is added in Plan 01/wave 1 — this plan consumes `colors.danger` and adds no theme file.)

**Review updates (reviews-mode) — three findings land here:**
- **Archived-guard at the write boundary.** `purgeContact` verifies `archived_at IS NOT NULL` INSIDE its transaction and asserts exactly one contacts row deleted, so a live contact cannot be purged even if a caller reaches the DAO directly — the two-stage safety is structural, not just UI routing.
- **Extension hook moves POST-COMMIT.** The photo-`unlink`/notification-cancel hook is a non-transactional OS side effect; awaiting it inside the open `BEGIN` holds the shared mutex and cannot be rolled back. It now runs AFTER COMMIT as idempotent best-effort cleanup via a registered adapter (Phases 5/11 wire it).
- **Impact counts corrected.** `contact_custom_values` is ONE row per contact (0/1), not a field count, so it is NOT rendered as a blast-radius count line; the rendered lines are the genuine multi-row children only, via a pure, unit-tested omit-zero helper.
</objective>
