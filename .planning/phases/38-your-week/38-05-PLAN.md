<objective>
Build the **Your Week** presentation module as self-contained components so the DigestScreen assembly (Plan 06) can mount it. Your Week is a retrospective summary — People reached / Interactions / Events metrics + a Profile-style activity heatmap scoped to the selected period, with inline day detail — reusing Phase 32 aggregation/components rather than reimplementing them (dossier §H/§I/§J, D-05).

Plan against the dossier (D-01). Reuse the heatmap HELPERS (buckets/heatmapLevel/classifyHeatmapCell + colors.heatmapScale), NOT the whole `ActivityHeatmap` component — it ships a lens switcher + prev/next navigation that §J forbids (RESEARCH Open Q1/A3). Digest is derive-only: no new relationship-domain schema, no persisted Digest cache (D-02, §O). Do not add the Profile calendar rotary or long-range history navigation (§J).

**Owner ruling D-09 (supersedes the earlier in-context-only read):** the Your Week period preference is surfaced in **BOTH** places — a **Settings row** in the Phase 37 Settings (its canonical home per dossier §I) **AND** the in-context Digest toggle. The two stay in sync because there is a **single source of truth**: the `yourWeekPeriod` `app_settings` key (Plan 02). The in-context toggle (Task 3) and the Settings row (Task 4, new) both read `getAppSettings().yourWeekPeriod` and write `updateAppSettings({ yourWeekPeriod })` — no second copy. Sync is **focus-time re-read, not live cross-screen propagation**: each surface reloads the shared key on focus (SettingsInteractionsScreen already reloads via `useFocusEffect`→`reload()`, :59-70) and after its own write, so a change on one surface shows on the other when it NEXT gains focus — there is no shared reactive settings store and no promise of instantaneous update while a surface is mounted off-focus (cycle-3 MEDIUM clarification). This honors §I and does NOT reverse D-03: D-03 governs plan *decomposition* (no one-plan-per-module), not preference *placement*; adding a single row to an existing Settings category is not "broad Settings restructuring." RESEARCH Open Q4's "in-context only" resolution is superseded by D-09.

Purpose: a ready, tested Your Week module Plan 06 slots into the Digest surface.
Output: `YourWeekSection.tsx`, `YourWeekHeatmap.tsx`, `DigestDayDetail.tsx`.
</objective>
