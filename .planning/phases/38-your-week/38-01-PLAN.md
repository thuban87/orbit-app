Purpose: establish Orbit's durable home/navigation model. Every later Phase-38 plan builds on the tab shell this plan proves.
Output: five-tab RootNavigator (initialRoute Digest); new DigestStack + EventsStack; DashboardStack cleaned of the promoted screens; TabParamList/stack param lists; TAB_ICON; a canonical product-label constant.
