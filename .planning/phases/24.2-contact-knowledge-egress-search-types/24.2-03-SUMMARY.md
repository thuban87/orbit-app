## Decisions Made

- `MEMORY_TYPE_REGISTRY[type].aiDefault` is applied only while inserting a new Memory; existing `allow_ai` rows are never recalculated.
- The present durable availability seam is `aiProvider !== "none"`, loaded from `getAppSettings`; it defaults and resets to false on read failure so Phase 36 can replace the source without changing editor wiring.
- `RANKED_FUEL_EXCLUSIONS` was left unchanged after confirming ADR-081 supersedes ADR-030 only; no ADR supersedes the live Off Limits constraints in ADR-036/050/078.
