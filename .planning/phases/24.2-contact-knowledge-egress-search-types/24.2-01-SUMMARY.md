## Decisions Made

- `source='ai'` fuel is preserved, not blind-deleted: it becomes an AI-off, general, user-provenance Memory. The rare off-limits safety-net case remains never-egress-eligible through `allow_ai=0`.
- Removal of the inert `confirmFuel` UI/code is deliberately deferred to Phase 36; this plan did not alter `fuel-dao.ts`, `ContactProfileScreen.tsx`, or `FuelEditor.tsx`.
- `sharedMemories` is optional in `PromptContext` until Phase 36 owns final prompt-string serialization, while the egress reader always returns an array.
