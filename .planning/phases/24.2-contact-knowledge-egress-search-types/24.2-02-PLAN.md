  <behavior>
    - Term "gift" (4 chars) vs "gift" → match (distance 0).
    - Term "gft" vs "gift" → match (distance 1).
    - Term "gfit" vs "gift" (2 edits, 4-char term) → NO match (≤1 cap for terms < 6 chars).
    - Term "birthday" (8 chars) vs "birthdya" (distance 2) → match (≤2 cap for terms ≥ 6 chars).
    - Term "birthday" vs "birtxxxx" (distance 4) → NO match.
    - Prefix: term "bir" is a prefix of "birthday" → match regardless of distance.
    - Substring: term "day" is a substring of "birthday" → match.
    - Multi-term query "gft bir": every term must match SOME token in the candidate's corpus text (AND across terms, OR across tokens).
    - Empty/whitespace term → no candidates (returns empty).
    - Tokenization is defined and tested: lowercase, split on whitespace AND common punctuation (so `"O'Connor"` yields tokens `o`/`connor`, an email `a@b.co` yields `a`/`b`/`co`, a URL splits on `/`,`.`,`:`); a diacritic case (e.g. `café`) is covered by a documented rule (fold or keep — pick ONE and test it).
