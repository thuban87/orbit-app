<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Replace the Orrery's rendering, camera model and visual language with a Skia 2.5D V2:
- a stable imperative renderer;
- a tilted strategy-map Home;
- a painted sky;
- thin sun-lit orbits with subtle pulses;
- rim-lit status bodies and a star sun;
- moon bumps;
- reticle focus with a docked card;
- a star-warp switch.

Fix five production Orrery defects first.

Unchanged:
- the Orrery's data, SQL and semantics (dense rank → ring, timestamp → angle, query-time status, Systems);
- the accessible companion sheet path.

Out of scope:
- iOS and web builds;
- the Skia 3 migration;
- the RNGH 3 / Expo SDK 58 upgrade.
</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

These mirror the dossier, which is authoritative and holds the detail, grounding and file:line refs.

Owner field-sheet decisions (2026-10-02):

- **D-01:** Renderer is Skia 2.5D. Lab C's three.js/WebGPU is rejected; its camera and layout ideas are harvested.
- **D-02:** Look: cinematic during transitions; a calm star chart with a 3D strategy-map feel on normal views.
- **D-03:** Tilted Home, ~62°, as one easily changed setting. Flat must remain supported. ⚠ Supersedes the phase-08 top-down Home.
- **D-04:** One-finger drag pans. No orbit gesture.
- **D-05:** A small, perceptible pan coast.
- **D-06:** Near-side size cap (Lab B CLAMP).
- **D-07:** Painted sky: more stars than Lab A, a much quieter nebula, Lab B's Standard-Dark graphite as the calm default, Lab B's light sky. ⚠ Supersedes ADR-179's "solid background" for the in-canvas sky.
- **D-08:** Per-System sky tint.
- **D-09:** Optional Lab C-style orbital-plane grid, tunable to off, with an owner checkpoint.
- **D-10:** Lab B's orbit light pulses, kept but not very obvious. ⚠ Supersedes ADR-077/048's "sole ambient animation".
- **D-11:** Lab B's thin, sun-lit orbit lines. Status colours and dash/fade cues stay.
- **D-12:** Contacts stay ON their orbit line; ring colour and angle carry status. ⚠ Supersedes HANDOFF §7 drift and the ADR-046 drift clause. Removes the prod drift push.
- **D-13:** Sun-lit faces with lighter shading only.
- **D-14:** Status as Lab B's rim + atmosphere glow.
- **D-15:** Lab A's star sun.
- **D-16:** Minimum body size at overview.
- **D-17 [informational]:** Lab B's and Lab C's moons are rejected. The moon behaviour is D-31.
- **D-18:** Reticle on the focused person.
- **D-19:** Lab C's focus framing: centred, large, card docked below. Tapping a cluster at far zoom zooms into it; the cluster panel stays.
- **D-20:** Don't hide the map behind the card. Keep today's glass.
- **D-21:** Dim everyone else while focused. The sun is exempt.
- **D-22:** Lab B's corner-bracket chrome on the Orrery HUD.
- **D-23:** Reorder: Lab B's charge arc + projected orbit outline. Hold time and haptic unchanged.
- **D-24:** The switch blends Lab B's star warp, Lab A's orbit streaks and a camera swirl. It must be smooth on the 3a.
- **D-25 [informational]:** No bloom, tilt-shift, lens flare or heat-haze.
- **D-26:** Light themes start from Lab B's light treatment. Galaxy Light gets its own flavour.
- **D-27:** Standard and Galaxy look distinct. The Galaxy nebula is more subdued.
- **D-28:** Hard gate: card secondary text ≥ 4.5:1 in all four themes over the new sky.
- **D-29 [informational]:** Heat and battery are answered by D-40.
- **D-30 [informational]:** Kill list (dossier): no gravity-well grid, Lab C gestures, Lab B/C moons, Lab C reorder, loading box, dark faces or dark overview.

Owner chat rulings (2026-10-02):

- **D-31:** A far-zoom moon bump that splits into moons on zoom-in (new). Lab A's stack pips are also kept and must look distinct. ⚠ Supersedes phase-08's "far overview → hidden".
- **D-32:** The star warp replaces "not light-speed". Prod spin/shed/capture, the 2100/180 ms durations, land-at-Home and ready-at-0.82 stay. ⚠ Supersedes phase-08 `:568`.
- **D-33:** Real initials with emoji-safe handling (no `Intl.Segmenter` on Hermes). Amends the Phase 29 full-name fallback.
- **D-34:** Stay on `@shopify/react-native-skia` 2.x. Skia 3 / Graphite is a later phase.
- **D-35:** Keep today's translucent cards (ADR-149). This clarifies 38.5 D-12's "full scrim" for the Orrery.
- **D-36:** The sky barely drifts, with slight parallax. Still under reduced motion.
- **D-37:** Information-layer idle motion is only twinkle, the sun pulse and the subtle orbit pulses. The sky drift (D-36) is separate environmental motion on the same ambient clock.
- **D-38 [informational]:** No progress wake. The tether is moot under D-12.
- **D-39:** The map runs up under the Orrery title.
- **D-40:** Idle: only low-rate ambient (twinkle, sun pulse, orbit pulses, sky drift) on one bounded clock. No warmer than today's Orbit on either phone.
- **D-41 [informational]:** The five lab-found defects are fixed in this phase (see D-52).
- **D-42 [informational]:** Portability is an architectural criterion (see D-46).

Derived engineering decisions (dossier §Derived Decisions):

- **D-43:** Lab A's imperative stable-tree renderer pattern, written fresh, as a new consumer of the existing `frame`. Drawn == tappable.
- **D-44:** Never stroke under perspective. Default: Lab A's world plane under the exact homography. Fallback: Lab B's ring field, chosen by an early spike on both phones against D-56.
- **D-45:** A Home profile `{tilt, lensShift, fitRule}`, with max tilt split from Home tilt (62° > today's `MAX_TILT`) and a dolly focal model. Flat stays valid.
- **D-46:** An `OrreryRenderHost` capability interface for Android-only and GPU-specific choices. One `OrreryTextService`. Pure gesture semantics.
- **D-47:** Label tiers and the tap-to-Profile threshold follow projected size. The focus zoom may exceed `IDENTITY_ZOOM`.
- **D-48:** One size mechanism in the projection (min size + near clamp). Lab B's visual-only FLOOR is rejected.
- **D-49:** One quantised ~30 Hz ambient clock. Base baked when static, with the old snapshot disposed. No 120 Hz idle. If D-10 can't meet D-40, tell the owner.
- **D-50:** Hue derived from `systemRefId`, with more than 6 buckets. No schema change.
- **D-51:** Authored per-theme Orrery tokens, including SkSL uniforms. Blend rule by theme mode.
- **D-52:** Defect fixes first: worklet captures (update `orrery-frame-mapper.test.ts:104,115`), O(1) key index + projection hoist, `OrreryTextService`, initials, the custom System name after a cold restore, the stale switch camera. Absorbs todo `2026-09-29-orrery-anr-choose-system`.
- **D-53:** The switch on a budget: a sprite-budget warp, O(N) setup, no renderer re-render, photos kept, shortest-arc swirl composed with the breath.
- **D-54:** Moon bumps and stack pips are distinct. ADR-105 limits apply. Indexed stack detection.
- **D-55:** Meet the contrast gate by dimming what is drawn under the card, re-proven with the ADR-169 both-extrema method in all four themes.

Codex-review rulings (2026-10-02):

- **D-56:** Day 1 is a release baseline of today's main on both phones (SurfaceFlinger presents). The owner then locks the numeric gates before visual work proceeds.
  - Starting targets: 6 Pro ≥ 55 / 3a ≥ 45 median presents/s at Size 120; tab return ≤ 1 s.
  - Hard gates from the start: no ANR at Size 120 or All Contacts; no switch stall over 100 ms (6 Pro) or over 150 ms (3a); idle no worse than main; the 3a usable and the 6 Pro smooth (owner checkpoints).
- **D-57:** The System Preview canvas gets the V2 look, flat (phase-09 `:311` kept), with no cinematic effects.
- **D-58:** Tab return: the active render lifecycle still stops on blur. A bounded reusable cache (photo derivatives, text, baked sky) survives the blur, with caps, eviction and release on background. It stays within 38.6 D-11's flat-memory gate. A new ADR clarifies ADR-170.
