## Honest read vs Lab B (policy §6)

- **Brackets: as in Lab B.** Arm, stroke, inset, opacity and plate radius (`RADII.sm`) are Lab B's values. On screen the brackets read at the same weight and colour as the 08/03 reference, on the triggers and on the controls. They are plainly visible.
- **Title over the map: as in Lab B (Galaxy Dark).** The bar is near-opaque at the top and fades into the map over the tail, with no hairline, as in the r3 presets reference. Standard Light has no Lab B reference. There, the bar reads as a near-white band, and its bottom edge shows as a soft step (luma 248 → 241, then fading to the map's 228). Not tuned; see taste calls.
- **Charge arc: as in Lab B.** It has a track ring and an additive accent arc, 2 dp each, at `bracketRadius`. Like Lab B's, it reads as a pale accent ring. Relative to the body it sits slightly wider than in Lab B, because V2 bodies are drawn smaller at this zoom.
- **Armed glow orbit and locked reticle: as in Lab B.** It is the same blue glow band under a bright line, with the reticle brackets on the held body. Because of the 62° tilt it shows as an ellipse where Lab B's was a circle.
- **Slot flash: visible.** A brief wider, brighter band appears on the target ring at each ring change. It is easiest to see in the half-speed clip.
- **Card and cluster:** both bugs are fixed (above).

No look missed its Lab B reference, so there is **no tuning commit**.

## Deviations from Plan

### Code agent (as reported; commits above)

1. **Brackets on more panels than Lab B.** They are on every surface in the plan's D-22 list: the System selector trigger and its open panel, the View options trigger and panel, each camera control, the cluster panel and the focus card. Lab B bracketed fewer of these.
2. **Tunables are kept at the top of their own files** (`OrreryHudCorners.tsx`, `orrery-header-scrim.ts`, `draw-overlay.ts`), per the CLAUDE.md convention, rather than in one shared tunables module.
3. **The armed glow uses `orreryInstrument`, not `orreryGlow`**, as Lab B did. The plan text named `orreryGlow` for the soft glow term. The Lab-anchoring correction ("port the lab look as drawn") takes precedence.
4. **Reduced motion:** the charge stays at 0 through the hold and jumps to 1 at activation. There is no animated fill, which matches the plan.
5. **New `orrery-header-scrim.ts`** (+ test) holds Lab B's stepped scrim bands (SCRIM_TOP 0.96 → SCRIM_BAR 0.84 → 0 over SCRIM_TAIL 44 dp).
6. **The ring-field half of H6 was skipped.** `src/components/orrery/v2/ring-field.ts` does not exist (Plan 08 chose world-plane), so the armed outline runs on the world-plane ring shader only.

## Surface to owner now

1. **First open: the System label flickers, and the sky is empty until the scene lands.** On a cold session (DEBUG), the Orrery tab shows the chrome over an empty dark sky for about 2 s before the map draws. The trigger label reads "All Contacts", then "Unavailable System" for about 1 s, then "Size 20". The loading box used to cover the canvas during this time; now the empty state is visible. The "Unavailable System" label also shows in a Lab B capture (08/03), so it predates V2. Evidence: `open-from-digest-sheet.png` and `raw/open-orrery-from-digest.mp4`. **Recommendation:** have Plan 14 or 15 keep the previous System's name (or show no name) until Systems hydrate. Plan 16's release build will show how long the empty sky really lasts.
2. **View options pushes the camera controls down under the "+" button.** This is pre-existing and also visible in Plan 11's `raw-03`. With the View options panel open, the controls column moves down and only the first button is fully visible. Disposition: as observed. It was listed for the orchestrator because the new brackets make the clipped plate easier to notice.
3. **Reanimated "Reading from `value` during component render" warnings** appear in the Metro pane on every Orrery session (about 300 per session here). An earlier session in the same pane, with unknown code, showed them as well, so I cannot attribute them to Plan 12. They are not crashes. Disposition: Plan 16 should check for them on the release build, where they do not log, and drop the item if they are dev-only.
