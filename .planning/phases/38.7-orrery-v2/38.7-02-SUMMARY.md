## Deferred / surfaced items: D-52 defect list for Plans 03-04 (plain language, with recommendations)

These are baseline facts about today's Orrery, recorded and not investigated (owner guardrail). Recommendation: fold all of them into Plans 03-04, with **G4** as the acceptance check.

1. **All Contacts / Orrery Stress crash.** Picking a large System crashes the release build on both phones (SIGSEGV null-pointer on the JS thread `mqt_v_js`; one trace through react-native-skia `JsiHostObject::get`). 6 Pro 5 crashes, 3a 2. **Hard fix (G4).**
2. **Size 120 load blocks the UI for ~30-60 s on the 6 Pro** (~23 s tab return on the 3a), and **any touch during the load raises an ANR**. **Fix:** load off the UI thread, or keep the UI responsive while loading (G4 "touches during a System load").
3. **Choose System freeze.** The 6 Pro raised `am_anr` with the sheet open. On the 3a the sheet takes 22-33 s to open (UI stall up to ~15-30 s). This is the first answer to todo 2026-09-29: **yes, it happens on a release build (6 Pro)**.
4. **The 3a freezes on touch at Size 120 even after the scene has drawn** (`am_anr` ×2, owner session 2).
5. **The UI thread stays busy at rest:** 6 Pro ~34 % of a core with nothing drawing; 3a ~98 % (pegged), skin ~39 °C. **Fix:** relevant to G6/D-40; V2's ambient clock replaces this.
6. **"Unavailable System" after a cold start.** The selector label stays wrong until the System is picked again (`src/screens/OrreryScreen.tsx:466` restores with no name; `src/stores/orrery-system-store.ts:104`). Cosmetic but misleading.
7. **Build host disk headroom.** droid had under 2 GB free with a build's leftovers. **Recommendation:** keep ≥ 30 GB free for Plans 08 and 16; arm64-only is now the rule.
8. **The owner's 8 old APKs** sit in `C:\Users\bwales\projects\orbit-app-old-apks\` on droid. **Recommendation:** the owner moves them back or deletes them as he likes.
