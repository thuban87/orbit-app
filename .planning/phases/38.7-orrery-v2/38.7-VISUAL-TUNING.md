**Plain statement (D-49):** the pulses cost about **+3 points of a core on the 6 Pro** (≈ +5 % of the Orrery's idle CPU; ~+1.4 UI thread, the rest garbage collection) and about **+7 points on the 3a** (≈ +12 %, mostly UI thread) — DEBUG. The 3a delta alone is above the locked G6 tolerance of +10 % CPU, so if it held on release the pulses would threaten D-40 there. They do not change presents/s (the ambient clock already presents ~30/s for the twinkle, drift and sun pulse) or the refresh rate. Reported, not dropped. Context (same DEBUG method, Plan 08 screening, before this plan's sky): surf fc bake Size 120 total 56.1 % → now 61 % with pulses off, so the painted sky itself adds ~5 points on the 6 Pro.

A cheaper pulse path is possible without changing the look (see Surfaced items in the 09 return): the per-tick scan of every ring builds a key string per body, which is where the extra GC comes from.

## Decision items (owner)

Recommendations are mine; nothing is defaulted.

- **G-GRID** — the D-09 orbital-plane grid. Options: **off** (`GRID_OPACITY` 0, nothing drawn) · **0.12** (faint polar chart under the rings) · **0.25** (clearly visible guides and spokes) · any other value. **Recommendation: 0.12.** It gives the plane the strategy-map read (D-02) without competing with the rings; it lives in the baked base, so it costs nothing at idle (one extra plane shader pass only while the view moves). Choose off if you still don't want a grid.
- **G-PULSE** — the D-10 orbit pulse intensity (and, optionally, budget). Options: **0.15** · **0.25** · **0.35** (`PULSE_INTENSITY`; budget 8 pulses at once). **Recommendation: 0.25, budget 8** — visible in motion, invisible in a still (the clips show it best). Cost: +3 points of CPU on the 6 Pro and +7 on the 3a (DEBUG; the 3a above the 10 % tolerance). If that worries you, say "budget 4" (about half the pulses on screen and roughly half the cost) or approve the cheaper-implementation follow-up in Surfaced items.
- **G-ALLSKY** — the All Contacts sky (D-08). Options: **untinted** (All Contacts keeps the neutral base sky, Lab B's rule; every other System has a hue) · **tinted** (All Contacts gets its own hue bucket like any other System). **Recommendation: untinted** — All Contacts is the "home" view of everyone, and the neutral sky makes every other System's tint read as "you are in a filtered System".
