# Phase 38.7 Plan 06: Render host, stable V2 picture canvas, world-plane rings and the overlay picture Summary

**The Orrery now draws through a fixed V2 picture tree (base, stars, legacy bodies/labels, overlay) that owns the one ambient clock. Orbit rings are drawn in world space under the camera's exact homography by an analytic SkSL line on cached annulus strips, never stroked under perspective. Polaris, the reorder outline and the focus outlines are overlay drawing. Platform and GPU choices sit behind one OrreryRenderHost.**

## Accomplishments

### Task 1 (tracer): render host, V2 canvas and world-plane rings
- **Render host (`v2/render-host.ts`).** `OrreryRenderHost` has `platform`, `opaqueCanvas`, `ambientDriver`, `bakePath`, `ringMethod`, `ringFieldMinRings`, `mali`, `devicePixelRatio()`.
  - Android keeps today's TextureView (`opaqueCanvas: false`), `frame-clock`, `bakePath: "none"`, `world-plane`. Plan 08's spike decides these.
  - iOS has the same values. Web bakes through `raster`. Any other platform is `"other"`.
- **Plane logic (`src/logic/orrery-plane-logic.ts`).**
  - `planeHomography` is built from `projectionBasis`, so it uses the same clamped lens shift, dolly focal and usable-rect centre.
  - Its third row is the normalised camera depth, so `w ≤ HORIZON_EPSILON` is exactly the existing near-plane cull.
  - Also `invertHomography`, `applyHomography`, `horizonScreenY` (the vanishing line; −∞ when flat), `discBeyondHorizon`, `minRadialScale`, `ringScreenPerimeter`, `ringScreenSpacing` and `ringDensityFade`. The three tunables are at the top of the file.
- **Shader (`v2/shaders.ts`).** `RING_PLANE_SKSL` takes uniforms only (inks as `half4`).
  - It computes a Jacobian-based screen-dp distance with a one-device-pixel feather.
  - It discards fragments at or beyond the near plane and dashes by world angle.
  - The `RuntimeEffect` cache compiles lazily, returns null on failure and logs once in development.
- **Specs (`v2/scene-specs.ts`).** `buildRingSpecs` reproduces today's look per status: colour, width, faded 0.7, faint trace 0.3, otherwise the style's opacity, and the 6-on-6-off dash (`dashPeriodDp` 12). It runs only on resource or theme change.
- **Draw (`v2/draw-plane.ts`).**
  - `drawPlaneRings` draws one `drawVertices` per eligible ring under H·S(R).
  - It skips a ring with no spec, an alpha below `RING_SKIP_ALPHA`, or a disc wholly beyond the near plane.
  - A 40-band strip ladder is built once per app (`ringBands()`).
- **Wiring.** OrreryWorld renders `OrreryV2Canvas` with the same `frame` and gesture. `ProjectedOrbitRing.tsx` is deleted.

## Behaviour changes for docs/systems/orrery.md (Plan 16)

1. **Rings** are drawn on the world plane under the camera homography with a constant screen width; they are no longer stroked polylines. At tilt 0 they look the same.
2. **Dashes** (wobble, rogue) are equal world-angle segments sized for a ~12 dp period on screen. At a tilt, near-side dashes are longer than far-side ones; before, every dash was the same screen length.
3. **Ring density fade (new).** When neighbouring orbits are closer than 20 dp on screen (on average), rings dim with the square of the spacing, down to 15 % of their alpha. Below 0.01 alpha a ring is not drawn.
4. **Layer order.**
   - Stars now draw above the rings.
   - Polaris, the reorder outline and the focus outlines now draw above all bodies; before, they were below the bodies, and the focus ring sat at its body's depth.
   - This is Plan 07's intended order.
5. **The ambient clock** lives in `OrreryV2Canvas`, with the same mount and unmount rule as before.

## Deviations from Plan

### Interpretations (inside the plan's latitude)

1. **"Not culled" for ring drawing** is read as "the ring's disc is not wholly beyond the near plane", not "the body is not culled".
   - Skipping every ring whose body sits beyond the horizon would make outer rings' visible near arcs pop in and out as people move around their orbit. That would be a visible regression from 38.7-05's open arcs, and the plan also requires look parity.
   - The tests prove both halves: a disc wholly beyond the horizon is skipped, and a culled body whose ring crosses the screen keeps its arc.
   - The overlay follows the plan's own wording: a culled body gets no screen circle, and its outline is skipped only when the disc is wholly beyond the horizon.
2. **`dashPeriodDp` instead of `dashCount` in the ring spec.**
   - Specs are built off the frame, and a fixed count would stretch dashes with zoom.
   - The count is derived per frame: round(visible screen perimeter / 12), clamped to 4–4096.
3. **Picture order: `base` (fill + rings), then `stars`.** The plan puts the fill in `base` and Plan 07 lists `base, stars`, so stars sit above the rings. The stars are tiny (≤ 1.8 dp, ≤ 0.55 alpha).
4. **Files outside `files_modified`:**
   - `v2/draw-sky.ts` and its test (a planned `draw-sky` module in PATTERNS).
   - `src/logic/orrery-frame.ts` (a comment) and `orrery-frame.test.ts` (ring assertions re-pinned to the homography).
   - `OrbitBody.tsx` and `SunBody.tsx` (`focusColor` removed, per Task 2).
   - `orrery-clock-context.ts`, `SystemPreviewCanvas.tsx` (comments), `services/orrery-scene.test.ts` (mock path).
   - The deleted `orrery-horizon-render.test.tsx`: its subjects (Polaris, ProjectedOrbitRing) are gone, and its horizon checks moved to `draw-overlay.test.ts` and `draw-plane.test.ts`.
5. **The frozen 38.7-03 `projectFrame` copy** in camera-logic tests keeps its behaviour. Only its ring-polygon field was renamed (`ringPolygon`), so the acceptance grep for `ringPath` is clean. The comparison strips it and newly checks the homography against it.
6. **H2 matrix test speed:** per-sample expects were aggregated to one per ring (2.4 s → 0.5 s), so it cannot time out under the parallel suite.
7. **Tracer gate.** Task 1's `<verify>` was re-run green (37 files / 442 tests, tsc, colors). Per `autonomous: true` and the owner's standing direction (as in Plans 03–05), execution continued without a human-verify stop.

## Deferred / surfaced items (plain language, with recommendations)

1. **The horizon handling for rings that cross the camera's near plane is proven in tests but not yet on a phone.** I expect it to work, but I can't confirm it on a GPU yet.
   - **What I expect:** the strip's triangles are clipped by the GPU in homogeneous space, and the shader drops every pixel at or beyond the near plane.
   - **Why it's unconfirmed:** that depends on Skia's `drawVertices` under a perspective matrix doing true homogeneous clipping on Mali and Adreno.
   - **When it shows up:** at the tilted Home with big Systems, or when zoomed in near the far side.
   - **Recommendation:** at the end-of-Plan-07 device check, look at All Contacts at 62° and Recenter. Outer rings should end cleanly at the bottom edge, with no streaks or missing arcs. If they don't, the fix is local to `draw-plane.ts`: draw straddling rings as a per-frame visible-arc strip.
2. **Ring cost at All Contacts is one `makeShader` + one `drawVertices` per ring per camera frame (about 1,000).** This is the D-44 default as planned. **Recommendation:** none here. Plan 08's spike measures it against the hybrid/ring-field fallback (`ringMethod`/`ringFieldMinRings` are already host fields).
3. **The density fade visibly dims rings when zoomed far out on large Systems.** At All Contacts and minimum zoom, rings drop to roughly 15–20 % of their normal alpha. **Recommendation:** check it at the Plan 14 device checkpoint. `RING_MIN_SPACING_PX` and `RING_DENSITY_FLOOR` are single numbers at the top of `orrery-plane-logic.ts`.
4. **Small look changes from layer order:**
   - Polaris and the focus and reorder outlines now draw above neighbouring bodies.
   - The stars now draw above the rings.
   - **Recommendation:** none; Plan 07 adopts this order. Mention it if it is noticed at the Plan 07 device check.
