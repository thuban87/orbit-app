- **Reticle: matches the lab look.** It has corner brackets, a thin lock ring, four ticks and a parked scan arc, in the accent colour. In absolute pixels the strokes are the same weight as Lab A and about the same as Lab B. Lab B's brackets sit a little further from the body and have a faint glow. 1. **`use-orrery-camera` exposes the camera motion epoch** (`d2755e15`). The C3-M1 card-height refit needs to know whether anything took the camera since the focus framing. The UI-side epoch check is that test.

5. Lab B's reticle under-glow was not ported (Lab A port only). The device pass confirms the reticle still matches the lab look.

## Surface to owner now

1. **The cluster zoom never fires after a System switch in the same session** (pre-existing; it breaks D-19's cluster zoom). Repro on the 6 Pro: switch Size 20 → Size 120, then tap the FF + GG cluster. The panel opens, but the camera only shifts the Home view above the panel; it does not zoom. A cold launch, or any session without a switch, zooms correctly (`cluster-sheet.png`, last tile vs second tile). Cause, from a temporary debug log: the OrreryScreen Home effect returns early on `forceSwitchHome` (`switchHomeGeneration.current === scene.generation`, `src/screens/OrreryScreen.tsx` ~1149-1158). That check stays true for the whole generation after a switch, so the cluster branch (`clusterZoomPose`) is never reached. It dates from 2026-09-08 (3834fd3c / 7bdf2a2d), so the old `frameBodies` cluster framing had the same hole. A likely fix is to consume the switch-home flag once the switch lands, or skip it while `clusterOpen`. It touches the C4-M3 switch-landing rule, so I did not fix it here. **Recommendation:** fold it into Plan 12 or a small gap fix, with a framing test that does switch → cluster tap.
2. **The occlusion dim under the focus card is invisible at today's glass.** Shipped `OCCLUSION_DIM` 0.7 vs 0 under the card: mean pixel difference 0.03-0.25 levels (stars and twinkle), against card ≈ 32 luma (`dim-card-sheet.png`, top row). The galaxy card glass is 0.91 opaque (`ORRERY_OVERLAY_TINT_OPACITY`), so the dim cannot "hide the map": the card already covers it. The handoff's worry does not apply, and there is no reason to lower it toward 0.45 for that. With the glass temporarily at 0.3 the dim darkens the card area by about 2-3 levels and stays exactly inside the card's rect, which confirms it draws and is aligned. The dim's real job is the Plan 14 contrast proof over bright content (sun, bright bodies) under the card. **Disposition:** as observed. Plan 14 owns the value.
3. **Moon focus does not re-frame.** Focusing a moon (Mara) puts the reticle on the moon and swaps the card, but the camera stays put. That fits D-73 ("moon focus only when fully split") and the moons are already on screen. Listed for the orchestrator only.
4. **Panning the focused person off-screen clears focus** (the existing `onFocusLost`). Observed and expected. Listed because it can surprise in a manual check.
5. **The focus card's satellite rows are clipped at the bottom.** LL's card lists Mara, then Theo below the visible card edge (the card scrolls). Pre-existing card layout; Plan 12 owns HUD and card chrome.
