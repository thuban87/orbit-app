## What was built

### Code agent (2ed21393, 38fa602f)

- **One switch-camera sampler** (`sampleOrrerySwitchCamera`, C3-H1). Drawing, the displayed-camera snapshot (interrupted publish, reduced-motion retarget) and the runtime helpers all use it, so a snapshot is always the camera drawn that frame. Every caller passes the transition being drawn (C4-L2).
- **Pure switch signal** (`sampleSwitchSignal`): warp, trail, breath and sky hue over the unchanged choreography. All are zero at the ends, with no spin, and under reduced motion. 2100 ms / 180 ms, land-at-Home and ready-at-0.82 are unchanged.
- **Lab B star warp as drawn:** the sky's own mid and near stars streak in their own tones (190 dp, push 0.14, swirl 0.32, alpha 0.95, thickness 0.85).
- **Sky tint crossfade** at draw time from progress 0.55 (Lab B `skyHueWeight`). No bake (H9).
- **Lab A orbit streaks** drawn on a new switch picture through a one-sided ring-shader arc (160 / 340 ms, max 2.4 rad, width 1.15, alpha 0.42 / 0.26), budget 120.
- **`OrreryWorld` is wrapped in `React.memo`** (`OrreryWorldInner` is kept for direct-call tests).
- **`onSettled` lands inside the prune call:** the runtime tells the screen it has settled inside the prune's JS call, so the satellite flush, the settled generation and the viewport adoption join one settle commit (C4-M1).
- **A held `worldViewport`:** the renderer viewport is held while a switch runs and adopted at settle. Panels and D-55 occlusion rects follow live measurements.
- **Guards:** a save resolving or failing mid-switch, and the save notice's measurement change, change no `OrreryWorld` prop. Satellite rows and errors reach the frame mid-switch only through the SharedValue publisher (C3-M2, C-M3).
- **Photos:** a key on both sides of a switch decodes once. An outgoing-only key retires only after every body layer acks. An unmount mid-switch tears down in reverse creation order, and a late decode is disposed (H3).
- **Not ported:** Lab A's sky kick and Lab B's far-star streaks.
- **Accepted:** a small hue jump on rapid re-selection.

### Lab C switch camera (8c1becdc, owner S-SWIRL pick)

The camera was copied from Lab C's code (`use-orrery-switch-runtime.ts` `interpolateCamera` and the `OrreryWorld3D.tsx` breath), not reconstructed from a description:

- **Base yaw path:** Lab C's raw linear mix (`SWITCH_YAW_PATH` "lab-c", new `switchBaseYaw`). Stored yaw lies in [0, 2π) and Home's is 0, so a camera turned just short of a full turn swings nearly the whole turn back. That is the "zigzag back to center". "shortest" (Plan 13's first build) stays as a one-word alternative.
- **Breath:** `applySwitchBreath` scaled by sin²(πp)·(0.5 + 0.5·intensity): zoom × (1 − 0.24·b), yaw + 0.38·b, tilt − 0.1·b (never below 0). New tunable `SWITCH_BREATH_YAW` 0.38.
- **Focal distance:** now the dolly focal of the pulled-back zoom (`dollyFocalDistance(zoom)` = 720 / zoom, Lab C's `labFocalDistance`). Before, the camera's own focal was divided by the zoom factor.
- **Kept from Plan 13, unlike Lab C:** the displayed-camera snapshot used for a retarget includes the breath, so an interrupted switch starts from what is drawn. The landing on Home is exact, and reduced motion is the plain crossfade with no breath.
- **Removed:** `SWITCH_SWIRL_TURNS`, `switchSwirlYaw`, `switchSwirlEnvelope` and the `swirl` field of the switch signal. The import of `shortestYaw` moved out of the runtime.
- **Tests:** the camera matches Lab C's formula, the swing peaks at 0.38 × scale, the landing is exact, and reduced motion has no breath.
- **Not built:** Lab C's bodies falling below the plane / rising from above during a switch (`SWITCH_FALL_EXTENTS` 0.42 / `SWITCH_RISE_EXTENTS` 0.55). The owner has not asked for it.
