# Phase 38.7 Plan 05: World and camera model for V2 (on-ring contacts, tilted dolly Home, horizon-safe projection, one size mechanism, tap-always-focuses) Summary

**Contacts now sit exactly on their orbit lines. Home is a 62° strategy-map profile with a dolly camera, and flat is one constant away. Every reachable projection culls beyond the horizon instead of throwing. One size mechanism makes drawn, tappable and labelled size agree. Label tiers follow on-screen magnification. A tap never opens Profile. One-finger pan keeps the touched ground under the finger, and a fling coasts visibly.**

## Accomplishments

### Task 1 (tracer): contacts on their orbit lines (D-12)
- **World.** `drift()` and every push are gone. A body is placed at `polarToXY(0, 0, ringRadius, angle)`. Extent = max(ringRadius + radius, sun radius).
- **Collision grid.** The collision test runs on a uniform grid; the cell is at least the largest touch distance, so only the 3×3 neighbouring cells are checked. It is exported as `collidingReferenceIndexes` and proven equal to the all-pairs scan on 1,000 members (dense cloud, spread cloud, real System). Neutrals are still never nudged, and the ≤ 1° / ≤ 4-unit bounds hold.
- **Geometry.** `DECAY_DRIFT_SPAN`, `ROGUE_DRIFT_SPAN`, `OrreryMetrics`, `OrreryBody`, `ringRadius`, `drawnRadius`, `driftPush`, `hitTest`, `deriveOrreryMetrics`, `clamp01`, `RING_GAP_SEED`, `MIN_GAP` and their tests are deleted; only tests consumed them (re-grepped). The module now exports exactly the on-ring primitives plus the radius constants.
- **Frame.** `ProjectedBody.worldRingRadius` is copied unchanged by `projectFrame`.
- **Reorder.** `ReorderDrag.drift` is removed. `captureReorder` reads `worldRingRadius`, `moveReorder` uses the pointer's world radius directly, and `previewReorder` puts a moved body on its new ring along its own angle.

### Task 2: Home profiles, dolly, lens shift, horizon safety (D-03/D-45, H2)
- **Tunables** at the top of `orrery-camera-logic.ts`:
  - `HOME_PROFILES` and `ACTIVE_HOME_PROFILE = "tilted"` (one assignment line);
  - `MAX_USER_TILT` 68°, `DOLLY_FOCAL_PX` 720, `HORIZON_EPSILON` 0.05;
  - the lens-shift clamp [0.3, 1];
  - the strategy-fit constants.
- **Camera model.**
  - `MAX_TILT` and `deriveFocalDistance` are gone.
  - `clampCameraPose` keeps 62°, clamps lens shift and sets the dolly focal.
  - The projection centre is `rect.y + rect.height · lensShift / 2` in the basis, the inverse, `tryUnproject`, `anchorCameraPose` and `panCamera`.
- **Horizon (H2).**
  - Bodies are `culled`.
  - `ProjectedFrame.center` is `WorldPoint | null`; nothing reads it (re-grepped).
  - Ring samples beyond the horizon are omitted: `ringBreaks`/`ringClosed` plus `traceRingPath`, and ProjectedOrbitRing/ReorderGhost never close an incomplete ring.
  - `frameBodies` uses the safe primitives. An unsafe trial pose never fits, and if none is safe it returns null.
  - Polaris: `polarisProjection` returns `WorldPoint | null`, `hitPolaris` returns false on null, and `Polaris.tsx` hides the marker at opacity 0.
  - `projectAnimatedFrame`/`projectSwitchChoreographyFrame` treat culled bodies as invisible and non-interactive.
- **Home fit.** `deriveHomePose(world, viewport, profile)`:
  - "whole-disc" is today's formula exactly at tilt 0 (tested against a frozen copy, positions included);
  - "strategy" is Lab C's far-rim/half-span bisection over the readability floor;
  - large Systems keep the floor and put outer rings off-screen.
- **Switch and recovery.** `interpolateCamera` mixes `lensShift` (fallback 1). `sampleRecovery` mixes it too and keeps the dolly focal.

### Task 3: one size mechanism, projected-size semantics, D-60, pan and coast
- **Size (`projectedBodyRadius`).**
  - Near cap: the Lab B perspective knee, then a soft viewport cap, never below pivot size; the sun is exempt.
  - Minimum size: 18 / 10 / 34 px diameters.
  - `hitRadius = max(22, radius)` from the same radius, and `frameBodies` bounds use it too.
- **Semantics (D-47).**
  - `magnification` = zoom × perspective, measured before the size mechanism.
  - `labelMagnification` = the median over on-screen, interactive contacts; it falls back to zoom.
  - `semanticLevel(magnification, …)`, thresholds unchanged.
  - OrreryWorld: the level reaction reads `labelMagnification`, the moons use `level.value`, and allocations use `labelMagnification`. There is no semantic use of `pose.zoom` left.
- **Tap rule (D-60).** `resolveOrreryTap`'s `named → "profile"` branch is deleted and `visibleNames` is removed from both resolvers and every caller. OrreryWorld's tap memo no longer depends on `allocations`, and the capture allowlist was updated. `tapIntent` lost its `IDENTITY_ZOOM` branch. The card's `onProfile`, the controller and the dispatcher are untouched.
- **Labels.** `LabelCandidate.depth` (camera-plane depth, positive = near). OrreryWorld's candidate builder fills it, and `compareRankedLabels` orders by priority, then nearest, then id.
- **Pan.** The pan's touch-down point is stored in samples. Each update passes the finger's previous position as `from`, and `panCamera` anchors the touched ground point while it projects with perspective ≥ 0.4. Otherwise it falls back to the centre-anchored mapping. Every update is capped at 160 pivot-depth points.
- **Coast.** `COAST_MS` 260, `PAN_COAST_POINTS` 48, anchored at the pivot. It is still disabled under reduced motion, and the yaw coast is unchanged.

## Behaviour changes for docs/systems/orrery.md (Plan 16)

1. **Placement (D-12).** Contacts never leave their ring. Decay and rogue are carried by ring colour, angle and the ring-style ladder; there is no outward drift. The world extent is the outer ring plus its body.
2. **Home axes (D-03/D-45).**
   - Home is `{tilt 62°, lensShift 0.62, strategy fit}`, so the pivot sits at 31 % of the usable height.
   - Recenter restores every Home axis, including tilt and lens shift.
   - The flat profile (0°, 1, whole-disc) is the old Home exactly.
   - The single switch is `ACTIVE_HOME_PROFILE`.
3. **Tilt range.** Two-finger tilt runs from 0 to 68° (`MAX_USER_TILT`); it was 0 to 60°.
4. **Camera.** A dolly: focal = 720 / zoom. Zoom now moves the camera, so at a tilt the near and far sides scale differently.
5. **Beyond the horizon.** Bodies past the camera's near plane are culled: not drawn, not tappable, not labelled, and never framed. Their ring arcs are drawn open. Polaris hides when north is behind the camera; the accessible north control stays.
6. **Sizes.** People never draw below 18 px, moons 10 px and the star 34 px (diameter). Near-side people grow at most 6 % from perspective and are softly capped near 8 % of the short side, but never below their pivot-depth size. The hit target is the drawn size or 22 px, whichever is larger.
7. **Label tiers** follow the median on-screen magnification of the visible people, not raw zoom. They are identical at a flat Home. Within a priority class, nearer names win collisions.
8. **Taps never open Profile (D-60).** A tap focuses: a new person, a different person, or the already-focused person (focus is kept). Profile opens only from the focus card's "Open Profile". Cluster taps still group.
9. **Pan.** One finger drags the ground under it, near the horizon it falls back to centre-anchored panning, and one update never moves more than 160 points. The coast lasts 260 ms, up to 48 points.
10. **Moons** appear by the last settled tier (projected size), not by raw zoom.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed issues

1. **[Rule 1 - Bug] Zero-delta pan jumped 1,000 units under the dolly** (Task 2).
   - **Issue:** `panCamera`'s near reach bound assumed the telephoto focal. With focal 720 the near reach lies behind the camera, so the denominator went negative and a 0-pixel pan moved the pivot.
   - **Fix:** the near side of the inverse is unbounded when its reach is behind the camera.
   - **Commit:** `be667387`
2. **[Rule 2 - Missing] `projectFrame` needed the camera pan extent** (Task 2).
   - **Issue:** the old focal-derived pan bound only recovered the gesture extent because the telephoto focal encoded it.
   - **Fix:** `options.extent` is added, and `projectAnimatedFrame`/`projectSwitchChoreographyFrame` forward it. OrreryWorld passes `frameExtent = cameraExtent(scene.extent)`, a new primitive capture added to the frame-mapper allowlist.
   - **Commit:** `be667387`
3. **[Rule 2] Recovery carries the lens shift** (Task 2). `sampleRecovery` mixes `lensShift` and uses the dolly focal, and `planRecovery`'s distance includes the lens-shift change. Recenter therefore animates every Home axis. **Commit:** `be667387`
4. **[Rule 2] `frameBodies` re-centring under a lens shift** (Task 2). The usable centre is no longer the projection centre, so the pivot now moves by the world offset between the bounds midpoint and the usable centre. At lens shift 1 this is the old iteration. **Commit:** `be667387`
5. **[Rule 2] `anchorCameraPose` uses `tryUnprojectToWorldPlane`** for its sky check instead of a hand-written denominator test. Behaviour is the same, and it can no longer throw. **Commit:** `be667387`
6. **The coast is anchored at the pivot** (Task 3). The centre-anchored mapping moved the pivot 55–64 points for a 48-point coast at 45°/62°. Anchoring makes the on-screen coast exactly the bound. **Commit:** `a09f3516`

### Interpretations (inside the plan's latitude)

- **Near cap.** Lab B's viewport cap is applied only to perspective growth: never below pivot size, sun exempt. Otherwise a focused body at MAX_ZOOM would be capped at about 33 px radius, which works against D-19's "large".
- **Pan cap is absolute** (160 points per update) rather than relative. The "100 px drag from the pivot" test runs as 10 gesture-like increments. A single 100 px update hitting the far side legitimately needs about 290 points at Home zoom.
- **Geometry deletions.** `RING_GAP_SEED`/`MIN_GAP` were deleted with `deriveOrreryMetrics`, the only thing they fed. The radius constants were kept.
- **Frozen-copy tests** (Plan 03) were not edited. The comparison was extended instead (new fields, the size mechanism, non-culling fixtures), and the old `MAX_TILT` value was inlined as `LEGACY_MAX_TILT`.
- **New test file** `src/components/orrery/orrery-horizon-render.test.tsx` covers render-level Polaris hiding and open ring arcs. It is not in `files_modified`.
- **`OrreryScreen.tsx` needed no change.** Its HOME_CAMERA, `frameBodies` and `deriveHomePose` uses work as-is, and the null-framing paths already existed.
- **Tracer gate.** Task 1's `<verify>` was re-run green (14 files / 177 tests, plus tsc). Per `autonomous: true` and the owner's standing direction (as in Plans 03/04), execution continued without a human-verify stop.

**Total deviations:** 6 auto-fixed (1 bug, 5 missing-functionality), plus the interpretations above. None is architectural, and no owner-bucket item was decided.

## Deferred / surfaced items (plain language, with recommendations)

1. **Tiny Systems land zoomed in at the tilted Home.**
   - **What happens:** Lab C's strategy fit keeps dollying in until the far rim reaches the top margin or the System is 1.2 screen-widths wide, up to zoom 4.
   - **Effect:** a 1–3-person System opens at roughly zoom 1.5–2.7, so its names (identity tier) show at Home. A sun-only System opens at zoom 4.
   - **Recommendation:** look at this at the Plan 14 D-03 device checkpoint. If you prefer Home to stay an "overview", set `HOME_STRATEGY_MAX_ZOOM` to 1, a one-number change.
2. **Initials will now be prepared for every visible person at overview.**
   - **Why:** the minimum size makes every visible contact at least 9 px in radius, which is above the text service's initials threshold (`ORRERY_INITIALS_MIN_RADIUS_PX = 7`, Plan 04).
   - **Effect:** at All Contacts more initials Paragraphs are requested. They are still bounded by the 1,200 hard cap and by visibility.
   - **Recommendation:** in Plan 08's spike, read `orreryTextService.stats()` at All Contacts. If pressure is high, raise the initials threshold above `MIN_CONTACT_PX / 2`, or tie it to magnification.
3. **The near cap is Lab B's starting value: near people at most 6 % larger.** With the dolly, the depth cue on the near side is now mostly position, not size.
   - **Recommendation:** tune `NEAR_PERSPECTIVE_KNEE` and `NEAR_RADIUS_FRACTION` at the device checkpoint. These are single numbers at the top of `orrery-camera-logic.ts`.
4. **The legacy 128-sample ring paths still cost about 21 ms per frame in Node at 1,000 bodies.** This is the same order as after Plan 03; culling adds a little. **Recommendation:** none here. Plan 06 passes `ringPaths: false` as planned. Note it if a debug device run looks slow before Plan 06.
5. **Carried, unchanged:**
   - `previewReorder`'s `indexOf` is O(bodies × ring members) per drag frame (Plan 03 item 1, for the D-23 reorder plan).
   - `focusEffectivelyOffscreen` does a linear scan (Plan 03 item 3).
   - OrreryScreen still caps single-focus zoom at `IDENTITY_ZOOM`. Plan 11 owns D-19/D-47 focus framing.
