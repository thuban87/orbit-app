# Phase 38.7 Plan 14: themes, contrast, the Preview and the Home tilt Summary

**Orrery cards and the Orrery title are proven at 4.5:1 in all four themes over the new sky, using the ADR-169 both-extrema method (pure black and pure white content bound every sky, nebula, star, initials and photo pixel). The only lever is the occlusion dim under cards, which now lives in theme tokens at 0.7 with the owner's 0.7 ceiling, and no theme needs more. The System Preview draws the V2 sky, rings, lit bodies and star sun, always flat, with its own resource-ledger epoch. The owner kept the 62° tilted Home, chose Galaxy Dark's nebula hues at 1.25× for Galaxy Light, raised the ring-fade floor to 80 %, loosened the near-side size cap, and asked for Standard Dark's title-bar edge to be smoothed like the light themes. All rulings are applied and re-proven.**

## What was built

### Contrast (Task 1, e0e3122c; re-proven after the rulings in ff0ed9a9)

Card proof: content pixel P (pure black, pure white, sky base/horizon, every nebula, sky-star, System-hue, star-palette and avatar-swatch token, and the initials ink) is dimmed toward `orreryOcclusion` at `ORRERY_OCCLUSION_DIM`. The card tint is then composited over it at the unchanged `ORRERY_OVERLAY_TINT_OPACITY`. `textPrimary`, `textSecondary` and `accentText` (every curated accent) must reach 4.5:1 against both the darkest and the brightest composite; the status glyphs must reach 3:1.

| Theme | Minimum occlusion dim for 4.5:1 | Worst ratio at the shipped 0.7 | Minimum title scrim for 4.5:1 | Title worst at Lab B's 0.84 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Galaxy Dark | **0.00** (4.50:1 at 0) | 5.50:1 textSecondary | **0.61** | 10.25:1 |
| Galaxy Light | **0.00** (4.95:1 at 0) | 5.67:1 accentText | **0** | 15.75:1 |
| Standard Dark | **0.04** (4.39:1 at 0) | 5.99:1 textSecondary | **0** | 14.41:1 |
| Standard Light | **0.00** (4.64:1 at 0) | 5.67:1 accentText | **0.04** | 13.98:1 |

- **D-62 did not escalate:** every theme passes well under the 0.7 ceiling, so there is no `D-62 escalation` marker. The dim stays at 0.7 for the look (T-DIM = DIM-A), not because contrast needs it.
- **The card tint, glass and backdrop ceiling are pinned unchanged by test:** `ORRERY_OVERLAY_TINT_OPACITY` 0.91 / 0.87, `CARD_GLASS_OPACITY` 0.05 / 0.5, ceiling 0.92 (D-20/D-35).
- **The title needs no extra scrim:** Lab B's 0.84 already passes in every theme, so no PROVISIONAL scrim was added. The ShellAppBar is unmodified.
- **The rulings left the numbers unchanged.** After them, a scratch recomputation through the same `contrastRatio` / `alphaComposite` path matched the code agent's numbers exactly. The new Galaxy Light sky moves nothing because pure black and white bound every sky pixel. SD-FIX only changes Standard Dark's scrim ink, and its bar's own chrome scrim is opaque (1.0).

### Occlusion-source contract (H5)

`orrery-occlusion-contract.test.ts` scans every Orrery text surface: `orrery-overlay` glass, `OrreryObstacle` ids, the notice/feedback sites and the immersive header. It checks that:

- each surface has an `ORRERY_OCCLUSION_SOURCES` entry;
- the focus card's entry is the derived `focusCardRect`;
- obstacle-rect surfaces do not animate position;
- "no-text" entries render no `AppText`;
- each source is covered by the card or title proof.

### System Preview (Task 2, 1b3c0935)

- **What it draws:** the V2 baked sky (still, untinted), the plane grid and sun-lit rings, lit bodies with photos and initials, the star sun, and a plain focus outline.
- **Camera:** `previewPose` is always tilt 0 / yaw 0 / lens 1.
- **What it leaves out:** warp, pulses, reticle, labels and the ambient clock. It keeps its own pan, pinch and frame-based tap.
- **Resources:** `createPreviewResources` owns photos, Paragraphs and the sky image under the Plan 07 ledger contract. It has its own epoch and layer ids (`preview-sky`, `preview-bodies` ack; plane and focus hold no ledger objects). On unmount it releases first and hands objects back after one UI round trip.
- **Tests:** flat pose, tap, the lifecycle gate, and that no cinematic layer mounts. Ledger tests cover a delayed ack, replacement, unmount order, a late decode/bake after unmount, remount, and isolation from the Orrery world's pins.

### Rulings applied (Task 5, ff0ed9a9)

- **GB-125:** `ACTIVE_GALAXY_LIGHT_FLAVOUR` "dark-hues"; `SKY_LOOKS.galaxy.light.nebulaStrength` 1 → 1.25. The lobes stay well under the shader's `min(1, …)` clamp (0.3 × 1.25 = 0.375).
- **FADE-80:** `RING_DENSITY_FLOOR` 0.15 → 0.8. `orrery-plane-logic.test.ts` and `draw-plane.test.ts` (which asserted the old floor via `< 0.3` without importing the constant) now pin the floor.
- **NEAR-C:** `NEAR_PERSPECTIVE_KNEE` 0.06 → 0.6, `NEAR_RADIUS_FRACTION` 0.08 → 0.12.
  - **Hit-radius check:** every consumer derives from the one `projectedBodyRadius`:
    - `projectFrame` sets `radius` and `hitRadius = max(MIN_HIT_RADIUS, radius)` (`orrery-camera-logic.ts`);
    - framing bounds in `frameBodies` and the focus-card discs (`orrery-focus-card-logic.ts` `projectDiscs`) call it directly;
    - label candidates offset by the frame body's `radius` + `ORRERY_LABEL_BODY_GAP` (`OrreryWorld.tsx`);
    - tap hit tests (`orrery-focus-logic`, `orrery-reorder-logic`, `orrery-recovery-logic`, `orrery-satellite-logic`) read `hitRadius`;
    - the reticle, charge ring and atmosphere read the drawn `radius`.

    So taps and labels follow the bigger drawn size, and nothing assumes ~6 % growth. The existing size-semantics test (`hit ≥ drawn`, `hitRadius == max(MIN_HIT_RADIUS, radius)`, growth ≤ pivot × (1 + knee), ≤ max(pivot, short × fraction), < raw perspective) is constant-relative and passes at the new values.
- **SD-FIX:**
  - **The new rule:** `headerScrimContinuous(themePackage, mode)` is true for both light themes and for Standard Dark (`HEADER_SCRIM_STANDARD_DARK_CONTINUOUS`). `headerScrimInkKey` and `headerScrimLayout` now take the package, and `OrreryImmersiveHeader` passes it.
  - **Standard Dark before:** the bar showed opaque `surface` at L≈27.8. Below it the scrim was `background` at 0.84, giving L≈16.9 over the sky base and ~21–22 over the real map (owner's reading).
  - **Standard Dark after:** the tail starts at the bar's combined opacity (1.0 `surface`) and fades over 44 dp.
  - **Galaxy Dark:** asserted unchanged. Its layout equals Lab B's single `background` stack at bar scrims 0.05 and 1.
- **Ruling notes only (values unchanged):** T-HOME (`ACTIVE_HOME_PROFILE`), T-TINYZOOM (`HOME_STRATEGY_MAX_ZOOM`), T-SCRIM (header scrim), T-DIM (`ORRERY_OCCLUSION_STYLE`, `ORRERY_OCCLUSION_DIM`) carry doc comments citing the ruling.
