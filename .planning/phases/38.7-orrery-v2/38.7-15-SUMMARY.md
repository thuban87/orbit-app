# Phase 38.7 Plan 15: warm tab return, switch start on the tap, FR-B and a first frame that is never black Summary

**Reusable Orrery resources (decoded photos, text Paragraphs and the CPU-baked sky) now survive an ordinary tab blur inside a hard 64 MiB / 128 / 4 budget and are dropped on background, after 5 minutes idle, or on a memory warning. A tab return mounts on the retained snapshot, already at Home. A System switch starts moving on the tap with a lead-in on the current scene while the destination loads, then hands off with no jump. The Orrery's SurfaceView votes 30 Hz, so the idle 6 Pro runs its panel at 60 Hz and renders at 30 (it was 120/120 before). The first frame is the theme's sky, and the cover lifts only once the content frame is on the display. It took three device fix rounds to get here, and a few DEBUG costs remain for Plan 16's release build to judge.**

## What was built

### Warm cache (D-58) and warm return (D-56)

- **Two lifecycles stay separate.**
  - The canvas, the ambient clock and the GPU surfaces (including the base bake) still stop and release on blur.
  - The warm owner holds keys, never pins, and only CPU-backed images. It is trimmed to its caps before `retainOnBlur` returns.
  - ADR-170 switch retirement is unchanged.
- **Tests:**
  - zero rebuild on a warm return;
  - caps and LRU, and an All Contacts-scale workload blurred 10 times stays within the caps;
  - background, idle and memory release, in both event orders;
  - out-of-order acks, Preview isolation, remount, late decode after unmount;
  - a texture-backed image is never retained;
  - a warm photo that is replaced or deleted is dropped;
  - full-cap progress (C-M1).
- **Warm return:**
  - Home is derived from the retained scene under the adopted viewport. Focus is cleared before the canvas mounts. `resumed()` is left to the `sessionReady` Home branch, which reassigns the pose only if the reloaded Home moved.
  - Framing tests (a)–(g) cover a panned and focused pose, a paused switch, an adopted obstacle, data changed while away, the latch, and the restore and different-System paths.
- **Not measured:** D-11 memory with the cache on (D-66 §5, per-plan 15 override). Plan 16 G7 judges it, and the lever is `ORRERY_WARM_MAX_BYTES`. **Code agent's warning:** a warm decoded photo that was drawn may keep a GPU texture attached (Skia can cache an upload with a CPU-backed image). If so, warm photos would count in Graphics/GL even though only CPU-backed images enter the warm set.

### FR-B (D-65)

- **Signal:** OrreryV2Canvas sends the vote from a UI reaction on change only, through `CanvasRef.setFrameRateVote`. It sends 30 while only the ambient clock animates. It sends 0 under reduced motion and during a switch, a gesture or camera motion. The vote applies only to the opaque (SurfaceView) canvas.
- **DEBUG 6 Pro** (`evidence/15-warm/frb-surfaceflinger-excerpt.txt`, raw `~/orbit-art/38.7/15-warm/frb/`):
  - The SurfaceView reports `requestedFrameRate: {30.00 Hz … Default}` at idle, after a tab round trip and after background → foreground.
  - `mActiveSfDisplayMode` reads 60 Hz and `mActiveRenderFrameRate` 30. It was 120 / 120.
  - The vote comes back within about 0.5 s after switches and after a fling during a switch.
- The release proof (the panel leaves 120 Hz at idle) is in Plan 16.

### Switch start on the tap (D-76)

- **The lead-in:** `orrery-switch-lead-logic` spins the world up exactly like the switch's accelerate phase, then holds the plateau. Warp and streaks rise with it, and Lab C's breath (D-77) eases in.
- **The hand-off:** the switch inherits the lead's momentum, so position, speed, signal and camera are continuous. The landing is exact.
- **Other paths:**
  - Reduced motion never leads; it crossfades.
  - A failed load coasts the lead back.
  - `settle()` clears the lead.
  - While the lead runs, busy is true, so there is no bake and the FR-B vote is withdrawn.
- **Cost work found by the device rounds:**
  - The lead's UI job is now the first thing the tap does.
  - The tap no longer republishes. The outgoing moons stay, and only the satellite reads are cancelled.
  - No text or tier report runs during the lead.
  - Body specs are sent as patches of changed bodies, coalesced at 64 ms: 34 → 8 writes per switch.
  - The base layer is reused while only the bodies move: in the bench, about 27 → 12 ms and 7,800 → 520 Skia calls per plateau frame.
  - Rings share one Paint per recording.

### First frame and the cover

- **First frame:** the sky spec is built before the first recording. A sky-only first frame (gradient and stars) posts at once, and the full scene records two frames after the surface exists. The canvas area paints the sky's base token.
- **Cover gate** (`cover-gate.ts`):
  - It arms once the content is recorded (the first-frame state is over and the body specs are published).
  - It lifts on the native answer for its token.
  - It is bounded: 3 s after arming and 6 s after mount.
  - "unlatched" lifts at once as `presented-unconfirmed`.
- **Native probe** (patched `SkiaBaseView`):
  - After the main-thread draw, a PixelCopy scales the newest queued buffer to 4×4. A buffer that is all black (every channel < 3) is retried.
  - Composite evidence then follows, bounded at 600 ms.
- **`c758ca30`:**
  - **The problem:** on SDK 37 the deprecated layer trusted-presentation callback never answered on the process's first surface. Every cold open logged `no composite evidence within 600ms trusted=true` → `unlatched` and waited the full 600 ms.
  - **The fix:** the window's next frame commit plus a vsync is now the evidence on API 29+. Trusted presentation is not used from API 35, and on API 34 it is raced against the commit evidence.
  - **Checks:**
    - the patch was regenerated with patch-package and applies to the pristine 2.6.2 tarball (its sha512 matches the lockfile);
    - every patched file is identical to node_modules;
    - the other nine patched files are byte-identical to the previous patch;
    - javac against android-35 and android-36 is clean.

### System selector name

- The store's initial or unknown name is `""` with `nameKnown` false, never the previous System's name.
- `systemDisplayName` uses the requested name when known, else the Systems catalog, else null.
- For null, the selector shows a fixed-width blank.

### Worklet default parameters (crash class)

`beginSwitchLead(intensity = SWITCH_LEAD_INTENSITY)` threw `ReferenceError` on the UI runtime on every switch. The cause: the worklets plugin unpacks the captured closure inside the body, after the default initializer has already run. vitest cannot see this.

- **Fixed in 6 places:** the lead, `drawContactBody`, `drawBodies`, `drawSun`, `deriveHomePose` and `frameBodies`.
- **Guard:** `orrery-worklet-default-params.test.ts` fails on any worklet default parameter that reads a non-literal identifier.

### The three fix rounds, by cause class

Plan 15 needed three device fix rounds after its first pass, which is more than the plan anticipated. The causes fell into five classes:

1. **On-device-only crash** (round 1):
   - **What failed:** a worklet default parameter read a module identifier and threw on the UI runtime.
   - **Why tests missed it:** vitest runs the worklet as plain JS.
   - **What guards it now:** a source-scan test.
2. **Ordering across the JS and UI threads** (rounds 1–2):
   - The lead's UI job was queued behind React's render.
   - The tap's empty publication removed the moons and rewrote every body spec in the same UI batch.
   - A cancelled recovery motion stayed "active".
3. **Per-update cost on a ~1,000-body System** (rounds 2–3):
   - Full body-spec rewrites for every photo decode and every initials report.
   - Text/tier reports during the lead.
   - Every lead frame re-recorded about 1,000 rings.
4. **Proxy signals taken as "on screen"** (rounds 2–3):
   - **First fix:** the cover had lifted on JS proxies (surface size, two frames), so the probe was made native.
   - **Second fix:** a drawn buffer is not a composited one, so it now needs composite evidence too.
5. **Platform API behaviour** (round-3 re-check): trusted presentation is deprecated from API 35 and silent on the first surface on SDK 37 (`c758ca30`).

Title: "Orrery keeps a bounded reusable resource cache across tab blur (clarifies ADR-170)". Status: Accepted (Phase 38.7, D-58, owner 2026-10-02).
