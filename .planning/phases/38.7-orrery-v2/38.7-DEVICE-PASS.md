### FR-B on release (beside G6)

Animation scales 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.0 stated first. Size 120 at Home, idle ≥ 20 s untouched: `dumpsys display`
`mActiveSfDisplayMode … peakRefreshRate=60.0, vsyncRate=60.0`, `mActiveRenderFrameRate=30.0`; `dumpsys SurfaceFlinger`
`SurfaceView[…](BLAST)#58650 requestedFrameRate: {30.00 Hz FrameRateCompatibility::Default …}`; `atrace -t 4 gfx view`:
SurfaceView frames at 31.6/s. **PASS: the panel leaves 120 Hz at idle** (60 Hz mode, rendering at 30). Evidence:
`evidence/16-release/frb-release-excerpt.txt`; raw `Pixel_6_Pro/frb/`.

**Finding (gap): the vote also caps motion that is not driven by a finger at 30 fps.** The vote is withdrawn (0) during
camera motion, but with no vote and no touch, SurfaceFlinger keeps the panel at the 30 Hz it is already running. Polls:
during an adb pan the vote is gone and the render rate is 120; during a Recenter flight the render rate stays 30.0. The
Recenter present intervals are all 33.3 ms (p50 33.28, p90 33.45), so G1 Recenter reads 29.5. The spike (no vote, panel
at 120) measured 36.0. The same cap applies to focus flights and to the part of a switch after the tap's touch boost
expires (Size 5 → Size 120: 60–68 frames/s for the first second, then 24–32). Recommendation: during motion, vote the
panel's maximum (or `FRAME_RATE_CATEGORY_HIGH`) instead of withdrawing, and return to 30 at idle. Code change: a gap item
for the owner.

**Owner-found bugs and their causes.**

1. **The switch played off to the side, then snapped; the map shifted when a dropdown opened.** Every measured obstacle
   was in the map's viewport, and the projection centre is the centre of the largest free rect. So opening the System panel
   (`orrery-system-panel`), the View panel, the cluster panel or any notice re-centred the map into the space beside it.
   A System row's tap calls `onSelect` before `dismiss()` (since `5de57de8`). The C4-M1 hold therefore froze the
   panel-shrunk viewport for the whole switch, and the settle adopted the live viewport, moving the centre in one frame.
   Fixed by the persistent-chrome framing; the hold is removed (`c84742de`).
2. **Focus card buttons ignored taps.** The camera controls' root was a 200 dp wide, `pointerEvents="auto"`
   box at zIndex/elevation 12 with a full-width scroll view. Only its 55 dp plates are visible, so the card docked under
   it and lost ~30–45 % of each button (the right side) to the invisible box. Separately, a hidden card kept its last
   position at opacity 0 and still took taps. Fixed: controls root `box-none` and a plate-wide scroll view (the
   measured obstacle is unchanged), the card at zIndex/elevation 13, and a hidden card off-screen (`fd502ba5`).
