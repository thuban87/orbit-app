## What this means

1. **The TextureView cannot meet G1.** It presents at ~20–27/s however fast the scene is (6 Pro Recenter 22, adb pan
   27.5; DEAD-ENDS A12). Only the opaque SurfaceView canvas lifts presents (Recenter 36, adb pan 53 at Size 120) and it also
   halves the All Contacts switch stall (383 → 191 ms).
2. **The SurfaceView costs idle heat on the 6 Pro.** It pins the panel at **120 Hz** whatever the ambient driver does
   (every SurfaceView sample, both drivers), and with the ambient running it adds ~10 % of a core over the TextureView. With
   animations on, the recommended configuration idles at 41.8 % CPU vs main's 34.2 % at rest. **Neither driver lets the
   6 Pro panel leave 120 Hz with the SurfaceView** (D-49: reported, not hidden). On the 3a (60 Hz panel) the SurfaceView is
   the CHEAPER canvas (32 % vs 49 % total).
3. **Frame-clock beats interval on release.** The interval driver avoids a per-vsync callback but pays a JS → UI write per
   tick; on release it is equal or worse on CPU (6 Pro 31.3 vs 42.4 % TextureView; 41.3 vs 42.8 % SurfaceView) and gives a
   jittery 21–25 presents/s instead of a steady 30. (On the 3a it is 2 % cheaper on the SurfaceView.)
4. **The bake is a large idle win, biggest at All Contacts** (6 Pro SurfaceView 70.0 → 41.7 %, TextureView 61.7 → 32.0 %;
   Size 120 57.1 → 41.3 %), for ~30 MB of GPU memory.
5. **Ring method.** The world-plane rings are a real but partial cost. Replaying the live base costs ~3.5 ms per frame at
   Size 120 and ~8 ms at All Contacts (from the live-vs-bake idle difference at 30 redraws/s, SurfaceView), plus its
   per-frame re-recording during motion (not separately measured). An All Contacts adb-pan frame takes ~40 ms (24.5/s), so
   even a ring cost of zero would leave All Contacts motion near ~30/s, short of G1's 45, and the 191 ms switch stall is not
   a ring-drawing frame. The remaining per-frame cost (frame projection, body and label pictures, label allocation over
   ~1,000 bodies, the switch's scene load) is not attributed by this spike. A ring field (Task 4) would therefore not by
   itself meet G1/G5 at All Contacts, and it costs a mechanism proof, a parity suite and a second release build (D-61).
6. **Everything is crash- and ANR-free** on both phones in every adb scenario (main: 5 crashes on the 6 Pro, 2 on the 3a,
   ANRs). The SurfaceView shows a **0.5 s black canvas** when the Orrery tab is re-entered (Pitfall 12; DEBUG screenshots in
   `~/orbit-art/38.7/spike/Pixel_6_Pro/screening/sv-artefact/frame048-052.png`), after ~0.8 s of empty background; leaving
   the tab is clean.

## Recommendation (RELEASE numbers only)

| Item | Recommended | Why (release evidence) |
|------|-------------|------------------------|
| Ring method (D-44) | **world-plane** (keep the default; no ring field) | Rings are ~3.5 ms (Size 120) / ~8 ms (All Contacts) of a frame; removing them would not reach G1/G5 at All Contacts (item 5). The All Contacts gap is per-frame body/label/projection work and the switch load: later plans must close it and Plan 16 re-measures. |
| Ambient driver (D-49) | **frame-clock** | Equal or lower CPU than interval on the 6 Pro (31.3 vs 42.4 %; 41.3 vs 42.8 %) and a steady 30/s; interval's per-tick JS→UI write costs more than the vsync callback it saves. |
| Opaque canvas | **on (SurfaceView)** | The only way G1 is reachable (TextureView capped ~22–27/s); halves the All Contacts switch stall; cheaper on the 3a. Costs: 120 Hz idle on the 6 Pro (D-49 report below), the 0.5 s black flash on tab return. |
| Bake | **on (`gpu-offscreen`)** | 6 Pro idle −28 % of a core at All Contacts and −16 % at Size 120 on the SurfaceView; ~30 MB GPU. |

### Result (2026-10-03)

Run 15:38–15:49 on the Pixel 6 Pro only (`1A071FDEE002BU`), spike release APK installed with `install -r`. Host selected
by URL (`orrery-spike host=opaque=on,driver=frame-clock,bake=on,ring=world-plane,minRings=400 source=url`), Digest → Orrery
re-entry confirmed the same host `source=mount`. System verified as Size 120 from a UI dump before each Orrery capture.
Each capture came after ≥ 65 s with no touches. Raw captures (full SF dumps before and after, `--list`, display mode,
`sf_sampler.py --probe-layers 10`, a 10 s all-layer present count, a 4 s `atrace gfx`): `~/orbit-art/38.7/d49/{a-orrery-idle,b-digest-idle,c-orrery-ambient-off}/`.

Two extra captures, neither changing any repo tool: `sf_sampler.py --probe-layers` only checks Orbit's own layers, so a
scratch `allprobe.py` (copied into `d49/`) counted presents for **every** layer in `--list`. And `dumpsys SurfaceFlinger`
on this build (Android 17) prints each layer's *requested* vote but not the scheduler's *computed* one, so a 4 s
`atrace -t 4 gfx` captured SurfaceFlinger's `getRefreshRateVote` decision for each layer.

| Scenario | SF mode (`activeMode` / render rate) | Explicit per-layer votes (on-screen layers) | Layers posting buffers (10 s, all layers) | Scheduler's computed vote (atrace) |
|----------|------|------|------|------|
| A. Orrery idle, animations ON (ambient ~30 Hz) | 120 / 120 | none: the app window `VRI-…MainActivity` = `{0.00 Hz Default, Category NoPreference}`; StatusBar, Taskbar, ScreenDecor ×2 = same NoPreference; the SurfaceView `…(BLAST)#49351` has no vote (blank Frame Rate column in the HWC table) | **only the Orrery SurfaceView**: 29.9/s (`allprobe`); `probe-layers`: SurfaceView 331 in 10 s, app window **0** | `layerHistory={active=1}`; in 4 s the SurfaceView was the **only** layer scored: 120 `getRefreshRateVote` calls, 117 directly under its name, 3 with another thread's marker in between, no other layer name. All 120 results: `Max (can't resolve refresh rate)` → `Max 0.00 Hz (0.81)` |
| B. Digest tab (no Orrery mounted) | 120 / 120 (SF's last choice; not re-evaluated) | same NoPreference votes | **none** (app window 0, no system-UI layer posted) | no `getRefreshRateVote` at all in 4 s (nothing posted, so nothing to score) |
| C. Orrery idle, ambient off (scales 0.0, then restored to 1.0) | 120 / 120 (not re-evaluated) | same; new SurfaceView `#49371` no vote | **none** (SurfaceView 0, app window 0) | none in 4 s |

The only layers that vote above NoPreference are off-screen: `VRI-NotificationShade` (`HighHint`) and the Android Auto
virtual displays (`GhFacetBar`, `HUN`, `GhToast`, category `High`). They are in the "Offscreen Hierarchy" and none of them
were scored.

**Conclusion.** The **Orrery SurfaceView** holds the 6 Pro at 120 Hz. The app's main window and system UI do not: during
Orrery idle the window posted no buffers, and no on-screen layer votes above NoPreference. The SurfaceView has no explicit
vote, so SurfaceFlinger estimates a rate from its presents. That estimate fails ("can't resolve refresh rate"), so the
SurfaceView votes **Max** and the selector picks 120. *Why* the estimate fails (probably uneven present intervals from the
frame-clock driver) was not investigated, per the measurement guardrail. On the Digest tab and with ambient off nothing
posts. SF's reported mode stays at 120 there only because no new content arrives to change it. Whether the panel then
idles in hardware (`KernelIdleTimer=true`, 80 ms) is **not measurable** from a non-root shell: the panel's sysfs
(`/sys/devices/platform/exynos-drm/primary-panel/{refresh_rate,panel_idle,time_in_state}`) returns "Permission denied".

**FR-B: worthwhile (likely to work, not yet proven).** An explicit `Surface.setFrameRate(30,
FRAME_RATE_COMPATIBILITY_DEFAULT)` on the SurfaceView replaces exactly the failing Max vote. That layer is the only one
scored at idle, so nothing else would hold 120: the main window posts nothing and votes NoPreference, and no visible layer
votes higher. With a 30 Hz explicit vote and no Max voter, the selector should pick the 60 Hz mode (render rate possibly
30). Limits: this is inferred from the SurfaceFlinger decision trace, not observed, so only an FR-B release build on the
6 Pro can prove it. Touch still boosts to 120 (`touchTimer=200 ms`). Any time the window posts (UI updates, other tabs'
work), that layer is scored too. Not checked: whether removing the SurfaceView's Max vote by making the presents evenly
spaced would let the built-in estimate resolve to 30 Hz without FR-B.

## Surfaced items (out of this plan's scope; plain language, with a recommendation)

1. **Something outside the Orrery keeps the UI thread busy at rest, app-wide.** With the Orrery not even mounted (Digest

   second from the UI runtime (JS thread ~0.2 %, so it is not React re-rendering). Some UI-thread animation or frame
   callback runs continuously across the app. It was already inside main's baseline (34 % at rest) and it eats into every
   idle-heat gate. **Recommendation:** a small investigation plan (find the always-running UI-thread work; likely an
   animated background/art layer or a frame callback that never pauses), outside 38.7's Orrery scope unless you want it here.
2. **The 6 Pro had "Remove animations" switched on** (all three animation scales 0.0), so the app runs it in reduced motion:
   the Orrery's ambient twinkle/pulse never moves there, and the Day-1 baseline soak on that phone measured that state.
   Left as found (restored after the motion-on samples). **Recommendation:** keep it for comparability with the baseline,
   but note G6 on the 6 Pro does not exercise the ambient clock; the motion-on soak above is the honest D-49 number.

   SurfaceView until the first frame (or delay showing the SurfaceView until its first present); owner to judge on device.
