acceptances.

1. **G6 Size 120 idle temperature fails as written.** The CPU passes by a wide margin (11 % against 34 %). The
   temperature miss comes from a warm start inside the cool-down rule (+0.83 °C over the baseline start); the phone cooled
   1.1 °C during the soak. All Contacts, which started cooler, passes. *Recommendation:* accept it as a warm-start
   artefact, or re-soak from a cold phone in a later device pass.
2. **Idle costs two thirds of a core with animations on** (66–67 %, 8 % GC; the spike measured 42 % before Plans
   09–15's ambient visuals). This is context only: main has no animations-on soak. *Recommendation:* profile the ambient
   tick on a release build in a later phase, starting with the GC (likely the budget-40 pulses, D-68, then the lit body
   shader and the nebula/twinkle).
3. **Emoji initials ellipsize.** "Mia 🌸 Nguyễn" draws initials "M…": `orreryInitials` takes the first grapheme of the
   first two words ("M🌸"), which is wider than the disc. Cosmetic. *Recommendation:* skip non-letter words when
   picking initials (a small later fix).
4. **3a companion sheet slow at All Contacts.** On the 3a release smoke, the "Contacts in this System" sheet did not
   open within 6 s at All Contacts (no ANR, no crash). The 3a is smoke-only under D-66. *Recommendation:* no action
   unless the owner wants the 3a looked at.

### ADR candidates

1. **Orrery keeps a bounded reusable resource cache across tab blur (clarifies ADR-170)** (D-58). The draft from Plan
   15 (`38.7-15-SUMMARY.md`), carried as written with one correction: memory is **measured in Plan 16 G7** (D-11 cold
   and background sets, release), not in Plan 15.
   - Status: Accepted (Phase 38.7, D-58, owner 2026-10-02).
   - Context: ADR-170 retires settled switch resources, so memory never grows with the number of switches. ADR-048
     and ADR-077 keep one unmountable ambient clock. Tab return was slow in production and in every lab, because the
     canvas unmounts on blur and every return rebuilt photos, text and sky.
   - Decision: two lifecycles. The active render lifecycle (canvas, ambient clock, GPU surfaces including the base
     bake) stops and releases on blur and background, as before. A separate cache of CPU-backed objects only (decoded
     photo derivatives, OrreryTextService Paragraphs, CPU-baked sky/nebula images; never GPU-texture-backed images)
     survives an ordinary tab blur. It is bounded by `ORRERY_WARM_MAX_BYTES` 64 MiB, 128 Paragraphs and 4 sky images,
     with LRU eviction. It is released on app background, after 5 min idle since the last visit, and on a memory
     warning. Disposal never touches an object a recorded picture still references.
   - Consequences: tab return reuses resources (G3 Size 120 0.451 s against 6.72 s on main). **Memory: Plan 16 G7, on
     the 6 Pro release, cold and background sets.** Graphics and GL grew (+15–51 %), but the cause is the opaque canvas
     and the base bake, not the cache: the cache is released on background and empty at a cold T0, and it held
     30.0 MiB of its 64 MiB at a blur. TOTAL PSS fell 26–31 %. The owner accepted G7 (MEM-1, D-79). ADR-170 stays in
     force.
   - Cites ADR-170, ADR-048, ADR-077 and 38.6 D-11.
   - Key files: `src/components/orrery/resources/orrery-warm-cache.ts`, `orrery-text-service.ts`,
     `orrery-photo-cache.ts`, `src/components/orrery/v2/nebula-bake.ts`, `src/screens/OrreryScreen.tsx`.
2. **The V2 renderer and render host** (D-43, D-44, D-46, D-49, D-64, D-65 with FV-1). Covers the fixed picture tree,
   world-plane rings under the camera homography, the opaque SurfaceView, the GPU-baked base, one quantised 30 Hz
   ambient clock, and the native frame-rate vote (idle 30, motion 120, reduced motion 0; patched Skia 2.6.2, D-34 no
   Skia bump). Partially supersedes ADR-077/ADR-048 where they name the old declarative renderer.
3. **Tilted strategy-map Home and dolly camera** (D-03, D-45, D-78 HOME-A), with D-12 (bodies exactly on their rings),
   superseding the drift clauses (see below).
4. **Persistent-chrome framing** (D-79). The map's framing comes from persistent chrome only; transient surfaces never
   move it. This reverses D-19's cluster-panel obstacle and the "feedback is a framing exclusion" rule. It could fold
   into ADR 3 if the extractor prefers.
5. **Ambient orbit pulses and the painted sky** (D-07, D-10, D-68, D-75): the explicit exception to "motion encodes
   data, not mood".
