## 2. The hands-on checks (one check per item)

**A. Pan and pinch windows (G1). The measurement; same as Day 1.**
1. Tap once on empty sky and wait 2 seconds (this lines up the clocks).
2. In `Size 120`, three times: pan with ONE finger for about 10 seconds, pause 3 seconds, then pinch in and out with
   TWO fingers for about 10 seconds, pause 3 seconds.
3. Put the phone down for 5 seconds. The phone switches itself to `All Contacts` (do not use the System selector).
   When it has settled, repeat step 2.

Answer: "done".

**B. Feel at Size 120 (G8).** During A, did panning and pinching in `Size 120` feel smooth? Yes / no (+ a word on what felt off).

**C. Feel at All Contacts (G8).** During A, did panning and pinching in `All Contacts` feel smooth? Yes / no (+ a word).

**D. Pinch, twist and tilt together (review fix CR-02).** After A, put two fingers down and, in one movement, spread
them, twist them and move both up the screen. Does the view zoom, turn and tilt together, without sticking? Yes / no.
(adb cannot send two fingers, so this one needs your hand. Right after it, the agent reads the phone's frame-rate vote
to confirm the idle 30 Hz vote comes back.)

**E. Reorder hold feel.** Press and hold a person for about a second: the charge ring fills, the phone buzzes, and the
person and its ring light up. Lift WITHOUT dragging (so nothing is reordered). Did the hold and the buzz feel right?
Yes / no. (The agent confirmed on screen that the hold arms; only the buzz and feel need your finger.)

**F. Focus zoom look (D-47).** Tap one person: the camera zooms past the old limit and docks their card. Does the
zoomed-in framing look right? Yes / no. Then tap "Clear focus".

**G. TalkBack through "Contacts in this System".** Last, after the recorders stop: turn TalkBack on, open the list
button ("Contacts in this System", right side), swipe through a few people. Do the names, the "Focus in Orrery" and
"Open Profile" buttons read sensibly? Yes / no. Then turn TalkBack off. (The agent already checked that the
accessibility labels exist; this is the by-ear check.)

## 3. Decisions (short codes; one recommendation each; "approve all" works)

**SW: switch smoothness at All Contacts (D-76).** On the release build the switch now **starts on the tap in every
direction** (16–100 ms; the sheet no longer lags when leaving All Contacts). Size 5 ↔ Size 120 is smooth. **To and from
All Contacts it is still slow:** leaving All Contacts runs at about 8–16 frames per second for the first ~1.25 s.
Going into All Contacts drops to about 8–12 frames per second for ~1 s in the middle. Clip (left: leaving All
Contacts, middle: into All Contacts, right: Size 5 → Size 120 for comparison; tap at 1.0 s; plays twice):
`.planning/phases/38.7-orrery-v2/evidence/16-release/switch-release-compare.mp4`.
- **SW-1 (recommended):** a gap plan builds "simpler rings while the switch moves" (the cheaper option from Plan 15)
  together with the FV-1 fix below, then shows you a labelled side-by-side (as now / simpler rings) to pick from.
  This plan could not film that option: it needs a code change and a new build.
- SW-2: accept the switch as it is.

**FV: frame-rate vote caps hands-off motion at 30 fps (new finding).** The new 30 Hz idle vote works: the idle panel
drops from 120 Hz to 60 Hz and renders at 30. But when the vote is withdrawn for motion, the screen stays at 30 Hz
unless a finger is on it. So Recenter, focus flights and most of a switch run at 30 frames per second. Recenter
measured **29.5** frames per second against the locked **55** (G1). The spike, without the vote, measured 36.
- **FV-1 (recommended):** a gap fix: during motion, ask for the panel's top rate instead of withdrawing the vote, then
  return to 30 at idle. The idle saving stays as is.
- FV-2: leave it.

**MEM: memory gate G7 fails, and the warm cache is not the cause.** Graphics memory is +21 % (cold start) and +45 %
(return from background) over Day 1, against a +5 % limit. Nearly all of it is the opaque canvas's screen buffers
(a flat +34–49 MB) and GPU textures, including the base bake's (+12–38 MB). Those are the canvas and the base bake you
approved in Plan 08. They are needed for the frame-rate and idle gains. Total memory the app holds is **26–31 % lower**
than Day 1, and native memory is 24–41 % lower. Shrinking the warm cache would not change the gate.
- **MEM-1 (recommended):** keep the build as is. Rule G7 on Graphics + GL as it stands, and record the reason (the
  opaque canvas and the bake), or restate G7 on total memory.
- MEM-2: ask for a gap plan that measures a lower-buffer variant (e.g. a double-buffered canvas, or a bake at lower
  resolution) and its cost to smoothness.

The other gates are reported in `38.7-DEVICE-PASS.md` and are not yours to act on here. The remaining fails are
G3 All Contacts tab return (1.008 s vs ≤ 1.0 s, 8 ms over) and G5 Size 120 ↔ All Contacts switch stall (174.7 ms vs
≤ 100 ms; the spike had 191). Task 4 lists them for sign-off, each with a recommendation. Nothing is marked accepted
without you.
