## Accomplishments

- **D-52 / ADR-105:** `indexSatellites` builds a member-filtered, deduplicated, per-parent index on JS once per (scene generation, rows). `deriveSatelliteBodies` reads it with no per-frame sort, stringify or row scan. A non-member sun gets no moons.
- **D-31 / M13:** `moonSplitFactor` smoothsteps the frame's own `sampledCamera.zoom` from IDENTITY_EXIT to IDENTITY_ENTER. At 0 a parent shows a moon-ink half-disc bump on its rim. Moons move out from the rim and fade in as the bump fades out. Moons are tappable only at split 1. The frame carries `moonBumps` and `moonSplit`.
- **C-M3 / D-53:** the satellite publisher writes a SharedValue the frame worklet reads. OrreryWorld no longer takes a rows prop. The focus card / companion copy is held during a switch and flushed once on settle.
- **D-54:** `detectStacks` groups overlapping visible contacts through a screen-space grid, and a test proves it equals the brute-force scan on 1,000-body frames. The front body draws ring-coloured pips on the side away from its moon bump, and the last pip becomes a "more" ring on overflow.
- **D-13 / D-14:** each contact is one lit-body shader draw: the photo or swatch, sun-lit with lighter shading, Lab B's status rim and atmosphere, rogue cold with no glow, decay/rogue desaturated. A defocus uniform sits at 0 for Plan 11.
- **D-15 / D-37:** the sun is Lab A's corona and granulated disc. Its detail swings only with the sun pulse and is fixed under reduced motion.
- Every shader compiles lazily. A null effect or failed build falls back to today's flat body. SkSL is compile-checked in CanvasKit.
- **H8:** a dev item, "Orrery: assign test portraits (app files/orrery-portraits)", puts the 16 fictional portraits on the first Size 20 members. Plan 01's restore undoes it, and a test covers seed → assign → restore → verify.
- **Task 5:** the owner's rulings were applied and recorded (below).
