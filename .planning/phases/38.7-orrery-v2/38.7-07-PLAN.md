<resource_lifetime_contract>
## Canonical resource-lifetime contract (H3) — single source; Plans 13 and 15 cite this section

Implemented once in `resources/orrery-generation-ledger.ts` and used by `orreryTextService`, `orreryPhotoCache` and any later native-object holder (baked sky images, switch sprites):

1. **Per-layer acknowledgement.** Every picture layer that draws specs (`bodiesBehind`, `sun`, `bodiesFront`, `labels`, and any later layer) acknowledges separately: ack key = `(layerId, generation, mountEpoch)`, written by that layer's mapper only after its `createPicture` call has returned, and forwarded to JS only after at least one further UI frame callback has run (mapper quiescence: no UI-thread mapper can still be recording against, or about to swap in, the old generation — the `<Picture>` swap may lag the mapper by a frame). That callback does NOT prove the native canvas has drawn the new picture — native draw is scheduled separately (`RNSkView.h:157`). Native draws are protected instead by `sk_sp` ownership: `RNSkPictureView::performDraw` copies `sk_sp<SkPicture> picture = _picture` before drawing (`node_modules/@shopify/react-native-skia/cpp/rnskia/RNSkPictureView.h:63-65`), so an in-flight native draw keeps the old picture alive, and a recorded `SkPicture` holds its own references to what it draws. The executor confirms (by reading the installed Skia recording path for `drawImage`/`drawImageRect` and `drawParagraph`, not by assumption) that the recorded picture owns or copies its SkImage and Paragraph contents, and records the finding, with file:line, in the SUMMARY; if a content type is found NOT to be owned by the recorded picture, stop and report rather than shipping disposal for it (C3-L1). Mappers never share one `recordedGeneration`. The ledger records which layers recorded each generation (the layers a generation's specs are published to).
2. **Retire only after every recording layer has moved on.** A generation's native objects (pictures, Paragraphs, SkImages, shaders) that the newer generation no longer references are disposed only after EVERY layer that recorded the old generation has acknowledged a newer generation within the same `mountEpoch`. Out-of-order acks across layers are normal; one layer's ack never retires another layer's references.
3. **Teardown order** (unmount and background): stop the ambient clock → detach the pictures from the canvas (layers render nothing / the Canvas unmounts) → after one UI round-trip confirms no mapper can still run, dispose every retained generation of that epoch in reverse creation order (objects handed to the warm owner by Plan 15 are first transferred to it as evictable warm retention — not a render pin — and excluded from this disposal) → bump `mountEpoch`.
4. **Stale epochs.** A late ack, or a late decode/shape result, tagged with a stale `mountEpoch` is discarded and its objects disposed immediately; it never publishes and never retires anything in the new epoch.
5. **Required tests** (ledger unit tests here; Plan 15 re-runs them against the warm owner): out-of-order acks across layers; a delayed consumer (a layer's ack held back, including the one-frame forward delay of rule 1 — nothing it recorded is disposed until it arrives); replacement (a newer generation replaces an object; the old one is disposed exactly once, after every recording layer acked); remount (new epoch while old-epoch acks are still arriving); background/unmount teardown order; a late decode completing after unmount.
6. **Every native-object holder uses this ledger** (C-H2) — none keeps its own "recorded" flag or disposes from an unmount effect: the text service and photo cache (here), the base snapshot (`base` layer, Plan 08), the neutral baked sky (`sky` layer, Plan 09), the switch streak texture (`switch` layer, Plan 13) and the System Preview, which runs its own ledger epoch with its own layer ids (Plan 14). Each holder's plan adds the delayed-consumer, replacement and unmount tests above for its objects.
7. **Warm retention is not a pin.** Render pins exist only for a mounted canvas's current published generation. The warm owner (Plan 15) holds evictable warm entries that count against the caps and can always be evicted (disposed) while nothing records them; it never pins.
</resource_lifetime_contract>
