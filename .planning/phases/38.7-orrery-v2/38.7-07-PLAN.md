<resource_lifetime_contract>
## Canonical resource-lifetime contract (H3) — single source; Plans 13 and 15 cite this section

Implemented once in `resources/orrery-generation-ledger.ts` and used by `orreryTextService`, `orreryPhotoCache` and any later native-object holder (baked sky images, switch sprites):

1. **Per-layer acknowledgement.** Every picture layer that draws specs (`bodiesBehind`, `sun`, `bodiesFront`, `labels`, and any later layer) acknowledges separately: ack key = `(layerId, generation, mountEpoch)`, written by that layer's mapper only after its `createPicture` call has returned, and forwarded to JS. Mappers never share one `recordedGeneration`. The ledger records which layers recorded each generation (the layers a generation's specs are published to).
2. **Retire only after every recording layer has moved on.** A generation's native objects (pictures, Paragraphs, SkImages, shaders) that the newer generation no longer references are disposed only after EVERY layer that recorded the old generation has acknowledged a newer generation within the same `mountEpoch`. Out-of-order acks across layers are normal; one layer's ack never retires another layer's references.
3. **Teardown order** (unmount and background): stop the ambient clock → detach the pictures from the canvas (layers render nothing / the Canvas unmounts) → after one UI round-trip confirms no mapper can still run, dispose every retained generation of that epoch in reverse creation order (objects handed to a warm owner by Plan 15 are re-pinned under that owner first and excluded) → bump `mountEpoch`.
4. **Stale epochs.** A late ack, or a late decode/shape result, tagged with a stale `mountEpoch` is discarded and its objects disposed immediately; it never publishes and never retires anything in the new epoch.
5. **Required tests** (ledger unit tests here; Plan 15 re-runs them against the warm owner): out-of-order acks across layers; remount (new epoch while old-epoch acks are still arriving); background teardown order; a late decode completing after unmount.
</resource_lifetime_contract>
