## Concerns

### Plan 01 — HIGH

### Plan 09 — HIGH

- The required pre-transaction merge-planning resolution is not actually specified: it says “reject, skip, or modified-at rule.” That is an unresolved product/data-integrity decision, so it cannot be implemented or tested deterministically.

### Plan 10 — MEDIUM

- A committed consumer ledger is valuable, but ripgrep-based enforcement needs a narrowly specified production-file scope and an explicit syntax inventory. Otherwise migrations, comments, tests, and future harmless reads will make it brittle or encourage broad allowlists.

- The device UAT appropriately covers the lifecycle, but the required “already-migrated v10 method+label build” should be made a preflight artifact/check rather than an informal assumption.

## Suggestions

- Revise Plan 01 to define:

  - transitive children requiring rebuild because their immediate parent is rebuilt;
  - per-table expected FK target and action assertions.

- In Plan 02, require `modified_at` and exactly-one `data_revision` behavior for successful Bind and Unbind.

- In Plan 08, make lifecycle changes an explicit aggregate operation or a clearly separated SQL branch so an edit cannot accidentally attempt positive→`NULL`.

- Before executing Plan 09, lock one merge-conflict resolution rule and specify whether the local row, incoming row, or entire restore is retained/rejected.

- In Plan 06, extract and test the async widget actionability guard independently from the React component.

**HIGH** until Plans 01 and 09 are corrected. Migration 011 can otherwise mishandle the post-v9 FK graph, and restore has an explicitly unresolved policy for a real v11 trigger conflict. The remaining plans are generally well ordered and implement the locked lifecycle decisions correctly.

# Phase 18.2 Bound/Unbound Lifecycle — Cross-AI Plan Review (Cycle 2)

## Summary

After verifying all ten plans against on-disk source, the re-cut is substantially sound: the v10→v11 migration numbering matches reality (`database.ts` registers through migration 010 at `TARGET_VERSION = 10`; no `011` exists yet), wave ordering respects real dependencies, the locked owner decisions (backup v3 and widget fail-closed scope) are wired into the right plans, and consumer ownership is mostly traced to the actual query/read files that today lack any `tracking_enabled` predicate. The phase should achieve its goals if executed as written.

The remaining gaps are concentrated in three areas: (1) migration 011’s contacts-table rebuild complexity, (2) asymmetric post-commit side effects for Bind vs Unbind, and (3) a few underspecified executor choices (backup merge-conflict resolution, nullable TypeScript contracts). None of these invalidate the plan set, but they are worth closing before execution.

## Concerns

### HIGH

- **Migration 011 is the highest irreversibility risk in the phase.** SQLite cannot relax `interval_days NOT NULL` in place; Plan 01 requires a full contacts rebuild with per-child FK/index/trigger proofs across nine dependent tables. The v9 rebuild pattern in `009-contact-method-normalization.ts:205-216` is the template, but 011 adds CHECK constraints, a one-way trigger, and three post-009 children not in `EXPECTED_CONTACT_CHILDREN`. Any missed FK retarget or child inventory gap is permanent for upgraded users (no remote DB repair). Plan 01’s low confidence estimate is appropriate; device backup-first UAT in 18.2-10 is essential, not optional.

- **Bind lacks symmetric post-commit proactive refresh (Unbind-only lifecycle effects).** Plan 06 creates `unbindWithLifecycleEffects` with `reconcileSchedule` + widget refresh; Plan 08 Task 3 routes Unbind through it but leaves Bind on the raw DAO. Profile already treats notification reconciliation as immediate after snooze (`src/screens/ContactProfileScreen.tsx:378-381`), so Bind restoring cadence eligibility likely needs the same post-commit path. Without it, decay re-arming and widget favourite restoration may wait until next foreground launch-sweep (`notification-schedule.ts:516-525`), producing stale proactive surfaces after an in-session Bind.

### MEDIUM

- **Plan 09 Task 2 leaves merge-conflict resolution unspecified.** The plan requires a pre-transaction merge-planning step for trigger-conflicting cadence but lists “reject, skip, or modified-at rule” without choosing one. Given the v11 one-way trigger (Plan 01) and existing merge-by-`modified_at` semantics in restore, executors may diverge. This should be decided before implementation, not at coding time.

- **Contacted Unbound status leakage until Plan 03 lands.** `getContactStatus` only guards `last_contact IS NULL` (`src/db/contact-status-read.ts:70-80`); a contacted Unbound contact with dormant positive cadence would receive real status/progress today because `PROGRESS_SQL` divides by `interval_days` unconditionally (`src/db/status.ts:59`). Plan 03 owns the fix; wave-2/3 ordering must not allow UI plans (07/08) to ship before 03’s contact-status-read changes, or profile may briefly show Bound chrome for Unbound rows mid-phase.

- **TypeScript contracts for nullable cadence are not centrally owned.** `ContactEditRow.interval_days` is typed `number` (`src/db/contact-read.ts:126`); `CreateContactFullInput.intervalDays` is `number` (`src/db/contacts-dao.ts:87`). Plans 03 and 08 both touch this but neither names a single “nullable cadence type sweep” task. Risk of partial updates leaving TS lies that contradict v11 DDL.

- **`reserved-columns.ts` still lists retired scalar columns.** `phone` and `email` remain in `CONTACTS_COLUMNS` (`src/db/reserved-columns.ts:37-38`) despite v9 dropping those columns from `contacts`. Plan 01 addresses removal, but until then the drift guard at `reserved-columns.test.ts:36` only chains through v6 — it would not catch a collision against the live v10 shape.

- **Compose entry for Unbound is unplanned and untested.** Superseded 18-06 included Compose changes; current 18.2-08 does not. `ContactProfileScreen.tsx:842` still navigates to Compose unconditionally. Widget Compose correctly fails closed (Plan 06), but there is no 18.2-10 UAT/backstop asserting profile-initiated Compose remains available for Unbound (explicit person-level work per dossier Cluster N). Likely intentional, but currently implicit.

### LOW

- **Plan 01 copy error in reversibility note.** Task 1 `<reversibility>` says “Migration 010 changes shipped SQLite table shape” but the task implements 011. Cosmetic, but could confuse an executor mid-migration.

- **Plan 08 alone runs `npx tsc --noEmit` early** while other plans defer the repo-wide gate to 18.2-10. Minor inconsistency; not harmful if 08 runs late enough that upstream types exist.

- **Plan 09 still carries “consider fold into 18.2-02” re-plan flag.** Owner kept it separate (correct given v3 scope); the flag can be struck to avoid executor hesitation.

- **`18.2-CONTEXT.md:13` still says “ships the v10 pure-shape migration”** while plans correctly say v11. Context doc drift outside the plan bodies; does not affect execution if plans are followed.

## Suggestions

- **Add `bindWithLifecycleEffects` (or extend the existing owner) in Plan 06** mirroring Unbind: DAO success → `reconcileSchedule` → `notifyWidgetDataChanged`, with the same failure-isolation contract. Wire Plan 08 Task 3 Bind dispatch through it. Evidence base: `ContactProfileScreen.tsx:378-381` snooze pattern and `notification-schedule.ts:495` full-set reconcile semantics.

- **Lock the backup merge-conflict rule in Plan 09 Task 2 before execution.** Recommend documenting the chosen rule in the plan action (e.g., reject merge when backup cadence would clear a locally newer positive cadence) and adding a named test case — avoids mid-implementation product calls.

- **Add one explicit “nullable cadence type sweep” bullet to Plan 03 or 08** covering `ContactEditRow`, `CreateContactFullInput`, `UpdateContactFullInput`, and `DashboardRow` so TS and SQL agree after v11.

- **Plan 01: fix the Migration 010 → 011 reversibility typo** and add an explicit assertion that `contact_methods`/`external_contact_links`/`contact_method_provenance` FK actions (`ON DELETE CASCADE` at `009-contact-method-normalization.ts:246-247`) survive rebuild unchanged.

- **Plan 10: add one manual/automated backstop** for “Profile Compose reachable for live Unbound contact” to make Cluster N explicit-person-level behavior testable, distinct from widget Compose fail-closed.

- **Plan 03: consider documenting the dormant-cadence Unbound + contacted case** in acceptance criteria — the most subtle STATUS_SQL hazard (`interval_days` positive but `tracking_enabled = 0`) — since `last_contact IS NOT NULL` alone is insufficient (`contact-status-read.ts:70-80`).
