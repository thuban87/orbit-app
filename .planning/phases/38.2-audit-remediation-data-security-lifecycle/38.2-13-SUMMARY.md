# Phase 38.2 Plan 13: Import Consent, Photo Retry and Purge Retirement Summary

Committed imports now keep unfinished photos retryable across relaunch, purge removes their sensitive workflow copies, and newly imported notes remain outside AI context until the user opts in per item.

## Accomplishments

- Photo-only Retry uses the canonical path lock and rechecks contact UID, row ownership, photo nullness and generation before writing. Publication and staging retirement share one transaction; a refused publication enqueues and drains a reference-protected delete intent.
- Consolidation and linking retire unused staged photos after commit. Complete or discarded sessions with outstanding photos surface on resume, and ImportComplete offers Retry and Skip remaining photos.
- Purge removes direct, linked, already-linked, consolidated and merged-origin workflow rows inside its transaction, scrubs pending candidate IDs, and passes staged paths to post-commit cleanup. A foreground sweep removes historical detached copies only when no external link still owns the source.
- New import-provenance Memories store `allow_ai = 0` under an enabled general default. Tests cover actual single, bulk and consolidated creation paths, AI context exclusion, per-item opt-in and a static guard over future import creators.
