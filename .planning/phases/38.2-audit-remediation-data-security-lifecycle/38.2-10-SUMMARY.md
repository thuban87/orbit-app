# Phase 38.2 Plan 10: Bounded, Cancellable Transfers Summary

Photo URL downloads now abort on every failed or abandoned path, and the Custom AI native transport bounds response bodies and settles each request once.

## Accomplishments

- `downloadImageToCache` links the caller to an internal controller and a 45-second deadline. The native no-reader fallback receives an AbortSignal, removes partial files on failure and rejects a completion racing with abort. `PhotoSourcePicker` cancels the submit on blur or unmount and cannot navigate after cancellation.
- `SecureResponseReader` limits successful bodies to 1 MiB and error bodies to 16 KiB, checks cancellation around blocking reads, and uses atomic settlement. The OkHttp call remains registered until body consumption ends. Cancel tombstones are pruned and capped.
- Native `ERR_RESPONSE_TOO_LARGE` maps to sanitized `response_too_large` and then `AiError("invalid_response")`. A debug-only synthetic probe is ready for Plan 15 wiring and Plan 16 Pixel checks.
