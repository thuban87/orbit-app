## Accomplishments

- Custom-photo targets resolve from the value's `fieldDefUid` and the incoming definition, with a local-definition fallback. - Restore captures stored and derived paths before contact, value, and definition deletion or Replace-all cascades erase them. Duplicate delete intents are idempotent. Finalize rows supersede older rows for the same canonical path, then settle under the per-path lock; cleanup rechecks the committed graph. A failed Replace-all finalize clears stale other-person bytes while retaining the retry row.
