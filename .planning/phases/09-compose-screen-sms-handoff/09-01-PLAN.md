<objective>
Land the three enabling prerequisites for the compose surface — the pure Send/Copy capability
resolver (CMP-03), the two native handoff modules (CMP-01), and the additive phone read (CMP-03) —
each independently node/typecheck-verifiable, so Plan 02 can assemble the screen with zero unknowns.

Purpose: The compose screen (Plan 02) cannot exist without (a) a deterministic, tested decision for
which control renders under each phone/SMS-capability combination, (b) `expo-sms`/`expo-clipboard`
autolinked into the build, and (c) `contacts.phone` reachable from the light header read. All three
are correctness-critical or build-critical and none has a user-facing surface, so they land first.

Output: `src/logic/compose-logic.ts` (+ test), `expo-sms`/`expo-clipboard` in the manifest, and a
`phone`-widened `getContactHeader`.
</objective>

<phase_goal>
> ROADMAP's Phase 9 `**Goal:**` line is written in outcome form, not the `As a / I want to / so that`
> user-story shape. The story below is **derived faithfully** from the phase Success Criteria and
> CMP-01/02/03 (not invented) — flagged here rather than blocking a fully-discussed, UI-spec'd phase.

**As a** user who is overdue with someone, **I want to** open one screen that shows me what to talk
about and lets me fire off a text — or copy it — in a tap, **so that** the gap between "you're
overdue with X" and the message actually being sent collapses.

This plan builds none of that surface directly; it lands the tested + native prerequisites the
Plan-02 slice consumes.
</phase_goal>
