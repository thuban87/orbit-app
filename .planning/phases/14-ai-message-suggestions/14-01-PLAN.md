<threat_model>
- Secrets: API keys never enter SQLite, logs, exports, route params, provider error display, or diagnostics.
- Egress: outbound prompt is a single immutable allowlisted projection; Custom is strict HTTPS only.
- Integrity: generation is read-only and stale/cancelled responses cannot alter drafts or records.
- Trust: first-send disclosure, every-time inspector, replacement confirmation, editable output, and no auto-send prevent delegated or covert communication.
</threat_model>
