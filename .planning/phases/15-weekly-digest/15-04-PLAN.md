## Phase Goal (user story)
**As a** person tending my relationships, **I want to** open a live "your week" screen —
who I reached, and who's quietly slipping — **so that** I get a calm retrospective the
always-current dashboard can't give me, on demand and on a schedule.

After this slice a real user can tap "Your week" on the dashboard and SEE the retrospective,
the overlooked groups, the gentle line, and the calm empty state — the phase's central
deliverable. (The notification-tap route into this screen + the Settings toggle land in
15-05, which depends on the schedule service; this slice reaches the screen via the
in-app entry, which needs nothing from 15-03/15-05.)

Purpose: DGST-02/03 become user-visible here; DGST-01's "opens a live screen" + empty state.
Output: DigestScreen.tsx, the Digest route, and the dashboard entry.

## Locked UI contract (15-UI-SPEC — do not restyle)
Section order, copy, tokens, spacing, and testIDs are LOCKED in 15-UI-SPEC. Reuse
ContactCard/Avatar for rows; the backlog nudge uses the dashboard footer-entry idiom; the
gentle line's inline names + the "+N more ->" affordance are the ONLY accent-tinted text.
No new colour, no new status colour, no animation (static content — no Skia loop).
