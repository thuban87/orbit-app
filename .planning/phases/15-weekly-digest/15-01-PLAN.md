## Phase Goal (user story)
**As a** person tending my relationships, **I want to** get one calm Sunday-morning
notification that opens a live "your week" screen — who I reached, and who's quietly
slipping — **so that** I get a scheduled retrospective the always-current dashboard
can't give me on a schedule, without being nagged.

This plan is the first slice: the reads + logic are the correctness surface the later
screen renders. They are split from the (device-UAT-only, node-untestable) screen so
every query and threshold is proven node-side — a testability constraint, not a
horizontal layer for its own sake.

Purpose: DGST-02 (retrospective + overlooked) and DGST-03 (gentle line) live or die on
choosing the RIGHT existing predicate and NOT re-deriving constants (RESEARCH Summary).
Output: `src/db/digest-read.ts`, `src/logic/digest-logic.ts`, and their vitest suites.
