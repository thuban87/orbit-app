<objective>
Close the loop: route a tapped Sunday notification into the digest screen, and add the
Settings toggle that arms/cancels the weekly trigger.

## Phase Goal (user story)
**As a** person tending my relationships, **I want to** tap the Sunday notification to land
on my "your week" screen, and turn the whole digest off if I want, **so that** the feature
is fully in my control and the notification actually goes somewhere.

This is the final wiring wave: it depends on the schedule service (15-03: reconcileDigestSchedule)
and the Digest route (15-04). After it, the notification-tap -> screen path and the Settings
toggle both work end-to-end.

Purpose: DGST-01 (independently toggleable; the tap opens the live screen). Output: the
notification-nav digest branch (+ test), the gate's param-less navigate, and the Settings row.

## Non-negotiables
- Untrusted OS input (V5): extend the resolver's narrowing to accept kind:"digest" WITHOUT
  trusting a contactId — a digest intent carries nothing to forge. Malformed -> null.
- The digest reconcile MUST live in the SHARED settings post-write path, not a one-row callback
  (review H1 / RESEARCH Pitfall 7). The shipped `persist` (:350-360) is the sole handler behind
  master ON/OFF (onToggleMaster :373/:380), the delivery-hour picker (onPickTime :397/:403), AND
  the digest toggle. Persisting the flag alone never arms/cancels the WEEKLY trigger; wiring the
  reconcile only to the digest Switch leaves master-OFF with digest:weekly still armed (a
  notification-control violation) and a delivery-hour change re-timing decay/birthday but not the
  digest until next launch. Fold reconcileDigestSchedule into `persist` so all three paths reconcile.
- Do NOT widen the shipped NotificationData interface or touch notification-actions.ts /
  notification-schedule.ts: the digest routing is handled in the resolver (which takes
  `unknown`) so the decay/birthday action + idempotency machinery is left byte-untouched
  (minimal surgical change, CLAUDE.md).
