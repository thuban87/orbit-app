<objective>
Add the interactive controls to the dashboard: the filter chip row (with category/battery chips built from the real tables), the four-option sort control, and the live name+fuel search box — all wired through `listDashboard` and the persisted `dashboard-prefs` store. This completes the dashboard's "full sort/filter/search" requirement and the search relocation (the standalone Settings search is retired in Plan 10).

Phase goal (MVP user story): **As a** person keeping up with my people, **I want to** open Orbit to a home screen showing who needs attention — sorted, filterable, searchable, with birthdays and favourites — **so that** I can see at a glance who to reach out to and act without hunting.

After this plan: a user can filter, sort, and search their contacts on the home screen, with the selection remembered across launches.
Output: `HomeScreen.tsx` with the controls layer added.

FLAGGED (owner decision A3): dashboard search widens past the never-contacted/snooze exclusions to archived-only (finds anyone non-archived, incl. never-contacted people saved via capture). The alternative is dashboard-population-only. Recommended default is the widened scope (implemented in Plan 01).
</objective>
