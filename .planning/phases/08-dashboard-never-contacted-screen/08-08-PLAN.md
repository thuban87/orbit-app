<objective>
Build the shared "Manage favourites" reorder screen and register its route. Because it adds a net-new dependency (`react-native-reorderable-list`, [ASSUMED] provenance), the plan opens with a BLOCKING owner-confirmation checkpoint; if the owner declines, the screen ships with the no-dependency up/down-arrow fallback. Either way, reorder computes via the pure `computeReorder` and persists via the transactional `rewriteFavouriteRanks`.

Phase goal (MVP user story): **As a** person keeping up with my people, **I want to** open Orbit to a home screen showing who needs attention — sorted, filterable, searchable, with birthdays and favourites — **so that** I can see at a glance who to reach out to and act without hunting.

After this plan: a user can reorder their favourites on a dedicated screen; the ordering persists.
Output: `ManageFavouritesScreen.tsx` + the ManageFavourites route.

FLAGGED (owner's bucket, risk/posture): the drag-reorder dependency. Recommended `react-native-reorderable-list@0.18.1` (peer deps satisfied by the installed reanimated 4.5.1 / gesture-handler 2.32.0); the no-dep fallback is up/down arrows.
</objective>
