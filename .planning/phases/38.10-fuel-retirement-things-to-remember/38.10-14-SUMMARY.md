# Phase 38.10 Plan 14: Add and Edit Contact inline Things to Remember sections Summary

**Add and Edit Contact now fill in Things to Remember on the form itself. An inline Off Limits list (shared D-66 field, solid "Add Off Limits Item") replaces the fuel editor. One memory draft list is split each render into Memories, Thought of Them, Gift Ideas and, on Edit Contact only, Imported from Contacts App. Last Talked About and Current Location are blank "add an entry" rows with a date capped at today. Allow AI is draft state on both forms: Edit Contact keeps provenance and lock markers, and Add Contact reads the real AI master and the per-type defaults.**
