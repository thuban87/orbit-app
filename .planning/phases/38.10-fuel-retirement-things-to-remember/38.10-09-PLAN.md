    - centeredScrollY puts the field's centre at the centre of [viewportTop, min(viewportBottom, keyboardTop)] and clamps to [0, contentH + spacer − viewportHeight].
    - A top field clamps to 0.
    - A bottom field needs the spacer.
    - A keyboard already absorbed by a resize (keyboardTop ≥ viewportBottom) means a spacer of 0.
    - A field taller than the room aligns its top to viewportTop plus margin.
    - An already-centred field produces no scroll change (no-op).
    - keyboardSpacer = max(0, viewportBottom − keyboardTop).
    - normalizeEntryText strips \r and \n (pasted or hardware Enter) to a single space and trims for save.
