# Phase 38.10 Plan 13: Memory-type section editors, MemoryEditor rework and the move sheet Summary

**Memories, Thought of Them, Gift Ideas and Imported now each open one shared section editor. It deletes at once with Undo, offers Pin/Unpin with Undo on linked entries, shows hidden entries dimmed with "Show on Profile", and moves entries between the five D-84 sections through a shared sheet. That sheet warns before dropping fields and offers Undo through the move DAO. The memory editor now keeps Allow AI in its draft and saves it with Save through the `editMemory` wrapper. It also saves label-only entries, locks the Imported type and locked AI switches, and validates links.**

## Carry-in finding: soft delete of linked memories

- The screens this plan owns never soft-delete:
  - SectionEditorScreen uses `deleteMemoryNow`, a hard delete with a tombstone, which frees the link slot.
  - MemoryEditor only calls the host's `onDelete`.
- The soft-delete `deleteMemory` is still called by two screens this plan does not own: `MemoryScreen.tsx:263` (plan 17) and `ThingsToRememberScreen.tsx:361` (plan 18; reachable until plan 16 rewires the Profile pencil). Both list linked memories.
- So until plans 16, 17 and 18 land, a linked memory can still be trashed from those two screens. Plans 10 and 12 described the symptoms:
  - the link slot stays held (`LinkedSectionTakenError`);
  - clearing that note shows the fallback refusal.
- Nothing leaks: AI eligibility requires `deleted_at IS NULL`.
- Since the button now reads "Delete" (D-69), those two screens briefly show "Delete" but snackbar "Moved to Recently Deleted". This is wording only, and it lasts until they are rebuilt.
