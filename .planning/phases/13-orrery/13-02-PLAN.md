## Assumptions surfaced for plan-review (do not silently resolve)

- **A2 (angle-map shape):** `progressToAngle` ships the linear full-orbit map (`(progress mod 1) × 2π`, split at 0.5 = 6 o'clock) — HANDOFF §7's stated default. The "top arc first-half / bottom arc second-half" phrasing also admits a non-linear map; keep `progressToAngle` a single trivially-swappable pure fn so a device-UAT tuning call (13-08) can replace only it. Owner may prefer a top/bottom-emphasis curve. Rejecting A2 on device is therefore a bounded single-function tuning edit (swap `progressToAngle`'s body only — no change to its signature, callers, geometry constants, render, or tests); it is not a structural change and does not reopen this phase.
- **A3 (drift curve):** drift grows linearly across the decay band and sits flat-maximum for rogue — a reasonable reading of "drifts further, rogue furthest". Only the `DRIFT_MAX` clamp is load-bearing; the curve is a top-of-file tunable.
