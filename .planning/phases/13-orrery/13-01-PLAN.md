<objective>
Migration 003 adds the two app-level sun columns to the single-row `app_settings` table, and the app-settings DAO is widened to read/write/validate them. This is the storage half of the assignable-sun (ORR-06) and self-sun-colour (ORR-05) features. It is the first schema change since migration 002 and is irreversible on real devices.

Purpose: Give the orrery a persistent home for "who is the sun" (`sun_contact_id`, NULL = self) and "what colour is my star" (`self_sun_colour`, NULL = resolve to `starPalette[0]` at read). App-level state, NOT a per-contact column.
Output: `migrations/003-orrery-settings.ts`, registration in `database.ts`, widened `app-settings-dao.ts`, and node:sqlite tests proving both migration paths and the DAO round-trip + validation.
</objective>
