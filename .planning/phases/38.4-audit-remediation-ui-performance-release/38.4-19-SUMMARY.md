# Phase 38.4 Plan 19: Contacts freeze (G2) + displayed-count header (D-55) Summary

**The Contacts "freeze" was the universal FAB's full-screen dial container coming back from Fabric view flattening without `pointerEvents="box-none"` after every dial close. From then on it swallowed every touch below the FAB until a cold start. `collapsable={false}` on the static box-none shell overlays fixes it, pinned by an AST contract. The Contacts header now counts the contacts each view displays (D-55).**

## Accomplishments

- **Reproduced and classified on the Pixel 3a (Task 1).**
  - The Contacts controls alone never wedged: 15+ population/filter/sort cycles in List and Card, both dismissals, rapid and double toggles, control switching, and a toggle during a refresh read. The JS thread spends about 1–2 s per committed toggle on the debug build, then goes idle.
  - The real wedge: open the FAB dial once, close it, and everything except the FAB is dead. The JS thread was idle, the focus window was Orbit, no extra window existed and the input queue was empty.
  - Temporary Fast Refresh probes (removed, never committed) showed every dead tap targeting tag 200, the dial container. `dumpsys activity top` showed that view absent while the dial is open and re-created as a new object on close.
  - Mechanism: `pointerEvents: BoxNone` does not form a view (`ViewShadowNode.cpp`). The re-created `ReactViewGroup` defaults to AUTO (`ReactViewGroup.kt:181`).
- **Excluded the false freezes.**
  - The debug LogBox "Open debugger to view warnings." toast is present from every cold start and eats every tab tap until dismissed with ✕. It is real, but debug-only and not caused by the controls.
  - Google Lens was opened once by a stray system gesture.
  - Small-target misses were ruled out by retrying each dead control at its centre with both input sources.
- **Fixed behind a RED contract (Task 2).** `collapsable={false}` on the dial container, the Snackbar root and the AssistBanner root. The same session's device check showed one native dial container across closed → open → closed, and no wedge.
- **D-55 (Task 3).** `dashboardHeaderCount` counts the displayed List or Card rows, including the frozen subset in selection mode. It is hidden on error, during the initial skeleton and at zero rows. HomeScreen's shared `listHeader` renders `dashboardHeaderCountLabel`. The empty state still reads `counts.live`, and `countLiveContacts(exec)` is still called once.
- **Re-verified on the device (Task 4).**
  - 6/6 passing repetitions each for population, filter and sort, with a tab switch after each, 3 scrim and 3 Back dismissals, and the JS thread at 0.0 % afterwards.
  - 5/5 FAB dial open/close runs followed by a control repetition.
  - E1: no List/Card divergence. D-55 holds in both views for a population, two filters and a search, and the count is hidden at zero.
  - E: SAME CAUSE. Reproduced before the fix after a Quick Log; not reproduced after it, on the warm deep link or the Quick Log.
