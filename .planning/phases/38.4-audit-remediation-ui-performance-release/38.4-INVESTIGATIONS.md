**Code under test.** `src/components/UniversalFab.tsx` at `07d3f54` (glyph fix only). The scrim `AnimatedPressable` ("Dismiss capture actions") and six `UniversalFabActionRow`s are always mounted. Invocation while closed:
- Touch: taps on the open-state and collapsed row positions did nothing; the screen stayed on Digest. `pointerEvents` does block touch.
- Keyboard: **ENTER on the TAB-focused hidden "Update Contact" row invoked it.** The "Choose contact" picker opened while the dial was closed (`rg039-closed-enter-on-hidden-row.xml`). It was closed with one Back.

### Outcome: **CONFIRMED**

With the dial closed, the collapsed action rows and the scrim are traversable (7 invisible keyboard focus stops) and invokable (ENTER fires a hidden action). `pointerEvents` gates touch only, and opacity 0 hides the rows from the accessibility-node dump but not from focus or activation. The FAB also exposes no expanded/collapsed state. This is sufficient evidence for the RG-039 remediation in Task 2.

What was not observed: TalkBack swipe traversal. Injected swipes bypass touch exploration (38.3 UAT-020b), so whether TalkBack itself lands on the alpha-0 rows is left to the owner check below.

**Owner TalkBack human-check (Plan 17):** with TalkBack on and the dial closed, swipe right from the tab bar through the FAB. Traversal must never land on a FAB action ("Add Contact" … "Memory") or on "Dismiss capture actions". Open the dial: all six actions must be reachable, and the FAB must announce expanded/collapsed.
