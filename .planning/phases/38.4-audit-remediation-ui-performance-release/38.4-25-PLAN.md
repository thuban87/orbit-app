<objective>
Two FAB rulings from the 2026-09-27 accounting list.

**1. D-52 (`OA-E3`): the FAB covers the last item.**
- The owner saw AI Data Permissions' "No information matches these filters." hidden under the floating +.
- Verified at planning:
  - The shared clearance already exists: `useBottomClearance()` in `src/navigation/use-bottom-clearance.ts` returns the measured tab bar + `FAB_SIZE` (56) + `FAB_EDGE_GAP` (16). It is documented in `app-shell.md` (Key Files and the tunables row) and used by Home, CardGrid, Backup and the Settings hub and children.
  - The FAB is hidden only on `FOCUSED_WORKFLOW_ROUTES` (`focused-route-classification.ts`; `UniversalFab` `hidden`). Every other route shows it.
  - At planning, 22 FAB-bearing screens with a scroll container did not use the clearance (the files_modified sweep list). `OrreryScreen` has no scroll container (its canvas owns its own overlays).
- Fix: apply the one shared clearance everywhere, and make it enforceable by a contract derived from the route registrations.

**2. D-45 (`OA-C2`): the FAB merges with Backup settings' accent buttons.**
- `BackupSettingsScreen.tsx` has several accent-filled controls (~:585, ~:673, ~:806). The FAB is also an accent disk (`UniversalFab.tsx` `backgroundColor: colors.accent` ~:456).
- Owner: "just give the FAB a border on this page."
- Fix: a route-keyed border, `onAccent` at 2dp, on that route only. The colour is a planner pick: `onAccent` on `accent` is the ADR-084 pair `accents.test.ts` validates for every curated accent, so the ring separates the FAB from any accent fill under it. The owner reviews the look on the device (Plan 17).

This plan runs in wave 7, after every other code plan, because it touches hosts that Plans 15, 16, 20 and 21 edit. Plan against the dossier (D-01); cite D-52 / D-45 and `OA-E3` / `OA-C2` in commits (D-02).
</objective>
