# Phase 38.4 Plan 18: Sheet large-text scroll (G1) plus D-42 orchestrator additions (NOT owner-approved) Summary

**At large text, compact and detail sheets now scroll their body inside the 40%/60% cap, so InteractionDetail's Edit/Delete row can be reached (D-32). The three sheets that own their own ScrollView opt out. A repo-wide contract keeps every future `<Sheet>` consumer to this rule. Added under D-42:**

- **A.** The open FAB dial also hides the undo Snackbar and the AssistBanner from accessibility.
- **B.** Profile History heatmap tap zones stop at the gap midpoint in every lens.
- **C.** `app.json` no longer says "orbit-scaffold".

## Accomplishments

- **D-32 (Task 1, tracer).** Added `SHEET_BODY_SCROLLS = { compact: true, detail: true, expanded: false }`.
  - `Sheet` takes `scrollBody` (default `true`). For compact/detail, the body is a `ScrollView` with `flexGrow: 0, flexShrink: 1`. It keeps the existing padding as `contentContainerStyle` and sets `keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"`.
  - With `scrollBody` false, the body is a `View` with `flexShrink: 1, minHeight: 0`.
  - These are unchanged: the `expanded` body, all height percentages, `overflow: "hidden"`, the handle, `edges={["bottom"]}`, `accessibilityViewIsModal`, dismissal, the `UnscopedTheme` reset, overlay-base and ConfirmDialog.
  - The tracer's `<verify>` passed end to end before any expansion task.
- **D-32 (Task 2).** `scrollBody={false}` now appears in exactly OrreryContactsSheet, DateDetailSheet (its "Log interaction" footer stays fixed below its list) and ProfileRelationshipSheets (both variants).
  - The new `sheet-consumers-contract.test.ts` scans all 21 `<Sheet>` blocks in 17 files. It reads literal and ternary variants. For ProfileLayoutEditor it looks only at the non-expanded branch, where the `{page === "chooser" ? (…) : (…)}` split is.
  - It checks that the opt-out set is exact, and that no FlatList/SectionList sits inside a default scroll body.
  - Mutation check: removing DateDetailSheet's opt-out fails 2 tests. The change was reverted.
- **D-42 A (Task 3).** Added `fabDialBackgroundA11y(open)`, which sets only `importantForAccessibility` and `accessibilityElementsHidden`. `Snackbar` and `AssistBanner` subscribe through `shellTransientStore(selectFabDialOpen)` above their early returns and spread the props on their `box-none` root.
  - `fab-dial-shell-overlays-contract.test.ts` classifies every `NavigationContainer` child in App.tsx. An unclassified new child fails the test.
  - It also checks that the three gates render null and no JSX, and that the two resume prompts render an RN `<Modal`.
- **D-42 B (Task 4).** Added the pure helper `cellHitSlop({ edge, gaps, target = 44 })`. Each side is `min(ceil((target − edge)/2), floor(gap/2))`.
  - ActivityHeatmap uses it with the real layout gaps: day/Month cells, Cycles cells (which previously had no slop), and Year cells, whose side facing the 8dp week boundary gets 4dp.
  - `hitSlopFor` is gone. The gap constants now drive both the layout and the slop.
  - A sweep test covers every fitted edge from 200 to 900dp.
- **D-42 C (Task 5).** `app.json` `name`/`slug` are now `"Orbit"`/`"orbit"`.
  - `npx expo config --type public --json` is byte-identical before and after.
  - Both Plan 13 introspect gates (permission set, icon wiring) print `ok`.
- **Docs.**
  - `app-shell.md`: Key Files row, D-31 section item 6, a gotcha, and 2 Changelog rows.
  - `interaction-history.md`: How It Works item 6, a gotcha, a tunables row, and 2 Changelog rows.

## Out-of-scope findings (for owner triage)

1. **The Category add/rename sheet is missing from the Plan 17 large-text device list.** The plan's consumer table called CategoryManagementScreen "expanded only". It also has a compact sheet for adding and renaming a category, which now scrolls at large text like the others. It should be on the Plan 17 font_scale 2.0 checklist. It is not added there yet, because I was told to add only the A/B rows.
2. **Three hand-built pop-ups do not use the shared Sheet**, so they do not get the large-text scroll fix:
   - the "Resume your import?" prompt
   - the "Resume your check?" prompt (contact reconcile)
   - the assist banner's "pending confirmations" sheet (this one does have its own ScrollView)

   The two resume prompts have no scrolling at all. At very large text, their buttons could be cut off the same way InteractionDetail's were. This is worth a check on the device pass.
3. **`userInterfaceStyle: "light"` in app.json is left as it is.** It tells Android to use its light native theme. This may be why system pop-ups (for example the `Alert` confirmations used by the assist banner) look light even when Orbit is in dark mode. Orbit's own light/dark switch does not read this setting. Changing it affects how native dialogs and pickers look, so it is your call, not a cleanup.
4. **A sheet that switches between `expanded` and a smaller size rebuilds its contents.** This applies to PostLogNoteEditor ("Edit Memory") and ProfileLayoutEditor (chooser to editor). Both already keep their state outside the sheet, so nothing is lost today. It is documented as a gotcha in `app-shell.md`.
