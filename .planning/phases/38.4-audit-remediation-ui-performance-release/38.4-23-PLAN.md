<objective>
Two small owner rulings from the 2026-09-27 accounting list. Both are presentation-only.

**1. D-49 (`OA-D3`; RG-034 follow-on, device-found like G1).** "Resume your import?" (`src/components/ResumeImportPrompt.tsx`) and "Resume your check?" (`src/components/ResumeReconcilePrompt.tsx`) are hand-built RN `Modal`s (Plan 18 out-of-scope finding 2).
- Verified on disk at planning: each renders a centred card (`sheet`: border, radius, padding, gap) holding a heading, a body `Text` and one or two 44–48dp `Pressable` buttons, with **no** scroll container and no height bound. At font_scale 2.0 on a narrow screen, the card can outgrow the window and push the buttons off screen.
- Fix: the same approach as Plan 11's `ConfirmDialog` (`src/components/ui/ConfirmDialog.tsx`). The heading and body go in a `ScrollView` with `flexGrow: 0, flexShrink: 1`; the card gets `flexShrink: 1`; the root is inset by the safe area. The buttons stay outside the scroll region, so they are always visible.
- The assist banner's `PendingConfirmationsSheet` already has `maxHeight: "80%"` and a `ScrollView` queue. Check that, pin it by contract, and change it only if it is not actually bounded.
- Adjacent (planner bucket, enforcing ADR-084): both Resume buttons paint their label in `colors.background` on `colors.accent`. Switch them to `colors.onAccent`, as D-20 did for the FAB glyph and Plan 05 for Backup settings.

**2. D-47 (`OA-B2`).** Hide the Profile layout editor's live preview entirely ("the layout editor preview is crap… just hide the entire preview"). Hide it, don't delete it. A top-of-file flag gates the `<ProfileLayoutPreview layout={draft} />` mount (`ProfileLayoutEditor.tsx` ~:406). The component and its RG-033 `AUD-UIA-021` geometry tests stay. Plan 17's preview-geometry device leg becomes N/A.

Plan against the dossier (D-01); cite D-49 / D-47 and `OA-D3` / `OA-B2` in commits (D-02).
</objective>
