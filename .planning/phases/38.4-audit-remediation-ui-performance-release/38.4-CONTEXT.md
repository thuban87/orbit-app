<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

Restated from the dossier and the audit synthesis — not new owner rulings. Owner rulings from the
discuss session get appended here as D-08+.

- **D-01:** The authoritative contract is the 38.4 dossier. Per-group detail is
  `docs/audits/2026-09-pre-release/synthesis/REMEDIATION-GROUPS.md` (RG sections listed above);
  finding evidence is in the linked `*/DOMAIN-AUDIT.md` packets.
- **D-02:** Preserve RG and **packet-qualified** finding IDs and each group's verification
  expectations. Bundle small fixes into coherent plans — not one plan per RG. Investigation-first
  items stay conditional.
- **D-03:** Preserve Standard/Galaxy visual authority, ADR-115 glass/translucency, protected hues
  and documented exceptions. Prefer existing shared primitives/tokens over parallel replacements.
  All colours through theme tokens (`check:colors`).
- **D-04:** Investigation gates — **RG-034** (`ui-accessibility/AUD-UIA-011`): confirm large-text
  confirmation reachability on-device before choosing a treatment. **RG-039**
  (`ui-accessibility/AUD-UIA-023`): inspect native accessibility nodes for closed/open/closing FAB
  before structural remediation; disproven → document and close.
- **D-05:** Performance claims only from physical-device measurement (never the desktop emulator);
  say which thread the evidence covers. RG-027/028 fixes must preserve correctness and transition
  behavior.
- **D-06:** Any index/schema change (RG-028) is a new forward-only migration — verify head+1 on disk
  at plan time, never a literal number (as of 2026-09-23: head 030, `TARGET_VERSION` 30).
- **D-07:** RG-038 routes explicit timestamps through the shared minute formatter established in
  38.1 (38.1 D-08). The user-choosable 12/24-hour preference remains **out of scope**.
</decisions>
