**1. D-44 (`OA-C1`; RG-008 `ui-accessibility/AUD-UIA-013` follow-on): AI Data Permissions counts reconcile with the list.**
- *Owner report:* the screen says "AI can currently access information from 1 contact · 3 items", but the list below shows several contacts, for example Abbe Vallen (1 item) and Allison Phoenix (3 items). "The numbers aren't matching the results."
- *Cause (verified on disk at planning; re-verify):*
  - `buildPermissionSummaryCopy` (`src/screens/ai-permissions-logic.ts`) computes the access line from the UNFILTERED **enabled** items. That is correct, and it is Plan 04's AUD-UIA-013 fix.
  - The list (`AIPermissionsScreen.tsx`) shows every item once "Enabled only" is off, enabled and disabled alike. The "Review existing…" link turns it off.
  - Each contact header shows only `{group.items.length} items · Review`, with no enabled count.
  - `isPermissionFilterActive` counts only type, Enabled only and search. So with type "All", no search and Enabled only off there is no "Showing N of M" line at all.
  - Nothing on screen says that the list includes items AI cannot access, so "1 contact · 3 items" looks wrong next to a longer list.
- *Fix:* the access line keeps its meaning and copy (Plan 04 / D-17). A **list line** always describes the rows below and replaces the conditional "Showing N of M" line. Each **contact header** states its AI-accessible count. Exact copy is planner discretion under D-44; the owner reviews it on the device (Plan 17).

**2. D-49 (`OA-D3`; RG-034 follow-on, device-found like G1): the resume prompts scroll their body at large text.**
- "Resume your import?" (`src/components/ResumeImportPrompt.tsx`) and "Resume your check?" (`src/components/ResumeReconcilePrompt.tsx`) are hand-built RN `Modal`s (Plan 18 out-of-scope finding 2). Each renders a centred card (`sheet`: border, radius, padding, gap) holding a heading, a body `Text` and one or two 44–48dp `Pressable` buttons, with **no** scroll container and no height bound. At font_scale 2.0 on a narrow screen the card can outgrow the window and push the buttons off screen.
- *Fix:* Plan 11's `ConfirmDialog` approach (`src/components/ui/ConfirmDialog.tsx`). The heading and body go in a `ScrollView` with `flexGrow: 0, flexShrink: 1`; the card gets `flexShrink: 1`; the root is inset by the safe area. The buttons stay outside the scroll region.
- The assist banner's `PendingConfirmationsSheet` already has `maxHeight: "80%"` and a `ScrollView` queue: check it, pin it by contract, change it only if it is not actually bounded.
