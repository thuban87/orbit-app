<objective>
Gap closure G1 (owner ruling D-32): at large text, the shared `Sheet` compact/detail variants must scroll their body so every action stays reachable. This is a **device-found follow-on to RG-034** (next to `ui-accessibility/AUD-UIA-011`, not an audit finding). Plan 11 found it while driving the RG-034 long-body confirmation on the Pixel 3a. At font_scale 2.0 (≈320dp and ≈349dp), InteractionDetail's `Sheet variant="detail"` clipped its Edit/Delete row below the sheet edge. The buttons were absent from the accessibility dump and unreachable by touch; only keyboard focus reached them (`38.4-INVESTIGATIONS.md` RG-034 side finding, `rg034-B-detail-sheet.xml`; `deferred-items.md` Plan 11).

Verified on disk (`src/components/ui/Sheet.tsx`, 104 lines):
- The sheet is a `SafeAreaView` with `maxHeight` 40%/60% (compact/detail) or `height` 92% (expanded), and `overflow: "hidden"`.
- Its body is a plain `View` with padding and no `flexShrink`. So in a max-height sheet, content taller than the cap is clipped, not scrolled. A consumer's own ScrollView inside that body is not height-bounded either, because the body grows to its content.
- The `overlayContent` comment already names the large-font clip risk.
- `sheet-contract.ts` holds the render-free variant contract (`SHEET_HEIGHT_PERCENT`, `SHEET_BODY_FLEX`).
- The Plan 11 ConfirmDialog fix is the precedent: a `ScrollView` with `flexGrow: 0, flexShrink: 1` inside a window-bounded card.
