## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the touched test files plus `npx tsc --noEmit`. Vitest passing does not mean tsc is clean.
- **After every plan wave:** the full suite command, including `check:colors`.
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** the full suite must be green, then the targeted device pass (Plan 17).
- **Max feedback latency:** about 60 s per task.
