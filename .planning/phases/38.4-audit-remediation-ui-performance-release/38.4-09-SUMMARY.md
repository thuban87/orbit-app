# Phase 38.4 Plan 09: Heatmap fit-to-width and layout-preview geometry Summary

**The Your Week and Profile History day/cycle heatmaps now size their cells to the width they actually have, and center. The Profile layout preview packs the same rows as the real Relationship Overview, using the Profile's own width, and draws them with the overview's column and gap formulas. The layout switch now follows the theme (RG-033 `ui-accessibility/AUD-UIA-010`, `AUD-UIA-021`; RG-030 `ui-accessibility/AUD-UIA-003`; D-13).**

## Accomplishments

- **Your Week (Digest).** The fixed `CELL_EDGE = 44` is gone. A full-width wrapper measures the available width with `onLayout`. `fitHeatmapCell` then gives `min(56, floor((w − 24) / 7))` whole-dp cells, and the grid is centered. Worked examples: a 320dp window gives 37dp cells, 360 gives 43, 411 gives 50, and wide screens are capped at 56. A pure sweep test proves no overflow at any width from 200 to 900. Nothing renders until the width is measured.
- **Profile History.** The 7 Days/Month lens (7 columns, cap 38) and the Cycles lens (5 columns, cap 52) fit and center. At a 320dp window the section is 254dp wide, which gives 32dp day cells and 47dp cycle cells. The Year lens is unchanged: `yearCellEdge: 13` still applies at every width.
- **Layout preview.** The preview now packs against `profileOverviewWidthBasis(window) = window − 66`. That 66dp is the full inset chain: `ContactProfileScreen` content padding, `ProfileSection` body padding and the `GlassSurface` border. So its column count, row membership and spans match the real overview for compact, auto (factory) and wide layouts at 320, 360 and 411dp. At 360dp the Profile overview is 294dp wide, below the 296dp needed for two columns, so both the real overview and the preview show one column; the old `window − 48` basis showed two. Tiles are sized from the preview card's own measured width with `overviewTileWidth` / `overviewColumnWidth` and `SPACING.sm` gaps. `flexBasis` percentages and the `SPACING.xs` gaps are gone.
- **Shared geometry.** `RelationshipOverview` now uses the same helpers, with no change in behaviour. A test reads the three inset style sites and fails if any of them drifts from the 66dp constant.
- **Themed switch.** The ProfileLayoutEditor switch now uses `trackColor={{ false: colors.border, true: colors.accent }}` and `thumbColor={colors.surfaceElevated}`.
- **Docs.** `digest.md` (Your Week sizing, tunables, gotcha 8 rewritten), `interaction-history.md` (lens fit, Year unchanged, tunables) and `profile.md` (the "Layout preview geometry" section and gotcha 19). Each has a Changelog row.
