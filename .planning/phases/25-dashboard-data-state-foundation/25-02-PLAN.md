Purpose: This is the eligible-universe half of the shared query contract the Control Surface (26), List (27), and Card (28) bind to. It is the highest-value expansion off the proven schema→store→query slice.

Output: `dashboard-query-logic.ts` grows the population predicate model + OR-union composition + per-population Default resolution; `dashboard-read.ts`'s population read grows from Active-only to the full deduped OR-union.
</objective>
