# Phase 18.1 Plan 01: Contact Method Normalization Summary

## Pre-approved v9 contracts

- **Storage:** The v9 rebuild retires `contacts.phone` and `contacts.email`; normalized, UID-bearing method/link/provenance rows are the sole authority. `interval_days` remains NOT NULL and every migrated contact remains Bound. Active external links are uniquely constrained by `(provider, external_contact_id)` while stale source evidence remains non-authoritative local provenance.
- **Phone actionability:** A parsed phone is actionable only when `isValid()` confirms it matches the applicable numbering plan. Invalid or incomplete nonblank input remains stored and editable, but non-actionable.
- **Representative test matrix:** device-default national and E.164 round trips; explicit `+` international input; an extension-bearing number; and a right-length-but-invalid number retained as non-actionable.
