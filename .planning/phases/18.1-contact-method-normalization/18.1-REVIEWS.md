## Two owner decisions (applied verbatim intent)

These two findings are **owner-bucket** calls (risk/product posture and reversal of a recorded
migration policy), not planner calls. The owner decided both; the plans transcribe the decisions.

- **H4 — device-region parsing (REPLACES "raw-only, no assumed region").** National-format legacy
  phone numbers parse using the effective default region (device region via `expo-localization`,
  overridable by `phone_region_override`), canonicalized to E.164 when parseable; `isValid()`
  (already owner-chosen in 18.1-01 Task 1) gates actionability. Numbers that fail validation under
  the effective region remain **stored** but non-actionable (never discarded). Owner: existing
  contacts must not silently lose Text until re-edited.
- **M1 — clamp + snapshot, keep running (REPLACES "reject atomically and leave user_version at v8").**
  A malformed `interval_days` (0, negative, or non-integer — reachable only via a prior restore bug)
  is **clamped** to a safe default at migration time, with the **original value snapshotted to
  `field_history`** in the same transaction; the migration continues and the app opens normally.
  Atomic rollback still applies to genuinely unrecoverable errors. Owner: do not permanently wedge
  an install over recoverable bad data.

## Owner decision (cycle 2, applied verbatim)

- **HIGH-5 — record the canonicalization region (risk-posture call).** The owner's cycle-1 decision
  (device-region parsing) canonicalizes national numbers under the device region and marks valid ones
  actionable. Cycle 2 surfaced that a *wrong* device region produces permanently wrong **actionable**
  numbers with no bulk remedy, and "canonical identity is never rewritten" means changing the region
  afterward does not repair them. Owner decision: keep device-region canonicalization and accept the
  residual wrong-actionable risk, AND **persist the region each number was canonicalized under** so a
  future explicit re-derivation is possible. Implemented as a `canonical_region TEXT` column on the
  normalized method row in the v9 migration (NULL for non-canonicalized/raw methods). Canonical
  identity is never auto-rewritten; re-derivation is a future explicit action.
