## RE-CUT NOTE

Re-cut from `.planning/phases/18-contact-data-normalization/18-01-PLAN.md` (Tasks 1, 2, and 3). This plan is the **method half** of the original 18-01: the data-bearing v9 contacts rebuild that retires scalar phone/email into normalized method/link/provenance rows, plus the pure phone/email normalizer.

**Stripped away to 18.2-01 (v10 pure-shape migration):** the entire Bound/Unbound lifecycle schema — nullable `interval_days`, the `tracking_enabled` column, both cadence CHECK constraints, the one-way `BEFORE UPDATE OF interval_days` trigger (finding **H3**), the `include_unbound_never_contacted`/`birthday_unbound_enabled` app_settings columns, and the `reserved-columns` update for `tracking_enabled`.

**Straddling finding ownership:** this plan **keeps C3-H2's method/child preservation proof** (FK-safe parent/child rebuild, every custom_field_values row and the Orrery sun reference preserved). The **app_settings-lifecycle-column half of C3-H2** (preservation proof for the two Unbound settings columns) plus **H3** (cadence CHECK/trigger DDL) both **moved to 18.2-01**.

`phone_region_override` is a method-parsing setting and is **kept here** — it is added to app_settings by the v9 migration. In v9 `interval_days` stays NOT NULL and there is no explicit `tracking_enabled` column, so all migrated contacts are implicitly Bound; the explicit lifecycle flag arrives with 18.2's v10.
