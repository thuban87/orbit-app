## Phase Goal

**As a** person keeping people in orbit, **I want to** see my favourites on a home-screen widget with their status colours and act on them in one tap, **so that** I can mark contact or open someone without launching the app.

> MVP slice: the two node-testable correctness cores that the widget's render (12-05) and navigation (12-07) consume — the tile-data shaper and the strict `orbit://` deep-link resolver. Both are pure and unit-tested here, ahead of the native surface. Neither imports the widget library.

<objective>
Build the two pure, node-testable cores of the widget's data + navigation spine: (1) the favourites tile shaper that reads the existing `listDashboard({filter:'favourites'})` projection and maps rows to widget tiles (truncating by rank), and (2) the strict `orbit://` URI resolver plus its ready-gated navigation gate.

Purpose: WDG-01 needs favourites-with-status-and-fuel without re-deriving status (avoiding the never-contacted='stable' HIGH-1 bug); WDG-02/03 need "Back → dashboard" JS navigation and V5-hardened handling of OS-delivered deep-link intents.
Output: `widget-data.ts` (shaper + reader) and `widget-linking.ts` (resolver + gate), each with its VALIDATION.md Wave-0 test file green.
</objective>
