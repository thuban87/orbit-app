## Phase Goal

**As a** person keeping people in orbit, **I want to** see my favourites on a home-screen widget with their status colours and act on them in one tap, **so that** I can mark contact or open someone without launching the app.

> Final MVP slice: make the widget real on the device. Prebuild the first custom dev client on the desktop, harden the regenerated manifest, add the boot/force-stop re-push receiver, then verify the device-only behaviours on the physical Pixel — including the killed-app headless mark bundled with the deferred Phase-11 check.

</objective>
