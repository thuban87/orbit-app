## Phase Goal

**As a** person keeping people in orbit, **I want to** see my favourites on a home-screen widget with their status colours and act on them in one tap, **so that** I can mark contact or open someone without launching the app.

> MVP slice: wire the widget's headless brain — the task handler that turns an OS click into a mark or a re-render, the event-push freshness wrapper, and the library entry registration. After this slice the widget is functionally complete in JS; the device build + boot receiver + UATs are 12-08.

<objective>
Register the widget's headless task handler and its freshness wrapper: route WIDGET_CLICK marks and render events, push event-driven updates, and register the foreground launch recompute.

Purpose: WDG-02 needs the headless mark handler; WDG-03 needs event-push + a launch recompute (no polling). The mark case mirrors the proven Phase-11 notification-actions structure (openAndMigrate before getExecutor; Logger-guarded; 30s budget). The launch recompute is a SweepHook (foreground-only); the boot/force-stop re-push is a SEPARATE native path (12-08).
Output: `widget-refresh.ts`, `widget-task-handler.tsx`, and the `registerWidgetTaskHandler` call in `index.ts`.
</objective>
