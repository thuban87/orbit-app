## Phase Goal

**As a** person keeping people in orbit, **I want to** see my favourites on a home-screen widget with their status colours and act on them in one tap, **so that** I can mark contact or open someone without launching the app.

> MVP slice: connect the widget to the running app — mount the deep-link gate so a tile tap navigates and "Back → dashboard" works, register the launch-refresh, and give the owner the in-app "Add Orbit widget" button. After this slice the widget is fully wired in JS; only the device build (12-08) remains.

<objective>
Wire the widget into the app shell: mount the `orbit://` navigation gate, register the foreground widget refresh, wire the event-push publishers at EVERY widget-visible foreground mutation site (exhaustive inventory), and add the Settings "Add Orbit widget" button.

Purpose: WDG-02's name/chevron + Message/Log deep links and "Back → dashboard" need the gate mounted on the shared navigationRef; WDG-03's launch-refresh needs the SweepHook registered before the cold-start sweep, its event-push freshness needs notifyWidgetDataChanged() called after EVERY foreground mutation that changes a widget-visible field — not just the prior 5-site subset (codex HIGH) — and its "Add widget" affordance needs requestPinWidget with graceful fallback.
Output: App.tsx gate mount + one-shot widget-sweep registration; the notifyWidgetDataChanged() publishers wired at the log/favourite/fuel/reorder call sites; a Settings add-widget row with a unit-tested result handler.
</objective>
