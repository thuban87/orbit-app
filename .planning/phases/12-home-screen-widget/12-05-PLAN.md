## Phase Goal

**As a** person keeping people in orbit, **I want to** see my favourites on a home-screen widget with their status colours and act on them in one tap, **so that** I can mark contact or open someone without launching the app.

> MVP slice: the widget's write spine (headless mark, node-tested over node:sqlite) and its visual surface (the RemoteViews tree). After this slice, tapping a tile writes a real interaction and the tree can render favourites; making it appear on the home screen is the device build in 12-08.

<objective>
Build the headless mark-contacted write seam (node-testable) and the RemoteViews render tree (device-UAT).

Purpose: WDG-02's small-tile tap is a headless mark through the single-writer DAO; WDG-01's grid is a RemoteViews tree of status-colour base64 avatars with the two-tap-region model. The mark logic is extracted so it is exercised over the node:sqlite harness (the killed-app native round-trip is device-only, 12-08); the render imports the widget library primitives (available after 12-02).
Output: `widget-mark.ts` (+ node:sqlite test) and `widget-render.tsx`.
</objective>
