## Phase Goal

**As a** person keeping people in orbit, **I want to** see my favourites on a home-screen widget with their status colours and act on them in one tap, **so that** I can mark contact or open someone without launching the app.

> MVP note: this phase is a native Android widget; its user-visible surface is gated on a device rebuild (12-08). Per the plan-phase reconciliation, the node-testable correctness cores are built and unit-tested ahead of the native surface. THIS plan is the first real, shippable user-facing slice: it upgrades the existing dashboard so every ContactCard shows its true status colour (retiring the OD-1 opacity placeholder) and lands the shared status tokens the widget will consume.

<objective>
Introduce the shared app-wide status palette — `statusStable` / `statusWobble` / `statusDecay` (joining the existing `rogue`) — as owner-approved theme tokens, and retire ContactCard's opacity-only status-ring placeholder (OD-1) to consume them with a redundant border-weight channel.

Purpose: WDG-01 requires "each avatar carrying its status colour." The palette is a single source of truth shared by the dashboard ContactCard (now), the widget bitmap (12-03/12-05), and the Phase-13 orrery. The hexes were owner-approved on 2026-08-16 (UI-SPEC ⭐ section): `statusStable #45B98A`, `statusWobble #E8C15C`, `statusDecay #E56A52`, `rogue #E0904A` unchanged.
Output: three new theme tokens seeded in space-dark; a ContactCard that reads real colour + escalating weight per status; accessibilityLabel preserved.
</objective>
