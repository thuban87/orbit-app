<objective>
Build the conservative duplicate-evidence engine: a deterministic external-linkage bypass (owning the active-link read) followed by a pure, tunable, node-tested advisory scorer that maps to five colourless user-facing outcomes — with incoming methods canonicalized before matching so the strong signal is not silently degraded.

Purpose: This is IMP-03's core. Bulk driver (plan 06), card grid + interrupts (plan 07), and consolidation (plan 11) all consume it. Keeping it pure, heavily invariant-tested, AND canonicalizing incoming methods is what prevents a name-only auto-link, a correlated-evidence false "probable", and a missed real match on a national-format number.
Output: `duplicate-evidence.ts` with `findActiveExternalLink`, the deterministic-first classifier, and tunable weights, fully node-tested including the negative invariants and the canonicalization-before-match behavior.
</objective>
