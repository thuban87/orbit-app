# Phase 38.9 Plan 09: Shared header on the 16 pushed Contacts/Digest/Events/Orrery screens Summary

**Sixteen pushed screens now use the house header with a chevron Back (or a ✕ Cancel on Crop photo and Merge impact summary). Off Limits, Edit interaction, Edit participant, Research and Compose show the person line; Edit Contact shows only its title. The group forms and Research now show the theme art. Compose's header Back takes the same origin-aware exit as the hardware Back. Survivor select's second step is now a guarded sub-step: Back returns to the picker and clears the previous pick, and Continue only works for the pair that has actually loaded.**
