# Phase 38.9 Plan 14: Glossary Enforcement Summary

**One label per concept app-wide: "Never Contacted", "Favorite(s)", "Wobbly", "Contacts" and "Log Interaction" replace the retired forms at every user-visible site. The labels come from `src/constants/glossary.ts`, a TypeScript-AST contract keeps the old wording out of `src/` and `app.config.ts`, and `docs/systems/glossary.md` documents the terms.**

## Accomplishments

- `glossary.ts` holds the decided labels (D-21, D-41). DIGEST, CONTACTS and EVENTS are re-exported from `product-labels.ts`, which stays in place.
- `glossary-contract.test.ts` parses every production `src/**/*.ts(x)` file plus `app.config.ts`. It checks only string literals, template pieces and JsxText, under skip rules (a)–(e) and the shared whole-token matching rule. It has self-tests for each skip rule, for the template-argument `new Error`, for `super(…)` being caught and for the C3-M2 caught and skipped cases. It also has a rename guard and an identifier guard. The pending allowlist is gone; the only allowlist left is the two developer-only literals, and it fails on stale entries.
- The scan found exactly the 36 sites in the plan's context list and nothing else. All are converted:
  - **Never Contacted (D-42, D-56):** Contacts filter, card/row status labels, Digest Horizon a11y and heading, Systems builder family and option, built-in System label, Orrery label context, and the hidden-empty line "N never contacted →". "Nothing in Never Contacted." follows automatically.
  - **Wobbly (D-22):** card, row and Orrery.
  - **Favorite(s) (D-49):** filter, card a11y, row/grid a11y ("Add favorite" / "Remove favorite" / "Not favorite"), snackbar and retry, "No favorites yet", unbind confirm, widget "Choose favorites", widget picker "Orbit — Favorites" / "Your favorite people, at a glance."
  - **Contacts (D-22):** Settings "Contacts right swipe" / "What a right swipe on a contact card does.", "Reset Contacts View", "Couldn't reset Contacts", "Retry resetting the Contacts view", archive confirm, Settings hub subtitle, notifications-off helper, "Dismiss Contacts controls".
  - **Log Interaction (D-22, ADR-132):** right-swipe option label. The stored value `log-contact` is unchanged.
- Dead `FabActionPlaceholders.tsx` deleted: no importer and registered in no stack. The four phase-22 ledger stubs for it (WINDOWS #13–16) are marked fixed.
- No schema, migration, backup or identifier change (D-12). `favourites`, `OrbitFavourites` and `orbit://favourites` are asserted unchanged.
