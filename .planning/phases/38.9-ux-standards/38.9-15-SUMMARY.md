# Phase 38.9 Plan 15: Event Noun and Plain-Word Copy Summary

**Group events are now called "Event" everywhere a user sees them. The developer wording from D-41 and D-63 is rewritten in plain words: "Same as event", "Careful actions", "Deleted items kept", "reach-out reminders", "Reset Manual Changes" and "Set for this category". The glossary contract now also blocks "group event" app-wide and the developer forms in the converted files. The renamed Systems reset action shares one constant between its button and its dispatch, so the rename cannot silently break it.**

6. **Template label "Set for this contact: freeform layout".** "freeform" was not named by D-41. *Recommendation:* keep it, or "…: custom layout".
