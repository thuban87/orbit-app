## Phase Boundary

**Objective:** Give Orbit one set of shared UX rules and components, then convert the app to them
mechanically, so 38.10–38.12 and Phase 39 apply standards instead of inventing their own. Today the
app mixes native Android dialogs with themed ones, has a dozen header/Back styles, throws away typed
work on Back in several forms, writes silently, and uses several names for one concept. After this
phase: one themed dialog family, one option sheet, one header with a chevron Back, a guard on every
form, a destructive-action rule, a feedback rule, one switch, one date format, one empty state, and
one glossary.

**In scope**
- Dialogs: themed confirm / destructive confirm / one-button notice replace every native
  `Alert.alert` except the snooze Alert (D-24, D-27, D-43); one themed option sheet replaces every
  native spinner (D-28).
- Destructive-action and feedback rules written as the app standard, applied here to the mechanical
  sites (D-30, D-32, D-39, D-46).
- Unsaved-work guard on every form, sub-editor and note-holding overlay; Add Contact (E-01) and Log
  Interaction (F-01) first (D-03, D-13..D-15, D-33, D-34).
- Shared header on every pushed screen with an icon-only chevron Back and an optional person line
  (D-16, D-44, D-45); pushed screens show the theme art (D-17).
- Shell: history Back across tabs (D-35); + forms open over the current tab (D-38); ComposeResearch
  is a focused route (D-18).
- Feedback primitives: inline validation + scroll-to-error (D-19); snackbar above overlays and
  inline errors inside sheets (D-40).
- States: two-tier chips codified (D-05); one Orbit switch (D-25, D-47); disabled look (D-26).
- One date-only display formatter (D-23, D-48); one empty-state component (D-20).
- Glossary and copy: canonical terms kept (D-41), enforcements (D-22, D-42, D-49), plural helper and
  label constants (D-21), plain-word rewrites of developer wording (D-41).

**Out of scope** (named neighbours)
- Area semantics: which deletes get Undo, where flows end, snooze unification, lifecycle on the
  Profile, fuel wording — 38.10–38.12 (D-10). The snooze Alert `HomeScreen.tsx:1528` is 38.11's.
- The accessibility-label sweep (A-30) and large-text items — Phase 40 (D-02).
- App-name literals — backlog 999.4 (D-10).
- Recoverable deletes / new storage (D-31, D-12).
- Themed date/time pickers (D-29).
- + overlap mid-scroll and + over an open Contacts panel — accepted as is (D-36, D-37).
- Renaming any decided term (D-41).
- Phase 39 lesson presenter, spotlight and coordinator (39's own); 38.9 supplies the overlay-open
  signal they read (D-24).

**Preserved behavior**
- ConfirmDialog action layout and non-dismissable destructive confirms (D-06).
- One Back per screen, the header's (D-07).
- Completion destinations: Add Contact → new Profile; person actions → that Profile (D-08).
- Purge impact summary; interaction delete hard + confirmed; Unbind confirmed (D-09).
- The + visible on browse surfaces including ordinary Settings children (D-11).
- Snackbar 6 s for all (D-04).
- No schema, migration or backup-format change (D-12).

### Dialogs and option sheets
- **D-27:** Every native decision `Alert.alert` (25 sites, all two-button) becomes a themed confirm; each of the 76 error/info Alerts becomes an error snackbar where the user can carry on, or a themed one-button notice where they are blocked. No production `Alert.alert` remains except `HomeScreen.tsx:1528` (D-10). (owner, Q-01)
  - The per-site snackbar-vs-notice classification is a planning task; the inventory is in the workpaper §4.A.1.
- **D-24:** Themed confirms and notices are called imperatively (`await confirm({...})`-style) through one host mounted once in `App.tsx`; the existing declarative `ConfirmDialog` stays for its 9 consumers. Overlays (BaseOverlay sheets/dialogs, the host, and raw RN Modals moved onto BaseOverlay or registered) report "open" to a registry separate from the shell dismiss stack, so Back/tab-retap `dismissTop()` can never close a non-dismissable destructive confirm. (derived: workpaper DC-01, DC-06; ADR-086; `RootNavigator.tsx:110,170-176`)
  - This registry is the "a dialog or sheet is open" signal Phase 39 D-58/D-97 reads.
- **D-43:** Themed dialogs (confirm, destructive confirm, one-button notice, discard prompt) are the elevated card with a 2dp `borderStrong` border, matching Phase 39's lesson cards (39 D-80). (owner, Q-18 = DLG-B)
- **D-28:** One themed single-choice option sheet, built from `CategoryChoiceSheet`, is used at every list length and replaces every native `@react-native-picker/picker` site: `CreateContactScreen.tsx:483`, `EditContactScreen.tsx:1241,1329`, `TouchpointRefineForm.tsx:300,327,373`, `ContactMethodsEditor.tsx:84`, `ImportReviewScreen.tsx:504`, `BulkImportSetupScreen.tsx:590`. `DropdownFieldWidget` ("Met via") moves onto it; `CATEGORY_SEARCH_THRESHOLD` no longer switches native vs themed. (owner, Q-02)
- **D-29:** Native date and time pickers stay; no themed picker is built. (owner, Q-03)

### Destructive actions and feedback
- **D-30:** App-wide destructive-action rule: a reversible action happens at once with an Undo snackbar; an irreversible one gets the themed destructive confirm (warning glyph, non-dismissable); a destructive button is never the loudest control at rest; on list cards destructive actions sit behind ⋯. The D-09 carve-outs hold. 38.9 writes the rule and converts its mechanical sites; 38.10–38.12 and 39 apply it per action. (owner, Q-04)
- **D-31:** No new recoverable-delete storage in this phase. Today's holding zones stand: Archived contacts; memories in Recently Deleted for 30 days (ADR-089); key people soft-deleted for snackbar Undo only (`relationships-dao.ts:162,181`); custom fields with values in Quarantine for 30 days (`services/field-sweep.ts:58`). Deleting an interaction, Off Limits item, photo, System or background template stays permanent. (owner, Q-05)
- **D-32:** Under D-30, Archive, Restore and Bind act at once and show a snackbar naming the person with Undo; Unbind keeps its confirm. 38.11/38.12 wire it on their screens. (owner, Q-06)
- **D-39:** App-wide feedback rule: every write that is silent today shows a snackbar naming the person (and the channel for logs); Undo is offered where the action is reversible; tapping Undo shows its own confirming snackbar. 38.9 writes the rule; areas apply it to their writes. (owner, Q-13)
- **D-46:** Snackbar copy is verb first, then whom: "Logged Message with Ann", "Archived Ann Lee"; Undo confirms as e.g. "Log removed". (owner, Q-21 = SN-A)
- **D-40:** Validation errors inside a sheet render inline under the field (D-19 primitive), and the snackbar renders above any open sheet or dialog so nothing fired from an overlay is hidden (today `<Snackbar />` mounts at `App.tsx:441` under every RN Modal; A-13 at `HomeScreen.tsx:2214`). (owner, Q-14)
- **D-19:** One inline-validation primitive: error text under the field, cleared when the field changes, and scroll/focus to the first blocking problem on Save; Save stays pressable (CAPT-14). Fixes Add/Edit Contact's unrendered "Enter a name to save." (`src/screens/create-contact-logic.ts:152`, `edit-contact-logic.ts:267`) and the `findNodeHandle(scroll)` → `measureLayout` bug (`CreateContactScreen.tsx:256-262`, `EditContactScreen.tsx:496-502`); applied to Group Log title (F-12) and System builder name (K-08). (derived: phase-13 §AD :561-563)
