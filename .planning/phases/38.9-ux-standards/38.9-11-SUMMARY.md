# Phase 38.9 Plan 11: Orbit Switch, Swatch Ring and Chip Tiers Summary

**A custom-drawn Orbit switch (outlined borderStrong track with a small textSecondary thumb off; accent track with a large onAccent thumb and an accentText check on), animated from a Reanimated shared value, now replaces all 30 React Native switches. Selected colour swatches on Appearance get a textPrimary ring with a surface gap and a check, and the two chip tiers live in one `chipTone` helper.**

- Started about 2026-10-11T10:50Z, finished 2026-10-11T11:18Z (about 28 min)
- 3 tasks, 4 commits, 33 files (8 created, 25 modified)

## Accomplishments

- **OrbitSwitch** (`src/components/ui/OrbitSwitch.tsx`): it takes the same prop names as RN Switch, plus an optional `busy`. Pressable target is 44dp with `accessibilityRole="switch"`. When disabled it is dimmed to 0.5 (the opacity `Button.tsx` uses) and drawn Off, and presses are ignored. `accessibilityState.checked` still reports the stored value (review L3). A `busy` key appears only when busy is true (C5-M2). The thumb's size and position come from a shared value through `withTiming`, and jump straight to the end state under the OS reduced-motion setting. The `thumbBox` worklet sits above its caller.
- **Contrast gate** (`orbit-switch-logic.test.ts`), all pairs at AA_LARGE or better:
  - the off thumb against surface and background
  - the on thumb against the accent
  - the accentText check against onAccent, for every package × mode, every curated accent × mode, and every Standard Light glass accentText variant
  - Every pair passed, so there was no owner stop and no allowlist entry.
- **Sweep:** the other 29 `<Switch>` sites in 14 files now use OrbitSwitch. `trackColor`, `thumbColor` and the hand-built role/state were removed. The two Orrery satellites switches pass `busy={busy}`.
- **Contract** (`orbit-switch-contract.test.ts`): an AST scan rejects every way of reaching RN `Switch` (named or aliased import, re-export, namespace member, `require`/`import()` destructuring) and any raw `<Switch>` tag. A self-test checks the scanner. The contract also pins the shared-value animation, the absence of `useState`/`set*` in OrbitSwitch, the worklet-above-caller order, and the busy prop on both Orrery switches.
- **RG-030 contracts kept:**
  - RelationshipEditor still asserts two switch regions, `accessibilityLabel=`, the constant "Hide from Profile" text and `value={draft.hidden === 1}`.
  - ProfileLayoutEditor still asserts its accessible name and now also checks for `<OrbitSwitch` and the absence of `<Switch`.
  - Only the trackColor/thumbColor assertions were removed.
- **K-28 / D-60:** a `ColourSwatch` component on Appearance draws both rows.
  - Selected: a textPrimary ring, then a surface gap, then the fill with a check.
  - Unselected: the fill inside the `border` ring, as before.
  - Accent rows draw the check in `onAccent`. Star rows use `starSwatchCheckTone`, which picks whichever of textPrimary and background contrasts more with the star colour. Every star colour × package × mode reaches AA_LARGE.
- **D-05:** `chipTone("primary" | "sub", selected)` in `src/components/ui/chip-tone.ts` is exported from the barrel. `durationChipTone` now calls `chipTone("primary", …)`, and its existing tests pass unchanged. Other chip screens were not touched (D-01).

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Added a semantic `check` icon to the registry**

- **Commit:** 7cb16001

- **Fix:** the contract went into the Task 2 commit, so every feat commit passes the suite.
- **Commit:** 92cdd2df

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] The contract timed out under load**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** parsing every production file took more than 5 s on the loaded box.
- **Fix:** files whose text lacks "Switch" are skipped before the parse (every form the scanner catches names `Switch`), and the test has a 30 s timeout like `no-native-alert-contract`.
- **Commit:** 92cdd2df

**4. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Test mocks updated for the new component**

- **Commit:** 92cdd2df

**5. [Rule 1 - Bug] Unused `colors` and `useTheme` removed**
- **Found during:** Task 2

- **Fix:** removed the unused bindings and imports.
- **Commit:** 92cdd2df

**6. [Plan wording] Accent-swatch check reads `colors.onAccent`, not a raw `ACCENTS[id][mode].onAccent`**

The rendered colour is the same, and Icon's token-only rule stays intact.
- **Commit:** a109cb0f
