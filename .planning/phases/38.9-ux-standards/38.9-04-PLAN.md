Facts verified on disk at planning:
- RN 0.86.2, new architecture (`android/gradle.properties:38`). `ReactNativeElement.measureLayout` returns early with a dev-only console.error when its argument is not a ReactNativeElement and never calls onFail (`node_modules/react-native/src/private/webapis/dom/nodes/ReactNativeElement.js:177-203`), so today's scroll never happens. ScrollView accepts `innerViewRef` (typed `RefObject<View>`, `ScrollView.d.ts:622-626`); the inner content view is a host view with `collapsable={false}` (`ScrollView.js:1736-1748`). Measuring a section against it yields a content-space y for `scrollTo`.
- Create `revealAndFocus` :250-265 and Edit :496-502 pass `findNodeHandle(scroll)`.
