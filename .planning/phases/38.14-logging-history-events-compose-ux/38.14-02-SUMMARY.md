# Phase 38.14 Plan 02: Compose shows what it sends, keeps its actions reachable, and asks before dropping a draft Summary

**What Compose shows now matches what it sends. The mode name, the Subject, Copy subject and a "Text to +1 312 555 1234" / "Email to maya@example.com" line all come from the mode Transmit uses. Empty messages cannot be sent or copied. Copy and Transmit sit in a bar pinned above the keyboard and the gesture bar, and the message box stops growing at 8 lines and then scrolls inside itself. The labels share one scrim, and copies confirm with a snackbar. Opening Compose for someone else over a typed draft asks Discard / Keep editing, and after Keep editing the AI reads only the person on screen.**

## Accomplishments

- **Defect #8 removed (D-42).** `composeModePresentation` takes the DAO-selected actionable primaries and returns `usable`, `modeLabel`, `showSubject`, `destinationLine` and `noDestinationHint`. The Subject field and Copy subject use `showSubject`, so they agree with `onTransmit`. Both copy handlers pass `presentation.usable ?? mode` to `resolveCopyTargets`, so when Copy subject is shown it always copies. A destination-free Email draft keeps its Subject (phase-14 :134, §E). The mode switch stays and stays pressable. Its text and a11y label are still keyed on the session mode (grep count 2, R3-02).
- **D-76 / D-46.** `composeActionAvailability` disables Transmit and Copy when the body is blank, and Copy subject when the subject is blank. `onTransmit` also returns early on a blank body. The `sending` latch is kept, and Transmit keeps its label (D-18).
- **D-75.** The pure `keyboardLift` sits beside `sheetKeyboardLayout`, which is unchanged. Compose's root records the frame height and the tallest resting height. It listens to `keyboardDidShow` / `keyboardDidHide` with cleanup. It pads by the lift while the keyboard is up and by `useFocusedBottomInset()` otherwise. Copy · Transmit (`compose-action-bar`) now sit after the ScrollView, and the header stays first. The message box is still a plain multiline `TextInput`, so typed line breaks survive. It has `maxHeight: composeDraftMaxHeight(fontScale)` and `scrollEnabled`.
- **D-44.** The seven label `ChromeScrim`s are gone. ONE `ChromeScrim` with the unchanged opacity token and radius `RADII.sm` backs the whole content column inside the ScrollView. The count is pinned to 1. No label needed an exception.
- **D-45.** `copyFeedback`, `copyTimer`, `flashCopyFeedback` and the inline `compose-copied` text are removed. Both copies now confirm with `showSnackbar({ kind: "success", label: "Message copied" | "Subject copied" })`.
- **D-72.** The load focus effect computes `freshSession` first, then checks `shouldPromptDraftSwitch`. When it is true, the effect awaits `confirmDiscard()` before `startSession`. Discard runs the load. Keep editing calls `navigation.setParams({ contactId: storedContactId, origin: undefined })`, and both paths respect `cancelled`.
- **R4-01 (AI egress).** `contactIdRef` is assigned on every render. `resolvePrompt` snapshots `requestContactId = contactIdRef.current` once and uses it for both `readPromptContext` and `readComposeResearch`. Only the focus effect's Research count still reads `contactId` directly (count 1). Nothing new is transmitted, and egress is narrowed to the person on screen.
- **D-42 Edit route.** `EditContactParams.focusSection?: "methods"`. Edit Contact expands Contact Methods once the form has hydrated and reveals its section once through `useFirstErrorReveal`'s `reveal`, guarded by a ref. Compose's "Add a phone number or email" passes `focusSection: "methods"`.

**2. [Rule 2 - Correctness] The "Review what is shared" modal moved out of the column scrim**
- **Found during:** Task 2

- **Fix:** The trigger button stays in the column. The modal element renders at the root, outside the scrim, under the same visibility condition. The ChromeScrim count is unaffected.
- **Commit:** `e7adddd1`
