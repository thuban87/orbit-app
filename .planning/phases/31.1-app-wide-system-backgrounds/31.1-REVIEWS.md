### 3. Concerns

#### HIGH

- **[HIGH] `ArchivedContactsScreen` will not reveal the background under the plan as written.**
  `src/screens/ArchivedContactsScreen.tsx` paints `colors.background` twice: the outer root `View`
  (line 184, `{ flex:1, backgroundColor: colors.background }`) AND an inner full-bleed `ScrollView`
  (line 188, `style={{ backgroundColor: colors.background }}`) that fills the entire content area
  below the app bar. Plan 03 Task 1 instructs "remove ONLY the inline `{ backgroundColor:
  colors.background }` from the OUTERMOST root View" and its acceptance criterion is "git diff shows
  only the outermost-root fill removed per file / Nested surface fills in each file are unchanged."
  Following that leaves line 188 opaque, so the shell background is fully occluded on a
  **presentation** screen — a direct failure of UAT row 1 ("Archived … System background clearly
  visible") and THEME-04. The correct fix removes the fill from **both** 184 and 188, which the
  stated acceptance criterion would flag as a violation. The "outermost root only" mental model is
  the root cause; a full-bleed content `ScrollView` is a page-wash, not a "semantic surface," and
  must also be flipped.

#### MEDIUM

- **[MEDIUM] Multi-state-root screens are under-specified; loading/error/empty states stay opaque.**
  Many files have 2–3 sibling full-page roots, one per render branch, each with `colors.background`:
  `ImportCompleteScreen.tsx:110/120/134` (loading / error / loaded),
  `ReconcileDetailScreen.tsx:142/143/144`, `MergeConflictsScreen.tsx:161/162/169`,
  `SurvivorSelectScreen.tsx:38/39`, `ReconcileCompleteScreen.tsx:37/38`,
  `CaptureScreen.tsx:550(errorRoot)/746(main)`, `EditContactScreen.tsx:551(loading)/569(main)`,
  `RestorePreviewScreen.tsx:168/181` (two ScrollView state-roots). "Remove only the outermost root"
  and "git diff shows only the outermost-root fill removed per file" are literally wrong here —
  there is no single outermost root. Following it flips one branch and leaves the others as opaque
  washes, so the same screen reveals art in one state and hides it in another. Impact is lower than
  the Archived case (these are dense, where a dense scrim ≈ opaque), but the acceptance criterion
  should not forbid the correct multi-branch fix.

- **[MEDIUM] Tab-based Orrery exclusion over-suppresses the background on non-Orrery screens.**
  Plan 01 sets `slotId={activeTab === "OrreryTab" ? "none" : undefined}`. But `OrreryStack`
  (`src/navigation/tabs/OrreryStack.tsx`) registers 12 screens, including the **presentation
  browse** screens `ThingsToRemember`, `RecentlyDeleted`, and `MemoryHistory` (all also registered
  in `DashboardStack`). Reached via the Dashboard tab they reveal the art; reached via the Orrery
  tab the shell host is forced to `none`, so the identical screen shows a solid background — an
  inconsistent reveal for the same route depending on entry tab, and arguably a D-04 miss ("behind
  every ordinary in-app page"). UAT row 6 wants the whole Orrery tab image-free
  (SystemBuilder/SystemsManagement over the orrery), so the tab-based choice is *internally*
  consistent with that row — but it collides with D-04 for the browse children. This is a
  product/taste call (owner's bucket): confirm whether the guard should be **route-based**
  (`routeName === "Orrery"`, which the focused-route store already tracks) so only the canvas is
  excluded and child browse screens still reveal art. Recommend surfacing to the owner rather than
  silently shipping either interpretation.

- **[MEDIUM] Automated gates cannot catch a wrong-line removal; the only net is human reading + Plan 04.**
  `tsc --noEmit` and `check:colors` both pass whether you remove a page-wash or a nested input's
  `backgroundColor: colors.background` (e.g. `MemoryHistoryScreen.tsx:155/181` inputs,
  `BulkReviewScreen.tsx:182` input, `BackupScreen.tsx:313` passphrase input, `SettingsScreen.tsx`
  has 8 occurrences of the token). With ~13 multi-occurrence files, the risk of flipping the wrong
  line (breaking an input's fill, or missing a wash) is real and invisible to the per-task verify.
  Recommend the executor enumerate every `colors.background` line per file and classify each
  (page-wash → remove; discrete surface/input → keep) before editing, and record that list in the
  summary.

#### LOW

- **[LOW] Plan 03 can be marked "done" while the feature is broken.** Because Plan 03's automated
  verify (tsc + check:colors) and its stated acceptance ("only the outermost-root fill removed")
  both pass even when Archived doesn't reveal and multi-root states stay opaque, the defect only
  surfaces at Plan 04's device UAT — a wasted plan→execute→review cycle. Tighten Plan 03's
  acceptance to a device/visual outcome ("each cooperating screen reveals the background with no
  opaque full-page wash across its loading/empty/loaded states") instead of a diff-shape assertion.

- **[LOW] RESEARCH's enumerated 34-screen list is inaccurate (harmless).** It lists `SystemBuilder`,
  which does **not** paint `colors.background` at root (the live grep excludes it), and is
  off-by-one. Plan 03 re-runs the grep at execution time so this does not propagate, but it shows
  the artifact's screen list was not fully reconciled with disk.

- **[LOW] Confirm an opaque themed background sits behind the transparent `NavigationContainer`.**
  With `theme.colors.background = transparent` (App.tsx:364) the container root no longer paints;
  the shell `BackgroundHost` covers `RootNavigator`, but during the `animation: "fade"` tab
  transition (`RootNavigator.tsx:185`) or before the host lays out, any uncovered instant would show
  the native window color. This is exactly Pitfall 2 (already device-gated), but a defensive opaque
  themed background on the app's outermost view (GestureHandlerRootView/SafeAreaProvider wrapper)
  would remove the flash risk regardless of how react-native-screens composites.

### 4. Suggestions

1. **Reframe Plan 03 around "remove every full-page wash," not "remove one outermost root."** For
   each cooperating screen, strip `colors.background` from the root **and** any full-bleed content
   layer (a `ScrollView`/`FlatList`/`View` that fills the scene) **and** every render-branch
   state-root, while preserving discrete surfaces (cards, rows, inputs, sheets, dialogs,
   snackbars, loading spinners). Replace the diff-shape acceptance with a reveal outcome.
2. **Fix `ArchivedContactsScreen` explicitly:** flip both line 184 and line 188.
3. **Decide Orrery exclusion granularity with the owner.** If
   ThingsToRemember/RecentlyDeleted/MemoryHistory should reveal art regardless of tab, key the
   guard on `routeName === "Orrery"` (route-based) rather than `activeTab === "OrreryTab"`
   (tab-based); the OrreryScreen opaque root already guarantees no bleed on the canvas, and the
   route-based guard still fires the overdraw optimization on the Orrery leaf.
4. **Add the optional route-coverage unit test** (RESEARCH Pitfall 4): assert every registered
   stack route resolves to its UI-SPEC density tier, so the map cannot silently drift from the
   stacks as screens are added.
5. **Have the executor emit, per file, the classified list of `colors.background` occurrences**
   (wash vs surface) in the plan summary, making the "review the code, not the diff" step auditable
   rather than trusted.
6. **Keep the pre-existing A1 (Android native-stack transparency) device proof as the first thing
   Plan 01 validates on the Pixel** — that ordering is already correct and is the single genuine
   architectural unknown; nothing above changes it.

### 5. Risk Assessment

**Overall: MEDIUM.**

The data/store/DAO/precedence layers are correct and low-risk — the setter mirror, the validator,
the density-map keys, the Profile fallback, and the Orrery opaque root were verified against source,
and no decision reversal and no schema/network/local-first violation were found. The risk is
concentrated in **Plan 03's screen-flip mechanics**: the "one outermost root per file" model is
wrong for ~13 of 31 files, and for `ArchivedContactsScreen` it produces an outright reveal failure
on a presentation screen that the plan's own acceptance criterion would enforce as "correct."
Combined with the device-only Android transparency unknown (A1), these are all catchable at Plan
04's UAT but would cost cycles and, in the Orrery-tab case, embed a product decision (tab-vs-route
exclusion) that belongs to the owner. With the Plan 03 reframing (remove all page-washes;
reveal-based acceptance), the Archived line-188 fix, and an owner ruling on the Orrery exclusion
scope, this drops to LOW.
