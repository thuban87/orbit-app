# 31.1-06 SUMMARY — Mode-aware glassy content cards

**Status:** complete — owner-approved on-device. **Type:** enhancement (owner feedback after 31.1-05).
No separate PLAN doc — iterated live on the debug Pixel with the owner watching.

## Motivation
After 31.1-05, lowering the veil alone read as imperceptible: the opaque content cards cover
most of the screen, so the veil only revealed the background in the gutters. The owner also
disliked cards reading as solid panels (esp. standard-light "solid white") and spotted a
two-layer artifact when cards were made translucent.

## Change (commits `ec2d2bb`, `4ab5ecc`, `a45d60e`)
- **Mode-aware glassy cards** — `cardTintOpacity(pkg, mode, density)` in `src/theme/tokens/
  surface.ts`: a card is glassy when the background **art tone matches the mode** (galaxy art
  is dark → glassy in dark; standard art is light → glassy in light) so the background shows
  THROUGH the content; **opaque** in the mismatched regime (galaxy-in-light, standard-in-dark)
  so text stays readable over a mismatched-tone art. `CARD_GLASS_OPACITY` galaxy 0.05 / standard
  0.5 (standard's mid-tone Dusk/Mesh set that floor under dark secondary text). Consumed by
  `GlassSurface` (all cards) and, mode-aware too, by `chromeScrimOpacity` (app bar / chrome).
- **Android-elevation fix** — removed the galaxy glow's `elevation` in `GlassSurface`: on a
  translucent surface Android renders it as an opaque dark inner rectangle (the owner's
  two-layer artifact). iOS shadow retained. Minor deviation from dossier §F "subtle glow"
  (Android only).
- **Veil lowered near-zero** — galaxy 0.02/0.10/0.22, standard 0.05/0.14/0.30.

## Verification
- `surface.test.ts` proves AA per asset for BOTH regimes across all four package×mode combos;
  **2927 tests pass**; tsc + check:colors clean.
- Device (Pixel): galaxy-dark glassy (Nebula shows through), galaxy-light opaque/readable,
  standard-light glassy (mauve shows through — "solid white" fixed), standard-dark opaque.

## Notes / follow-ups
- This is a lightweight adoption of the galaxy↔dark / standard↔light coupling — glassy cards
  without the data migration; a stepping stone to the parked theme-merge (which would remove the
  mismatched combos). See memories `mode-aware-glassy-cards`, `android-elevation-opaque-on-translucent`.
- The elevation removal / glass model touch ADR-087 / dossier §F; a superseding ADR should be
  written when the theme-merge is taken up (or at the milestone KB extraction).
