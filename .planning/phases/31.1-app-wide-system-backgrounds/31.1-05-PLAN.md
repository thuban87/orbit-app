## Problem (confirmed: source + on-device)

`BackgroundHost` (`src/components/ui/BackgroundHost.tsx:128-132,167`) paints the selected
asset, then overlays a full-screen scrim of `colors.surface` at the **card** density opacity
(`surfaceOpacityForDensity`, `surface.ts:105/114`): galaxy 0.88/0.93/0.97, standard
0.97/0.99/1.00. That leaves only 0–12% of the art, so every selection looks identical on
regular screens. The picker swatches render raw art (no scrim), which is why Settings looks
fine but nothing else does. Selection/persistence are NOT the fault (ruled out: swatches
render, store+DAO write path verified).

The app's AA text-contrast guarantee lives at the **card** level: `surface.test.ts:124-144`
composites text over `card-tint @ liveGlassTintOpacity over each asset's brightestPixel` — it
never involves the host scrim. So the full-screen host scrim is redundant with the cards text
actually sits on; lightening it behind cards stays within the recorded AA invariant.

## Decision — D-31.1-05-A (within owner-approved Option A; reflect in ADR-087 supplement)

Decouple the **host veil** opacity from the **card surface** opacity. Introduce
`backgroundVeilOpacity(pkg, density)` — a new, much lighter per-package/density token consumed
ONLY by `BackgroundHost`. Card `surfaceOpacityForDensity` / `liveGlassTintOpacity` are
UNCHANGED, so the card AA proof stays green. This weakens the ADR-087 "density scrim == surface
opacity" coupling for the host layer only; owner approved the direction. Values are tunable
constants at the top of `surface.ts` (CLAUDE.md convention) and get tuned on-device.

Bare-on-background **chrome** (which does not sit on a card) gets its own local backing so its
readability no longer depends on the veil.
