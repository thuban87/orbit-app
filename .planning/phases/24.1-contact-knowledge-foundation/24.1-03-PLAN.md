This plan owns merge/purge because relationships carries the only cross-contact FK (linked_contact_id), which is the non-trivial half of the merge.

Output: relationships-dao (add/edit/soft-delete/restore/purge) + relationships-read (list + linked-contact join + visibility), merge-dao + purge-dao knowledge coverage, all node-tested.
