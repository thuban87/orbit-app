# Phase 38.12 Plan 01: Indefinite Snooze + Migration 034 Summary

**Per-person mute folded into Snooze as a `'9999-12-31'` sentinel date that every snooze reader already honours, converted in migration 034, which also carries the 'source' review-memory family and the resume "asked once" columns.**

## Accomplishments

- Migration 034 (head+1 checked on disk: 033 was the head, TARGET_VERSION 33):
  - rebuilds `reconcile_source_snapshot` with `'source'` allowed, copy, count-and-match check, then DROP and RENAME;
  - adds nullable `resume_prompted_at` to both session tables;
  - converts every `reminders_off = 1` row to `snooze_until = '9999-12-31', reminders_off = 0`.
- `INDEFINITE_SNOOZE_UNTIL` and `isIndefiniteSnooze` added to `snooze-dao.ts`. No reader changed. A reader-by-reader test proves the D-98 behaviour on the full migration chain.
- The shared snooze sheet offers "Until I turn it off" between the presets and Choose date. It writes through the existing `onUntil`, so Undo, single-flight and the snackbar are unchanged. The custom date picker now stops at today + 10 years.
- `snoozeUntilLabel` now formats the snooze end for the sheet header, the confirmation and the Overview tile. The tile reads the effective (unexpired) snooze (D-37) and shows "Snoozed · until you turn it off" for an indefinite snooze.
- The per-person mute switch is gone from Edit Contact. `remindersOff` stays in the form state as a pass-through.
- Birthdays are unaffected: a grep confirms that `listBirthdayNotificationCandidates` and `buildBirthdayRequest` never read `snooze_until`.

## Decisions Made

- The sentinel storage (option A), the +10-year picker cap and the choice's position in the sheet are this plan's own design choices, as the plan states.
- The D-134 conversion runs whenever `reminders_off = 1`. A muted person who also had a dated snooze therefore becomes indefinitely snoozed: the mute meant "never remind", and indefinite keeps that meaning. `modified_at` is left alone, as the plan asks.
- `RelationshipOverview` gets an optional `todayLocal` (default: today's local date), so the tile uses the same day as the host's sheet and the tests can fix the date.
