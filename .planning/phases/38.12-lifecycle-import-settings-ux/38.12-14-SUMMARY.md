## Decisions Made
- **Arrival computation:** stagger 0 and an invalid `now`, so the helper returns the day's own quiet-rolled slot, which is exactly the roll the scheduler applies. The scheduler skips a birthday alert whose slot moves off the birthday (`notification-schedule.ts` `buildBirthdayRequest`). So when the roll crosses midnight and Birthday alerts is on, the arrival line adds: "Birthday alerts can't move to the next day, so they won't be sent."
- **Helper copy:** "Reminders that fall inside quiet hours wait until quiet hours end. The weekly digest comes at your reminder time." The digest trigger ignores quiet hours (`digest-schedule.ts` schedules at `deliveryHour` only).
- **Paused vs degraded:** the paused line is hidden while the degraded note ("Notifications are off" after a denial) shows, so the page never gives two different instructions at once.
- **Orbit Center picker:** the `ContactPicker` modal wrapper takes no host rows and has no "You" option. The Bound-only rule (`listSunCandidates`, `sunOccupantIsSelf`) means the picker's own all-contacts load would offer people who can't be the center. So the shared list body (`ContactPickerList`) is used with host rows, inside the shared `Sheet`, with a "You" row and Cancel.
- **Appearance sections:** I read plan step 1's grouping as a list of rows, not a regrouping. The four existing sections are kept, and only "Your profile" is retitled.

## Deferred / out-of-scope items
- **Birthday alerts are silently lost with a late reminder time.** If the reminder time falls in the evening part of an overnight quiet window (for example a 10 PM reminder with quiet hours 9 PM to 8 AM), every birthday alert is skipped: the scheduler won't deliver one a day late. This plan only makes the page say so. Whether the scheduler should send it at the start of the day instead is a product call for the owner. Recommendation: a small follow-up that schedules the birthday at quiet-hours end on the birthday itself.
- **The weekly digest ignores quiet hours.** It always fires at the reminder time. The helper now says so. Whether it should respect quiet hours is a product call.
- **"Star colour 1…N" accessibility labels** on Your star still use the British spelling. American spelling is a standing rule (38.9 D-49), but this plan's spelling scope was "Center" only. It is a one-line fix.
- **Double dim on gated switches:** a disabled row is drawn at 0.5 opacity and its disabled `OrbitSwitch` is also at 0.5, so the switch ends up around 25%. Check the look at the device pass. If it is too faint, a `SettingsRow` change could exempt the trailing control from the row dim.
- **Orbit Center list has no "current" mark on a contact.** The shared picker list has no single-select state. "You" shows as selected when it is the center, and the current center's name shows on the Orbit Center row.
