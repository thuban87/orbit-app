Settings row and section card (D-119)

One section card per section, rows divided by a hairline. A row that opens something ends in a chevron; a row with a switch has no chevron; an info-only row (Version, Your data) has neither and does not react to a tap. Shown in both theme packages, light and dark. The "Your data" line is the D-125 copy for the owner's review.
