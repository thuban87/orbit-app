# Phase 38.12 Plan 27: Quiet hours no longer lose notifications Summary

**Birthday alerts that fall in quiet hours now fire at the quiet-hours end on the birthday itself, through the new day-bound helper `sameDayAllowedInstant`. The weekly digest moves to the quiet-hours end on Sunday through `sameDayAllowedHour`, and a quiet-hours change now re-arms it. The Notifications page arrival line and helper describe the new behaviour. Decay reminders are unchanged.**

## Accomplishments
- `sameDayAllowedInstant` in `fire-instant.ts`:
  - It returns the day's delivery slot (plus stagger) when that slot is allowed and still ahead.
  - When the slot is inside quiet hours, it returns the quiet-hours end on the same day (plus stagger).
  - When neither works, it returns null.
  - Every date is built from local components and checked against the day after normalising, so a DST day can shift the hour but not the day.
- `buildBirthdayRequest` now uses `sameDayAllowedInstant`. Example: with a 10 PM reminder and quiet hours from 9 PM to 8 AM, the alert now fires at 8:xx on the birthday. Before, it was skipped. The "today" copy never moves to another day (ADR-041).
- The weekly digest is scheduled at `digestFireHour(settings)`. That is the delivery hour, or the quiet-hours end when the delivery hour is quiet. It still fires on DIGEST_WEEKDAY (Sunday), keeps the same single identifier and the defer-one coordinator, and stays a WEEKLY trigger. Drift is compared against the adjusted hour, so a change to quiet hours re-arms the digest.
- Re-arm paths (confirmed): every Notifications write, quiet hours included, goes through `persistNotificationSettings`, which fires both reconcilers. Restore (`restore-apply.ts`) and the launch sweep also re-arm.
- Notifications page:
  - `ReminderArrival` gained `sameDayHour`, computed by `sameDayAllowedHour`.
  - When the decay roll crosses midnight, the arrival line now says birthday alerts and/or the digest "still come on their own day, at <time>", depending on which of the two are on. It no longer says birthday alerts won't be sent.
  - The helper now reads: "Nothing arrives during quiet hours. Reminders wait until quiet hours end; birthday alerts and the weekly digest come when quiet hours end on their own day."
- Docs: `docs/systems/notifications.md` (birthday and digest steps, plus a changelog row) and runbook pitfall 3 in `local-notification-integration.md` are synced.

## Decisions Made
- **The digest stays on Sunday.** For an evening delivery hour inside an overnight window, the plan said the digest fires "the next morning", which is Monday. ADR-055 decided Sunday delivery and lists "Monday delivery" as a rejected alternative ("the retrospective is a Sunday close"). Firing on Monday would reverse a recorded decision, which is the owner's call (CLAUDE.md "Whose decision is it"). D-142 says only "send the digest reminder at the end of quiet hours", and the quiet-hours end on Sunday morning meets that. It is also the same same-day rule D-142 gives birthdays. If the owner wants Monday instead, that needs a superseding ADR.
- **Birthday stagger on the quiet-end slot:** following the plan ("plus stagger"), so a burst of birthdays doesn't hit Android 15's notification cooldown. The diff compares at hour granularity, so existing requests don't churn.
- **Still skipped:**
  - a birthday whose reminder time has already passed (outside quiet hours);
  - a quiet birthday whose same-day quiet end has already passed (for example, the app first opened at 9:30 AM on the birthday with a 10 PM reminder).

  This is the plan's "keep today's behaviour when the birthday's slots have all passed".
