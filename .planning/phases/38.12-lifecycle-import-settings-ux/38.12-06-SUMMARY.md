## Decisions Made
- **Profile backgrounds (plan Task 3 step 2 asks which):** the GlassSurface behaviour is kept by passing it **through SettingsSectionCard's container** (`surface="glass"`), not by leaving GlassSurface as the page's card. The saved-background thumbnail cards are unchanged GlassSurfaces.
- Row value colour is `textSecondary` for every row (component shape is Claude's Discretion per CONTEXT). This drops the accent-text colour the region value had.
- Section headers are uppercase `label` role in `textSecondary` above the card; cards use `RADII.lg`, matching GlassSurface.
- Interactions chip groups: the old heading becomes the card's section header, and the caption + chips sit inside the card. The Interaction Assist card has no header (no new copy invented).
- Orrery busy/error lines moved out of the Display section to sit just under its card.
