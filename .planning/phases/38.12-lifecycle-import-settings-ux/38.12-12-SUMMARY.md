**A binned Profile now shows a "<name> is in the Bin" banner with Open the Bin, offers only Layout and Background in ⋯, draws a disabled off star and reads "In the Bin" for Orbit Status; one typed `ContactInBinError` guard makes bind, unbind, snooze, unsnooze, favourite and Edit's participation change refuse binned contacts, and Unbound people lose the Snooze entry and tile and get a dimmed off star that explains itself.**

## Accomplishments

- Binned Profile (D-93, D-34, D-35): a banner below the Hero names the person and its one button opens the Bin. `openBinFromProfile` pops back to `Archived` when the Bin is already under this Profile, and otherwise opens it in the Contacts tab through `navigateIntoTab`. The ⋯ menu shows only Profile Layout and Background. The star is disabled and drawn off whatever the stored rank. The Unbound Bind card is hidden. Orbit Status reads "In the Bin". Restore and Delete were not added to the Profile; they stay in the Bin list (D-20).
- DAO guards (D-34, C-7): `assertContactNotInBinCore` runs inside the open write transaction before any write, in `bindContact`, `unbindContact`, `snoozeContactCore`, `clearSnoozeCore`, `setFavouriteRankCore` and `clearFavouriteRankCore`. `updateContactFull` refuses a `tracking_enabled` change for a binned contact, and an edit that leaves participation unchanged or omits it still saves.
- Callers (convergence c3 D-5): headless or foreground "Snooze 1 week" on a binned contact is now a no-op that still cancels the decay notification and records the key. The five bulk favourite and snooze writers skip binned ids without rolling the batch back. Any other error still rolls the batch back.
- Unbound (D-94, D-36, D-21): there is no "Snooze…" in ⋯ and no Snooze tile, and the stored snooze is untouched. The star draws off and dimmed even when a dormant rank is stored. A tap shows "Bind <name> to make them a Favorite." and writes nothing.
- Dead code (D-41): `bindEnabled` and `canStartLifecycleTransition` were removed, along with their tests.
