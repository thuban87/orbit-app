# Phase 38.12 Plan 22: Lifecycle feedback, Bin and Unbound lists Summary

**Restore, Bind and Unbind now act at once with a named snackbar and Undo. The Bin reads on every focus and shows photo, name and binned date, with Delete permanently moved behind ⋯. The Unbound list shows when each person was last contacted and can be opened from Settings → Contact Management.**

## Accomplishments

- **Bin list (D-31, D-40, D-32).** The list reads in `useFocusEffect`. It shows "Loading the Bin…" until the first read, and re-reads after a failed Restore or Delete so a stale row disappears. Each row (`BinRow`) shows the Avatar, the name (wraps, never truncated) and "Moved to the Bin Oct 13" through `formatDisplayDateOrFallback`, plus a visible secondary "Restore" button. Delete permanently is the only item in the row's ⋯ (`AnchoredMenu`), and it still opens the impact-summary purge confirm. Restore runs `restoreContact`, then the widget refresh and `reconcileSchedule`, then shows "Restored Ann Lee" with Undo. Undo calls `archiveContact`, then `notifyWidgetDataChanged()` and `reconcileSchedule` (Move to Bin's after-effects, L-2), then shows "Moved Ann Lee back to the Bin". A double tap on Restore is ignored while a restore is still running. `listArchived` now also selects `photo`.
- **Profile (D-32, D-33, D-95, D-38).** Bind and Unbind go through `runProfileLifecycleTransition`: write, re-read, then "Bound Ann Lee" / "Unbound Ann Lee" with Undo. Undo runs the opposite transition and confirms itself. A confirmed Unbind closes the ⋯ sheet before the write. The Bind card reads `bindCardModel`: a person with a saved frequency sees "Bind · Every 30 days" and a "Change" link that opens the FrequencyPicker in place. While it is open, the button label follows the pick. A person who never had a frequency keeps today's picker flow. The favourite toggle publishes `notifyWidgetDataChanged()` after the write.
- **Unbound list (D-39).** `listUnbound` returns `last_contact`. The row subtitle is "Last contact <date>" or "No contact logged" and is also read in the row's accessibility label. A query change (or Clear) scrolls the list to the top. The list keeps taps while the keyboard is up (`keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"`).
- **Settings entry (D-97).** `UnboundContacts` is registered in the Settings stack and its param list. Contact Management lists "Unbound contacts" under "Bin". The Contacts ⋯ entry is unchanged.

## Decisions Made

- **Undo confirmation copy** (D-04 requires one; the wording is mine): "Moved <name> back to the Bin", "Unbound <name> again", "Bound <name> again". The owner sees these at the device check.
- **Change before Bind (D-95).** `bindContact` keeps a dormant cadence and ignores a passed interval, so a changed frequency is written first through `setProfileContactFrequency`, then the bind runs. Undo of that Bind unbinds and puts the saved frequency back. The two writes are separate transactions. If the bind fails after the frequency write, the person stays Unbound with the new frequency and an error snackbar shows. That is harmless: cadence is data and ADR-062 keeps it.
- **Subtitles** (Claude's Discretion per CONTEXT): the Unbound row reads "Last contact Oct 13" / "No contact logged". The Settings row reads "People outside your active orbit. Open one to bind them again."
- **Bin first-load failure:** the existing "Couldn't load the Bin" notice is kept, and the screen shows "Couldn't load the Bin. Please go back and retry." instead of the loading line. It never shows the empty state.
