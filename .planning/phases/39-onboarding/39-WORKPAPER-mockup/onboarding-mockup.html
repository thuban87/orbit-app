    Patterns, accessibility, and choices for you to make

    Accessibility treatment (D-32)

    • Each card is a labelled dialog. Focus moves into it on open (the same as BaseOverlay today) and goes back to a sensible control on close.
    • The card's text always names what the spotlight points at, so the cutout is never the only way to know.
    • Skip can be reached on every step. Android Back acts as Skip, never as cancel for the underlying action.
    • No step needs a gesture to advance. Gesture lines (swipe, pinch) come with a TalkBack equivalent in the dialog's label.
    • Reduced motion: no spotlight pulse, no slide between steps, cards appear without fading.
    • Text uses the app's type roles and scales with system font size; the card grows upward and scrolls internally rather than clipping.
    • Buttons keep the 44dp minimum touch target. Card text on surfaceElevated meets AA.

    Open questions for the owner

    1. Card style. Opaque surfaceElevated card with a strong border and a pointer, as drawn. Alternative: the glassy card treatment used elsewhere in Galaxy.
    2. Contacts step with nobody visible. With only Maya (Bound, Not yet), the Contacts list is empty by design. Keep the step as drawn (point at "1 not yet contacted"), or show a sample row while the step is open?
    3. Scrim darkness. 80% of the background colour for spotlight steps, 50–55% for tips over a panel or chart. Darker, lighter, or one value everywhere?
    4. Skip on one-step tips. D-18 puts Skip on every lesson, but on a one-step tip Skip and Got it do the same thing. Keep both, or show only Got it there?
    5. Systems tip over the open panel. D-58 forbids starting a lesson over a sheet; D-66 triggers on the panel opening. Allow this one exception, or show the tip on the Orrery just before the panel opens?
    6. Step counter form. "Step 2 of 4" text plus small progress bars. Alternatives: text only, or bars only.
    7. Where Orient begins. Drawn as: after saving Maya, the app moves to Digest and Orient starts. Today saving lands on the new Profile.
    8. Relationship Basics on the form. Drawn expanded so the Monthly default is visible (D-51). Confirm, or prefer a one-line summary such as "Bound · Monthly · Not yet" that opens the section?
    9. Welcome's local-first line (D-35). Keep it, reword it, or drop it?
    10. Lesson names on the card. Drawn as "Getting around", "Getting ready", "Logging", "Tip". Keep, rename, or drop the eyebrow?
