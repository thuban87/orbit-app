Still present on 2026-10-16. Numbers are the original todo's; review ids in brackets. The dossier should decide on each one: fix it, or leave it on purpose.

- **1 [B1 IN-02]:** tap Population, Filters or Sort and switch tabs within about one frame, and the panel can open on the Contacts screen you just left. The hidden panel then swallows your next Back or tab re-tap, which is the D-62 symptom reached through a narrow race. The trigger opens the panel inside the async `measureInWindow` callback (`DashboardControlRow.tsx:189-209` and the Filters/Sort twins). That callback can run after the blur-close `closeDashboardPanelOnBlur` (`dashboard-panel-store.ts`) has already fired.

- **18 [D IN-04]:** star a contact and leave Home at once, and a full Home reload runs in the background. The user sees nothing; it is wasted work. The cause is that `favourite` is not in `DEFERRABLE_WHILE_HIDDEN` (`dashboard-refresh-scheduler.ts:58-62`), and the scheduler comment wrongly says the toggle is always on a visible Home. Fix either the deferral or the comment.
