Still present on 2026-10-16. Numbers are the original todo's; review ids in brackets. None breaks a requirement, but the dossier should decide on each one: fix it, or leave it on purpose.

**User-visible**

- **3 [A IN-04]:** after a Bound import, Import Progress can sit at 100% while reminders are recalculated, and Duplicate Review does not wait for that (`DuplicateReviewScreen.tsx:258`). The bound-effects helper is copied in three places: `import-lifecycle-effects.ts:38`, `import-driver.ts:259-262` and `import-acquire.ts:199-211`.
- **4 [A IN-05]:** with birthday reminders for Unbound contacts turned on, an Unbound import's birthdays are not scheduled until the next launch or foreground (`import-lifecycle-effects.ts:11`).
- **15 [C IN-03]:** if closing the import session fails after contacts were created, reminders are not refreshed until the next launch. A single import also says "Couldn't import contact" for a contact that was in fact created (`import-driver.ts:259-262`, `import-acquire.ts:199-211`).
- **16 [C IN-04]:** for a hand-edited or non-Orbit backup whose `exportedAt` ends in "Z" or has milliseconds, Restore shows "Source date: Unknown time". The cause is `RestorePreviewScreen.tsx:344`, which goes through `formatDateTimeMinuteOrFallback` → `parseLocalMs`, and that accepts local wall-clock times only.
- **17 [D IN-03]:** on AI Data Permissions, when nothing is enabled yet, a search or type filter that matches nothing says "AI can't access any contact information yet." It should say "No information matches these filters." (`ai-permissions-logic.ts:155-159` picks the empty copy whenever `access.items === 0`).
- **21 [D IN-07]:** before the model catalog loads, the picker briefly shows a "Current model: <raw id>" row that disappears when the card arrives. That row always shows the raw id, never the model's name (`ai-model-picker-logic.ts:134, 146`; `AIModelPickerScreen.tsx:293-295`).
