## 2. What the owner disliked, with causes in code (record as dead ends)

| Dislike | Cause in code (verified) |
|---|---|
| **One-finger orbit "awful"** | `strategyCamera` maps one-finger pan → `{kind:"orbit"}` (`use-orrery-camera.ts` diff, Pan.onUpdate). `ORBIT_YAW_PER_POINT 0.0076` (a full-width drag ≈ 180°) and `ORBIT_TILT_PER_POINT 0.0052` (≈ 250 pt sweeps the whole tilt range), `orrery-gesture-logic.ts:22-23,103-115`. Inertia lasts 900 ms (`ORBIT_COAST_MS`), velocity × 0.32 s, capped at 1.2 rad yaw and 0.35 rad tilt (`orrery-recovery-logic.ts:19-27,118-137`). This removes one-finger pan entirely |
| **Two-finger pan/tilt over-sensitive** | In strategy mode the two-finger "tilt" recognizer becomes a **pan** (`kind:"pan"` with the centroid dx and dy) and the two-finger tilt gesture is gone. Activation is `hypot(Δcentroid) ≥ TILT_DISTANCE 28` in any direction (`orrery-gesture-logic.ts:15`, use-orrery-camera tilt `onTouchesMove`), and it runs `Simultaneous(pan, pinch, rotation, tilt)`. Centroid drift during a pinch or twist therefore also pans. `panCamera` (CL:789-821) anchors the plane point, so at 62° tilt one screen point of vertical drag moves about 1/cos(62°) ≈ 2.1× as much ground (more toward the far rim). Pinch, two-finger pan and twist were **never exercised on device** (adb has no multi-touch; pkg INTERACTIONS, README owner checklist) |
| **Too dark at overview** | `AMBIENT_INTENSITY 0.22` (S3:126); `FOG_NEAR_FRACTION 0.8` of dolly distance (fog starts *before* the pivot) and `FOG_FAR_EXTENTS 1.9` (S3:84-85, 984-989); `RING_FAR_OPACITY 0.18` (S3:109); `RING_STROKE_SCALE 0.45` (S3:115); dense ring floor 0.05; lit spheres with night sides (`KEY_LIGHT_INTENSITY 3.2`, face ambient 0.3); and no labels at the overview tier (production rule) |
